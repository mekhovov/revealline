The three raw PNGs total 8,024,867 bytes. They are exact copies of the built-in image-generation outputs; the originals also remain at their tool-provided locations. No cropping, resizing, recompression or image editing was applied. The prompt requested an ideal 2048 × 1024 output; the actual returned dimensions above are retained as delivered.

The scenes share detailed 16-bit-inspired pixel treatment, petrol/navy shadows, apricot light, subdued fictional invading military equipment and a recognizable four-rotor Ukrainian FPV. The three compositions use an orchard irrigation crossing, an asymmetric rail-workshop courtyard and a night canal/bridge district. The military nationality is the fictional narrative brief; the unmarked vehicles are not evidence of a specific real incident. There is no text, Z marking, HUD, capture line or graphic injury visible in the inspected outputs.

Exact tool arguments are in [tool-requests.json](tool-requests.json), with readable copies in [prompts](prompts/). [provenance.json](provenance.json) records the tool mode, inspected shape/style references, raw-output paths, dimensions, hashes and review limits. The existing project pictures and supplied drone photographs were visually studied; they were not supplied as editing targets, cropped, embedded or claimed as newly licensed assets. No reference-game artwork was used.

The illustrations still need artistic acceptance, a managed derivative/size decision, real image import/decode checks and browser evaluation with runtime actors. Night Crossfire especially needs a phone-size contrast review: its dark foreground supports atmosphere but has not been qualified on an actual device. These are still illustrations, with no animation, sprite-sheet, complete media pipeline or production-release claim.
