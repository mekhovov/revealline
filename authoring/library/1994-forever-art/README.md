# 1994 Forever: three original reward illustrations

Three original AI-assisted pictures for review and future content production. They are not installed in any campaign, runtime catalog, build or release.

| Illustration       | Raw original                             | Actual dimensions | PNG bytes |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | ----------------- | --------: |
| Midnight Arcade    | [PNG](originals/midnight-arcade.png)     | 1774 × 887, 2:1   | 2,522,301 |
| Rainy Video Rental | [PNG](originals/rainy-video-rental.png)  | 1774 × 887, 2:1   | 2,779,895 |
| Last Train, Side B | [PNG](originals/last-train-cassette.png) | 1774 × 887, 2:1   | 2,429,841 |

The raw PNGs total **7,732,037 bytes**. Each is an exact byte copy of its built-in image-generation output, with the tool original retained. The prompts requested an ideal 2048 × 1024 image; the actual returned 1774 × 887 dimensions are preserved. No crop, resize, palette reduction, color adjustment, recompression or image editing was applied.

The pictures share ink/navy, violet and petrol shadows, amber/peach light and restrained cyan/magenta accents. Their compositions differ: an intimate diagonal arcade interior with a large joystick and CRT; a rainy rental-shop street with a foreground VHS cassette; and a carriage-table cassette player and headphones before a warm station landscape. Blank signage, abstract screen art and unbranded devices keep generated lettering and recognizable game artwork out of the intended content. No legible text, familiar copyrighted characters, HUD or baked gameplay lines were observed in the full output inspection.

These are detailed modern pixel-art illustrations rather than certified indexed-palette or hand-authored grid assets. Fine cabinet wear and rain reflections are visually dense. The train player resembles a portable tabletop recorder rather than a slim personal cassette player; that is an artistic review point, not a verified replica of a particular device. The scenes suggest 1989–1994 without claiming a documented location or exact historical reconstruction.

Exact generation arguments are in [tool-requests.json](tool-requests.json), with readable copies in [prompts](prompts/). [tool-responses.json](tool-responses.json) preserves the returned output paths and save guidance without duplicating base64 image payloads. [provenance.json](provenance.json) records dimensions, SHA-256 hashes, raw-source byte equality, valid PNG chunk CRCs, visual observations and remaining checks. All three were new generations using the built-in image tool; no references were supplied as image inputs and no fallback CLI was used.

Before integration, review the artwork with opaque partial concealment and real actors/trails at phone and desktop sizes, select bounded runtime derivatives, verify image import/decode and assign authored map/theme identities. Human artistic acceptance, game readability, storage budgets and runtime/offline behavior remain pending. This delivery adds three reviewable raw originals; it does not complete the bulk P6 content or device qualification.
