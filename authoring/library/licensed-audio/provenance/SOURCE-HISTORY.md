## 3xBlast's archived restriction and public correction

The retained archive is 51,754,956 bytes, SHA-256 `a374646659200a98798684545262824658bd2407c6e94340da25429f695283b0`. It contains the seven Vorbis originals and a 590-byte INFO file, SHA-256 `d0a016f21f1e1f3ec7bc70982f80fe6f8f8a3612e52270de19e9ddbee1567861`. The INFO declares CC0 but also says: “Don't re-upload these tracks or re-sell them as is.” Keep that file unchanged.

On the creator's primary page, moderator MedicineStorm's December 9, 2021 warning explains that such an added restriction conflicts with CC0. The creator's reply dated December 9, 2021 at 15:23 explicitly removes the stipulation and says the page was edited. The moderator's edited response marks the matter fixed, and the current page declares CC0 without that restriction. A December 12 creator comment separately reports an updated archive for rendering errors; this does not establish the audible quality of our exact download. [Primary page and dated discussion](https://opengameart.org/content/7-pop-punk-chiptune-tracks).

The retained captured page is `3xblast-source.html`, SHA-256 `689cd2ecb83cb35ea8197248b8f1fcae3c4bb7620d29f45f3342645c96403302`. The failed first retrieval/partial archive and the successful resumed-download receipt are both retained in their original directories. The final seven original hashes and sizes are listed in `../sources.json`; `derivatives.json` and `derivative-inspection.json` retain conversion and runtime MP3 inspection evidence. The archive was not rewritten to remove its stale INFO.

The production basis is creator-declared CC0 together with the explicit public removal, while carrying both the contrary archive text and the dated correction in source provenance. Each derivative identifies its exact OGG parent and conversion recipe. Neither redistribution of source bytes nor codec conversion makes these original RevealLine compositions, and “Looped” in a source filename does not establish seamless MP3 playback.

## Evidence limits

The old Holizna `catalog.json` and export reports predate the later `browser-receipt.json`; their earlier “native pending” text remains historical, with the later receipt providing the limited short-playback supplement. The complete decode records measured PCM duration, peak and sample RMS. Sample RMS is not LUFS and full-scale samples are not proof of audible clipping. No full listening, loop-seam, in-game mix or subjective music approval is recorded for any of the 24 candidates.

The source compiler reads accepted MP3 originals and derivatives only. The copied encoder script is historical provenance with its original cache paths, not an automatically executed build step. `derivatives.json` records each exact OGG parent, MP3 output, full decode durations and the authoring-only encoder. Encoder packages/wheels and temporary PCM are excluded from this tree and the runtime.
