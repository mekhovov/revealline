# Idle Frequency: arcade revision

The first Idle Frequency candidate was rejected by the user as too slow and unsuitable for the game. That recording, score, renderer, composer and measurement receipt remain unchanged. The feedback and exact original hashes are retained in [idle-frequency-feedback.json](idle-frequency-feedback.json).

The replacement is a new original composition: **144 BPM, 92 bars, 2:35**, F-sharp minor, with a kick, articulated bass and FM melody in the opening bar. It was subsequently **rejected by the user and remains unpublished**: the rhythm still feels too soft/slow and the instrument/melody palette is wrong. See [second feedback](idle-frequency-arcade-v2-feedback.json). No further full render is authorized yet. Technical measurements do not establish that the style or musical quality is acceptable.

## New score and production

The arrangement is a four-bar cold open, a 16-bar A melody, an eight-bar bass/arp variation, a 16-bar B answer, a four-bar groove break, a developed 16-bar A return, an eight-bar lead/bass exchange, a 16-bar A/B relay, and a four-bar rhythmic sign-off. The break retains kick, hats and a moving bass line. No passage switches to half time or ambient pads alone.

The A and B melodies contain different eight-bar sentences with deliberate call/response. Written bass figures change their octave, fifth and approach-note patterns; chord inversions, counterlines and tom/snare fills distinguish returns. A separate local renderer revision gives the FM bass and lead independent operator envelopes, short drum transients and less reverb send. FM voices are oversampled 2×; this is general original synthesis, not an exact legacy-console emulation.

- [24-bit / 48 kHz stereo FLAC master](candidates/idle-frequency-arcade-v2/master.flac)
- [Measurement receipt](candidates/idle-frequency-arcade-v2/receipt.json)

The encoded MP3 measures −16.028 LUFS integrated and −5.262 dBTP estimated using 4× polyphase oversampling. The master was encoded as native 24-bit FLAC and decoded again to confirm an exact PCM hash match. This is technical verification; no assistant full listening assessment, user approval, originality adjudication or in-game mix approval is claimed.

Use the same existing Python environment and encoder package as the first production batch. To reproduce, first preserve or choose a new output revision: the renderer intentionally refuses to overwrite an existing receipt. Run `compose_retro_v2.py`, then `render_retro_v2.py scores/idle-frequency-arcade-v2.json --scratch <empty-scratch-directory> --encoder-path <existing-lameenc-package-directory>` from this directory. No model or sample download is involved.

Only this replacement was rendered after the feedback. Glass Highway and the other six previous recordings are unchanged. The remaining 29 planned productions are held pending a satisfactory style review.
