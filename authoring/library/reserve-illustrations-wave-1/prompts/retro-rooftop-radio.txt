Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: one original collectible reveal-picture illustration for a game, not a screenshot or sprite sheet.
Format: a complete 2:1 wide composition, ideally 1792 by 896 pixels. Preserve the entire scene inside the image; no borders or lettering.
Medium: polished modern pixel-art illustration with intentional crisp stepped clusters and rich material detail. Large readable silhouettes first, selective small reward discoveries second. Cohesive navy/petrol shadows, luminous cream highlights, warm amber/coral light and controlled cool accents. Avoid photographic texture, blurry brushwork and uniformly noisy microdetail.
Reveal design: three connected visual discoveries across the wide scene, a strong main object recognizable at 320 by 160, broad quieter mid-value regions between clusters, clean silhouettes when only a corner or central band is uncovered. No baked fog, concealment, capture lines, grid, HUD, pointers, UI, text, lettering, watermark or signature. Do not imitate any existing artist's composition or copyrighted game artwork.

Theme: 1994 Forever. Create an evocative unbranded 1989–1994 summer rooftop listening nook above a small neighborhood at blue hour. A large portable twin-speaker cassette boombox with unmistakable cassette window and chunky mechanical buttons rests on a low painted table in the foreground, beside foam headphones and two plain cassette cases. A broad striped awning and a small television aerial frame the middle; a warm-lit rooftop greenhouse and distant apartment windows form the second and third discoveries. An orange extension cable lies slack as a real object, never a game trail. Soft peach sunset beyond violet rooftops, restrained cyan and magenta reflected accents, tactile beige plastic, charcoal speakers and folded denim. No arcade cabinets, copied album covers, recognizable product model, brand, readable dial text or software/game characters. Make the main radio and open sky clearly readable at thumbnail scale; small plant pots and rooftop objects reward the full reveal.
