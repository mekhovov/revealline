What the timestamps mean

The requested time asks the browser to seek. An observed frame timestamp comes from its decoded-frame callback and can differ. Without that callback, the preview labels an approximate playhead and unavailable frame timestamp. Neither value promises arbitrary frame-number accuracy. Downloaded PNG bytes are the actual captured picture.

Frame stepping appears only after the browser reports a presented-frame timestamp. The tool repeats bounded seeks until that timestamp changes; it never invents a frame rate. A browser may skip more than one frame, so review the observed timestamp and PNG. Browsers without that callback keep these controls disabled.
