# Team outcome generation prompts

Slots `team.capture.joint` and `team.recovery` accept centered 32×32 transparent PNGs or `team.outcome.v1` recipes. They decorate actual joined cuts and actual two-player reserve recovery respectively. No bonus, health amount, collision, invulnerability duration or new ability is implied. Player numbers and the circle/diamond identities remain game-owned, as does outcome text. Critical warnings take placement priority.

Generate an original FPV Field Kit outcome badge with sparse crisp pixel clusters in the resolved theme palette. Keep the pivot at (0.5,0.5), binary transparency and at most twelve opaque colors. No text, background, glow, blur or new mechanical symbol. Return source plus prepared 32×32 PNG, actual creator/source/rights and generation prompt. Verify dimensions, alpha, occupied bounds, native size and both Team arenas; inspect the appropriate event scene and reduced effects. A candidate is not approved production artwork.

The host observes each fixed simulation step; the presentation retains at most one cue per role for one second of active game time. Paused gameplay retains the same cue without replay. New attempts and backward seeks clear old cues. Studio restores only its own command-earned sample when looping. Victory/loss retire live cues. All historical simulation behavior remains unchanged.
