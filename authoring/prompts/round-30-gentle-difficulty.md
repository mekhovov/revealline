These five prose examples are separate from the existing **124 CLI prompt templates** and add no catalog entries or skills. They cover the working implementation under verification, not a release or hardware claim. Read [Campaign difficulty](../../docs/campaign-difficulty.md), its [accepted plan](../../docs/round-30-gentle-difficulty-plan.md) and [Runtime Maintainer](../skills/xonix-runtime-maintainer/SKILL.md). Derive Standard/Gentle through the shared helper and compare the finite changes against normalized defaults and schema maxima. Preserve map geometry, required objectives, recipe identities and picture bytes. Test controller select-preview Cancel/Confirm, reader exits and a restored saved mode differing from the preference. Do not use a pause/autosave call to implement a preference-only change.”

3. **Shared access, separate results.** “Complete different maps in Standard and Gentle through real core/replay APIs, then complete one in both. Check one shared clear per declared map, attainable short-chapter appearance tiers, replayability after out-of-order restores and mode-labelled clean/gold results. Exercise the shared 1,000-score cap and merge eviction honestly, plus the separate 30-minute recorder and 7,200-second collection limits; never clip results or increase a format budget.”

4. **One picture, explicit saved mode.** “Use a real illustrated chapter with Gentle-only and mixed-mode wins. Remove/reinstall the pack, test an art-only update and keep unknown rows archived. Check all four celebration families and Reduced effects through visible UI; do not infer successful animation or decoding from static source data.”

5. **Prove completion and readable consequences.** “Record new legal Gentle routes for the shipped maps in both steering modes without editing state, ticks or old Standard expectations. Include Sentinel missed openings/recovery and original objective requirements. Separately inspect the real campaign UI at 320×640, 844×390 and 1280×720 CSS sizes: current/next mode, readable Retry reset note, save limits, reachable native controls and source-specific export cancellation. Report necessary scrolling and exact evidence boundaries. Structural validation, Practice rewards suppression and injected-pad tests are not physical-device or human-balance acceptance.”
