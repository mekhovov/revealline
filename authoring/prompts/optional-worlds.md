# Optional worlds: reusable requests

Use the [shared delivery contract](../../docs/feature-delivery-workflow.md#optional-chapter-authoring-contract) and [current authoring interface](../../docs/optional-worlds.md). These are adaptable requests, not generated production assets or invented pack fields.

The successor's supported optional list is the four original picture variants plus [Route Choices — Tactical](../library/fpv-route-choices/README.md). Use its existing separate campaign, full roster and 38-context proof. Its three distinct layouts and three originals are separate counts from the four variants' twelve pictures over three reused layouts. It remains optional: no default campaign replacement, automatic install or core-cache body download.

- **Original Ukrainian chapter:** “Create three original Ukrainian heritage reward pictures for a new optional chapter. Choose regionally specific subjects from primary references; preserve source PNGs and effective prompts. Give each map a distinct focal story readable under black partial concealment. Record imaginative interpretations honestly. Register a new campaign identity and verify both steering policies before publication.”
- **FPV variation:** “Create a dusk FPV Front picture chapter with recognizable small Ukrainian FPV silhouettes, blue/yellow identification and no Z on friendly equipment. Keep painted vehicles distinct from active threat markers. Reuse reviewed geometry only when explicitly recorded; do not call three skins three new mechanics.”
- **1994 nostalgia:** “Create arcade, video-rental and late-train reward scenes with varied compositions, navy shadows and restrained warm light. Preserve original pixels and provenance. Inspect bright trail and warning readability over reflections. Existing procedural synth is reused music unless actual new tracks are produced and auditioned.”
- **Spend management world:** “Build an original fictional resource-and-savings city chapter using consistent navy/cyan/amber art. Use approved brand references for exact logos; otherwise label original mascot and city imagery as concepts. Keep campaign progress separate and frame goals in the registered game mechanics.”
- **Installation review:** “Review a new optional chapter from download through Install, Choose, first capture, Pause, saved reload and stopped-server continuation. Inject a valid same-ID pack with different art in a modeled test and require an explicit conflict. Preserve unrelated flight bytes and installed packs on cancellation or capacity failure.”
- **Tactical chapter review:** “Install Route Choices from More worlds without changing the paused Pressure Lines run. Choose it explicitly, read each recommended class and compare successful equipment/counterplay with an omitted-action failure in both steering policies. Check all three earned originals and separate Gentle progress. Retain the original Arcade study, installed-library cap and exact source PNGs; qualify release-prefix downloads and actual cold recovery before publication.”
- **Asset update:** “Prepare a new revision of this optional artwork with a parent-linked source and new transport/content hashes. Preserve frozen downloads. Decide explicitly whether this is a new authored chapter or a future presentation assignment; do not change old simulation identities by accident.”
- **Release:** “Freeze the reviewed source with the optional-capable adapter, prove body inclusion and core-cache exclusion, merge the PR after checks, verify every public byte and the real browser journey, then publish the immutable release and report remaining limits.”
