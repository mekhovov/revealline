# Restore a historical Team presentation

> Inspect the currently accepted `.rlteam` reader and its exact uploaded owner.
> Identify the published theme/source/runtime hash independently of the envelope
> manifest hash. Preserve the original serialized runtime and every dependency
> byte. Admit only a code-owned, unambiguous theme/revision/collection association;
> do not authorize a revision range or infer the newest theme by ID.
>
> Stage a separate owned host and picture. Use that snapshot for preview, painter
> and applicable published audio; retain it through Retry and release it only
> after successful replacement or disposal. Failed/cancelled/stale work must keep
> the old attempt usable. Restore the prior painter after temporary preflight.
> Never replace historical fallback artwork with newer Team roles implicitly.
>
> Keep original manifests as explicit retained compiler inputs. Verify schema,
> source, paths, sizes, hashes, ownership inventory and unchanged current output.
> Account for every optional offline dependency and measure build capacity;
> historical import support must not inflate every core cache past its budget.
> Record source checks, browser observations, waived tests and public/device
> evidence separately. Label reconstructed fixtures honestly and preserve all
> historical releases, authored maps and player data.

See [the implemented contract](../../docs/team-retained-imports.md).
