# First Flight authoring and verification prompts

Eight prose prompts for the implemented [First Flight course](../../docs/first-flight.md) in working v0.16.0; latest frozen release v0.15.0. They are separate from the shared CLI's 124 templates and do not add a new imported pack format. The lessons are **Close a line**, **Find the empty side** and **Bring the picture home**. Use these against the actual implementation; a prompt is not test evidence or proof of beginner understanding.

1. **Explain closure clearly.** Review **Close a line** in the real game. Rewrite only its instructional copy so a new player understands leaving safe ground, the vulnerable live line and returning to safe ground. Keep one current task and reviewable details. Preserve exact step IDs, map identity and completion predicates. Test the displayed copy at compact and short-landscape sizes; do not claim that copy review establishes beginner comprehension.

2. **Prove the empty-side example.** Inspect the finite lesson registry and observer. Using normal recorded inputs in both steering modes, close the exact center divider, then turn left on each accepted row. Check actual before/after live-line and claimed-cell sets, both retained enemy regions, objective capture and the real win. Verify an alternate legal win is reported as missing the suggested example. Never use `cut.closed.cells` as a live-line length or copy a capture-size heuristic.

3. **Verify recovery without inventing success.** After securing the divider, make a legal failed second cut, recover and complete the taught route. Confirm earlier safe territory remains and only the unfinished cut disappears. Retain the verified divider step through nonterminal recovery; a fresh Retry starts a new observer. Never force the player to lose a life, fabricate a failure event or alter cells to produce the desired explanation.

4. **Protect the real saved flight.** Start an ordinary campaign flight, make a live cut and enter training through its visible action. Check the entry's no-save freeze, existing-slot envelope preflight, replay verification, explicit save success and readback. Exercise malformed slot, storage failure, cancellation, ownership loss and a stale async context with bounded injected adapters. Verify that failed/cancelled entry suppresses lifecycle autosaves until explicit resume/new attempt. Compare the retained checkpoint through a real return, Load saved flight and Resume. Compare public complete backups against the post-entry baseline after training wins and in-memory settings changes. Distinguish the intentional entry checkpoint write and earlier ordinary Help autosave from course activity; do not claim the individual Ready Export attempt action exports the stored slot.

5. **Check all input boundaries.** Use the actual Controller Lab course choices with remapped Confirm, Back and Menu. Test held Confirm across Next/Start, reader Done, Skip, Retry, Stop, Hold/Toggle Boost, blur and disconnect. Separately use keyboard and visible Hold/Tap direction controls. No held action may automatically start another lesson or fire an ability after a UI transition. Report software gestures separately from physical-controller testing.

6. **Inspect layout and markers.** In Playground's First Flight preview, check 320×640, 390×844, 844×390, 844×501 and 1280×720 actual child viewports. Capture Control geometry and inspect the current task, complete reader text, lesson controls, line/turn-band markers and result. Keep meaningful shape/text distinctions, at least 44px action targets, no horizontal overflow and reachable vertical scrolling. A marker must not intercept input or obscure the real threat. Record actual pointer media rather than calling a resized desktop a touch-device test.

7. **Propose a themed teaching variation.** Draft a Ukrainian heritage, 1990s arcade or fictional spend-management presentation for the same three learning concepts. Specify independent artwork, palette, sound and label replacements, preserve originals and provenance, and compare silhouettes at game scale. Identify any requested new behavior as a separate reviewed code change. Do not claim that a visual pack can replace the finite trusted tutorial registry or supply executable teaching logic.

8. **Freeze and check the actual course artifact.** Run the applicable behavior suites and required release gates after implementation is stable. Build an isolated candidate, test real course play and saved-flight return, then archive the exact reviewed commit under a new label. Verify the archived source reproduces the distribution, prepare its offline files through Settings and repeat disconnected course navigation. Keep earlier releases and proof files unchanged. State the artifact identity, measured outcomes and remaining physical/player-observation limits.
