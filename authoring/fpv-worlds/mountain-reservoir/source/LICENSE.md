# Original Reservoir source artwork

The geometry, placement and color design generated by `scene.mjs` and the
deterministic mineral-grain texture generated by `mineral.mjs` are original
RevealLine work by RevealLine contributors, dedicated under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

No third-party meshes, textures, photographs or competitor assets are included.
The source uses the repository's existing Three.js geometry utilities and pinned
glTF preparation tools; their existing licenses remain separate and unchanged.
