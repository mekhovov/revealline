This revision closes the triangular sky gaps behind the western and northern
terraces. The eight bilingual challenges, their routes, all collision surfaces,
and the land-side water boundary are unchanged. The new scene and its sixteen
fresh demonstrations have a new exact pack identity; keep r8 recordings with r8.

- [Playable world pack](mountain-reservoir.r9.rlpack) — import through Library.
- [Editable project ZIP](mountain-reservoir.r9.zip) — import through the creator.
- [Sixteen demonstrations](mountain-reservoir.r9.proofs.json) — import after the
  exact r9 pack, one per challenge in Self-level and Acro.

Use GitHub's raw download for each file. The public Pages deployment does not
serve the repository's authoring directory. This optional revision adds no new
challenges: the bundled catalogue stays 196 / 14 worlds; installing Reservoir
yields 204 / 15. See the [eight challenge descriptions](../r8/README.md).

Qualification: 315 asset checks, 575 ordinary-flight/independent-replay checks,
and ten actual-renderer observations covering matched west/north/grazing views
plus Low/Balanced/High, Pixel and shared-industrial seam visibility. All sixteen
fresh flight results match r8's retained physical results. The fixed matched
Balanced views retain the same draw counts and registered resource owners;
the 34 additional mesh triangles contribute 68 submitted triangles in those
observed passes. These counts are not frame-rate or hardware evidence.

This bounded seam repair does not finish the representative Reservoir art
standard. Materials, composition and broader camera coverage remain separate.
The stopped-server offline journey and full imported-player/editor matrix in
r8 are historical baseline evidence, not newly repeated r9 claims.
