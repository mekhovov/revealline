# Lunar regolith and survey-material foundation

This opt-in appearance study replaces the plain olive ground with dark mare, pale uplands, soft crater ejecta and exposed graphite rock. Original dust mottling is scaled in metres in global coordinates, so changing local origins or crossing cells does not reset its phase. Near-surface detail fades with pixel footprint rather than becoming distant stripes. No imported images, textures or runtime modules are added.

The independent `lunar-survey` scenery profile recognizes only the five exact original composition-r1 palettes, and leaves textured or unrecognized surfaces unchanged. Rocks receive cool graphite/dust variation; survey structures receive restrained two-metre alloy panel joints; amber identification marks remain distinct. This is a surface treatment, not new model detail. The simple dish, shelters, sparse foreground composition and broad unpopulated land still require further authored work.

Both profiles use existing staged material ownership and live tier invalidation. Performance keeps broad low-frequency variation; Balanced adds metre-scale mottling; Quality adds finer grain. All remain opaque with unchanged depth writing, side selection and vertex positions. They do not hide terrain, make holes, change clearance or affect collision. The setting is explicit, with all existing styles and the default plain profile preserved. There is no Library pin or default player appearance change.

`verify-regolith.mjs EXACT_COMPOSITION_R1_DIRECTORY` verifies all 114 immutable asset files and all six authored-start windows, comparing plain and three tiers. Position/normal/index bytes, support queries, swept contacts and charged geometry are identical; disposal happens once. Peak geometry remains 548,808 bytes. Shader hooks are assembled across origins, quality invalidation is checked, and unknown/textured palettes are excluded. This does not replace native shader/render review or claim device FPS.

Review URL parameters are `companion=/region/terrain/region.json&ground=lunar-regolith&surface=lunar-survey&views=lunar-basin`. Native material acceptance is pending separately from source geometry and the corrected camera gate. The world is fictional and uses the existing terrestrial drone physics; it does not simulate lunar gravity or a vacuum. The geometry source remains the bounded 768 m composition-r1, not the unbuilt 8 km design target.
