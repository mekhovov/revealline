## Evidence before imitation

Record game edition, direct source URL, timestamp or screenshot, observation type and uncertainty. A still establishes appearance, a visible manual establishes its documented rule, and a temporal sequence supports the specific transition seen. None proves input feel, complete enemy taxonomy or player retention. Keep XPOSED 2016, Reloaded and Switched separate. SeXoniX's public stills do not establish its dynamic mechanics. Reference audio has not been auditioned in this round.

## Decisions that change authoring

- **Space:** compose a recognizable route problem—bar, offset gates, chambers, broken ring or broad spiral—before increasing enemy speed. Art motifs may inspire geometry; actual cell data determines it.
- **Terrain:** Reloaded distinguishes walls blocking both sides, player-only slow terrain and player-lethal terrain that enemies ignore. Local terrain needs a new layer; `blockedRects` and a global enemy-speed modifier are not substitutes.
- **Threat domains:** distinguish open-field, changing-contour, revealed-ground and territory-eroding roles. The current `enemy.patrol-boundary.v1` follows only the outer border. New roles need explicit extensions, including activation/spawn rules and semantic art roles. Claimed ground is not unconditionally safe when claimed-space threats are enabled.
- **Capture:** select a registered fill policy and use its defined anchors. Current `fill.enemy-seeded.v1` treats every live field enemy as an anchor and excludes boundary patrols; it has no per-enemy selection flag. Selecting anchor eligibility independently for future roles requires an extension. A reference enemy's appearance or movement alone does not establish its fill role. Evaluate captured markers after filling. Contact pickups require a separate model.
- **Score and progress:** distinguish current coverage, first-time captured cells, one-time objective rewards, actual result medals and gallery visibility. Erosion must not enable unlimited area-score farming. A full-picture victory reward does not mean the player captured 100%.
- **Progression:** recommended ordinary completion unlocks the next level; speed/survival/exceptional capture invite mastery replay. Per-level speed medals are separate from hard failure timers. Preserve a user's explicitly selected alternate progression.
- **Visual hierarchy:** bright player and active route, recognizable threat silhouettes, distinct wall/slow/lethal textures, quieter artwork during action. Painted vehicles, cultural motifs and logos are not live actors. Review a real small rendered board before claiming phone readability.
- **Devices:** preserve the complete arena and challenge coordinates across screen sizes. Different topology or rules means a new challenge identity; changing art or orientation does not.
- **Audio and feedback:** key proposals to semantic events such as cut closure, life loss, pickup collection, status expiry, cells reopened and victory. Separate ordinary capture from completed-art celebration. Proposed events, prompts and sound descriptions are not delivered runtime/audio assets.

## Round 07 motion and interface decisions

- **Keep all three appearances:** compact/microtile, detailed-object and hybrid are independent presentation variants over the same geometry and motion trace. Hybrid is the proposed default, not a replacement for the other two. Props must not hide real gaps or imply nonexistent ones. Keep all four families' own palettes, materials and subjects.
- **Small avatar, bright head:** the Pack 6 0:11 sample shows a warm player core leading a cooler cut. The approximate 12–18-pixel core in a 1280×720 video frame is a rendered reference observation, not an engine collider measurement or universal target. Measure our avatar's occupied pixels at actual play scale. Body, rotor/wing parts, shadow and glow may be separate; their appearance must not change the collider.
- **Separate live cut from secured frontier:** unfinished trail and leading head remain the clearest action signals; completed ownership uses a quieter contour. Render these above decoration. Capture particles must not obscure the head or next legal turn. Read velocity, direction and event state without using animation to change movement, fill or damage.
- **Retain progress through an ordinary life loss:** Foreseen 2:28.1–2:29.8 shows the failed trail removed, lives reduced and the prior coverage/score/geometry retained, followed by a visible respawn. Model impact location and respawn location as separate cues when the selected rules support that behavior. The roughly 1.2-second reappearance interval in those samples does not establish when controls resume, a protection window or optimal recovery timing. Enemies and the timer continue in the observed sequence; do not infer a global pause from a death effect.
- **Zero is not automatically death:** Foreseen 3:00 and 3:30 show continued play at zero; the 6:00 result shows completion without the speed award. Label medal expiry separately from mission failure. Only an explicitly selected hard-timer rule should end the run on timeout; current-schema support must be checked before writing either behavior into a pack.
- **Reward artwork and keep repetition quick:** Pack 6 2:33–2:45.5 shows quota, whole artwork, stars, bonus tally and final actions as distinct beats. Preserve actual achieved coverage while presenting the complete picture. Our proposed fast-repeat mode makes final actions available early and lets one deliberate input finish a sequence without accidentally starting another run. The roughly 12-second reference sequence does not prove it is unskippable.
- **Gallery and UI are states:** Pack 6 0:00–0:05 shows selected card → full-picture preview → the same selection. Preserve focus/scroll through preview and return, offer a clear replay action, and make touch preview toggleable. Six visible cards are a viewport, not a pack-size limit. Focus, press, loading, error, pause and result states need legible cues across keyboard, touch and controller; device reflow and reduced-effects variants remain proposals until tested.
