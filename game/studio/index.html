            Organize missions, campaigns & packs
            
      
              Choose an untested Solo / Versus island or a two-seat Team island template. Duplicate
              copies missions and whole campaign/pack contents with independent IDs; maps and
              artwork remain shared immutable pins. Editing geometry creates a private revision.
              Shared child membership is preserved within a copied pack. Archived children remain
              archived. Add to parent keeps existing memberships. Remove from parent detaches only
              that membership. Delete refuses referenced or nonempty items and never removes shared
              maps or assets. Archived items stay editable here but are omitted from candidate
              Journey lists. Changing a campaign band never retunes its missions: every member must
              already fit that band or the next (capped at 12), or the edit is rejected.
