Import your own replay

Choose a xonix-replay.v3, xonix-replay.v4 or xonix-replay.v5 or xonix-replay.v6 JSON file, or paste one below. Version 4 supports staged encounters; version 5 supports 72 × 36 boards with optional encounters; version 6 supports Classic rules. Versions 7–10 retain foundations, relays, directional fields and Sentinel encounters. A revealline-replay-presentation.v1 file also preserves recorded Solo FPV actors. The replay remains limited to 32 MiB / 30 recorded minutes, plus at most 12 KiB of appearance metadata. Verified inputs demonstrate reproducibility, not player identity or an authentic performance.

A different atmosphere.

Themes change the preview scene and raw replay actors. Recorded FPV actors stay pinned. The imported route, class abilities and result stay exactly as recorded. Original pictures, music and interface are not restored. This theater is silent.

The examples use normal movement, equipment and class-change inputs. They are demonstrations, not player completions. To try them yourself, install Fieldcraft in the game’s Library.
