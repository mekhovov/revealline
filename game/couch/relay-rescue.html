YOUR FIRST CONNECTION

  1. Leave the edge. Draw a line through the dark field and return to a safe route to claim it.
  2. Meet in the middle. Steer both moving heads together to bank a shared cut. Crossing an old part of a partner’s line is harmless.
  3. Watch the red shapes. Enemies can hit your craft and unfinished line. Hunters mark a route before charging. Wait for recovery or use Support to protect the crossing. Both approaches face the same enemy tools.
  4. Cover each other. Tap Support to slow nearby enemies and intercept sparks. Hold it beside a downed partner for one second to rescue them for free.
  5. Open both approaches. Capture anchors A and B to expose the core. Then make a new cut that captures the core itself. The shield blocks entry. Bait the Hunter’s warning and cut during recovery; banking its cell clears it.
