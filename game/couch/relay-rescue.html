How to play and controls

Read without starting or resuming.

Tap a direction to keep moving. Banking a cut stops your craft; steer again to leave. Escape or Menu pauses both players. Resume together is always explicit.

Sunflower: WASD steer, Left Shift Boost, Q Support.

Skyline: arrow keys steer, Right Shift Boost, Enter Support.

Controllers: stick or D-pad steers, right shoulder holds Boost, South uses Support, and Menu pauses. Each player keeps their controller assignment.
