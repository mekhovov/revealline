YOUR FIRST CONNECTION

  1. Start small. Draw a short loop back to safe ground to claim it. Banking stops your craft; steer again for your next cut.
  2. Join when ready. Steer both moving heads together to bank a shared cut. Crossing an old part of a partner’s line is harmless.
  3. Watch the red shapes. Enemies can hit your craft and unfinished line. Hunters mark a route before charging. Wait for recovery or use Support to protect the crossing. Both approaches face the same enemy tools.
  4. Cover each other. Tap Support to slow nearby enemies and intercept sparks. A dashed ring marks an enemy while it is slowed. Hold Support beside a downed partner for one second to rescue them for free.
  5. Open both approaches. Capture anchors A and B to expose the core. Then make a new cut that captures the core itself. The shield blocks entry. Bait the Hunter’s warning and cut during recovery; banking its cell clears it.

How to play and controls

Read without starting or resuming.

Tap a direction to keep moving. Banking a cut stops your craft; steer again to leave. Escape or Menu pauses both players. Resume together is always explicit.

Sunflower: WASD steer, Left Shift Boost, Q Support.

Skyline: arrow keys steer, Right Shift Boost, Enter Support.

Controllers: stick or D-pad steers, right shoulder holds Boost, South uses Support, and Menu pauses. Each player keeps their controller assignment.

Tap Support to slow nearby enemies and intercept sparks. Hold it beside a downed partner on safe ground for one second to rescue them. A new direction cancels a rescue. A downed player can crawl along safe ground to meet their partner.
