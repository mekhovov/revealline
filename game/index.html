THE SHORT VERSION

Make the next cut count.

  1. Blue ground is safe from field enemies. Leaving it begins a vulnerable gold line.
  2. Return to blue ground to close the cut. Regions without a field enemy are revealed.
  3. Enemies on both sides can leave only your line captured. Use that line to begin another cut.
  4. A field enemy touching your line, or crossing your own line, costs a life. Border patrols can also hit you on the outer edge.
  5. Reach the coverage target and capture any required gold objectives. The clock is for optional medals; it never ends your run.

Controls: arrows/WASD, Shift boost, E ability, R supply, Escape/P pause. Touch: hold a direction, or enable Tap steering and tap again/■ to stop. Boost uses the same hold/toggle choice. Standard controllers: D-pad/stick, A action, X supply, shoulder boost, Start pause.

Each class explains its actual effect in the flight deck. Scout shows current direction hints and hidden objectives; supplies refill charge-based classes. A shield absorbs one contact by safely cancelling the line.
