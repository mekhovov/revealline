## Scope

This bounded successor redesigns three existing Team identities. The v0.141.0 complete-specialist
edition and every earlier Team edition remain directly selectable with their original data,
pictures and progress ownership.

| Mission           | Distinct route decision                                                      | Concrete partner benefit                                                                 |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Stepping exchange | Take the nearer offset step or expose a longer cut to the central bank.      | The first secured step shortens the opposite pilot's next departure.                     |
| Divided workshop  | Work mirrored outer chambers independently or contest the open centre first. | A central bank lets either specialist cross over and protect the other chamber.          |
| Shared detour     | Neutralize the slow west passage or enclose the lethal east passage first.   | Either neutralized passage becomes ordinary shared ground leading to the central return. |

Actors, support roles, travelling-impact policy, pressure presets, objectives, coverage goals,
original pictures, campaign order and Next behavior remain unchanged. The successor changes only
walls, foundations, terrain placement and route guidance for these three identities.

## Cultural source boundaries

- [Museum Fund of Ukraine, Krolevets woven towel 108366](https://museum.mincult.gov.ua/collections/rushnik-kroleveckiy-108366)
  documents triangular side bands, small central rhomboid forms and end symmetry. Stepping
  exchange borrows only alternating side cadence and end symmetry.
- [Museum Fund of Ukraine, hand-woven runner 235942](https://museum.mincult.gov.ua/collections/235942)
  documents five zones, narrow separating bands, a central rhomboid focus and mirrored outer
  organization. Divided workshop borrows only zoned, mirrored and central-focus organization.
- [National Museum of Ukrainian Pottery, tactile ornament panels](https://opishne-museum.gov.ua/ornamenty-yaki-mozhna-vidchuty/)
  documents unequal openwork panels installed beside a route. Shared detour borrows only unequal
  spacing and alternating solid/open passages.

No source object, motif, weave draft, carving plan, stitch plan, palette, meaning or coordinates are
copied. The sources constrain broad compositional vocabulary; they do not prove gameplay balance or
cultural approval.
