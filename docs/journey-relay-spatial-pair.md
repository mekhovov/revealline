# Journey spatial successors: Relay pair

Status: candidate-only successor revisions; balance review pending.

This seventh copy-on-write batch changes only **First link** and **Second approach**. It preserves their mission IDs, names, reveal pictures, objectives, relay links and gates, policy/catalog identities, coverage targets, modes, campaign order and all earlier editions. It does not enroll these candidates in the default Journey or change a version or release.

## Route decisions

| Mission         | Short/pressured approach                                                                 | Longer/controlled approach                                                                               | Signature-mechanic proof                                                                                                                                     |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| First link      | Descend through the central relay between paired baffles and close on the upper landing. | Move west on the outer rail, use the open signal channel and establish the offset service landing first. | The central cut captures `landing-relay`, opens `south-bridge`, and a separate safe-ground movement crosses that bridge to the lower landing without damage. |
| Second approach | Descend through the eastern relay and close on the middle landing.                       | Travel along the outer rail, enter the open western stepped arch, and close on the left landing.         | The eastern cut captures `east-relay`, opens only `east-bridge`, and a separate safe-ground movement crosses it to the right landing without damage.         |

Neither gate awards coverage. Walls remain blocking geometry, reclaimed foundations remain the only authored internal return surfaces, and slow terrain affects the player only while unclaimed.

## Visual vocabulary

First link uses an original, abstract FPV antenna-diversity/service-link arrangement: symmetric baffles frame the active relay and a separate landing offers a longer fallback route. It is functional topology, not a literal component diagram.

Second approach uses original stepped wall openings inspired by the stepped-arch placement described for a Zakarpattia woven runner in Ukraine's Museum Fund. The museum object is cultural research only: no artwork, coordinates or ornamental drawing is copied. Source: [Museum Fund of Ukraine, runner with stylized flowers inside stepped arches](https://museum.mincult.gov.ua/collections/221109).

## Automated evidence and limits

Focused checks cover copy-on-write preservation, geometry/topology diagnostics, retained initial regions, idle opening safety, two distinct first returns for every Gentle/Standard/Expert and immediate/grid-center combination, and an actual relay-open-plus-gate-crossing route for each mission. These checks use deterministic seeds and the shared runtime resolver.

They do **not** prove full mission clears, final balance, enjoyment, readable failure explanations, physical keyboard/controller/touch behavior, small-screen legibility or human willingness to retry. Existing whole-Journey, historical and release acceptance remains outside this candidate PR.
