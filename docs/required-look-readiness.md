# Required selected-look readiness

Authored Solo Journey attempts prepare the selected craft and explicit scene overrides before replacing the previous run, result or picture. Choose, Next and Retry share this preparation owner. Direct briefing Start and Resume use the existing paused preparation owner, including restored authored flights; cancellation cannot bypass the guard through another Start control. One deliberate action enters play when picture and look are ready; failed or cancelled preparation keeps the previous owner and offers retry.

The painter's `prepareLook` is separate from its historical `setLook` API. Preparation checks the registered body, loads and decodes required images with a finite deadline, and returns a single-use adoption handle. The host verifies operation ownership before committing. A superseded look, changed compiled snapshot or aborted signal cannot adopt. Unadopted images lose their callbacks and source; accepted and borrowed pixels remain drawable.

Already available identical source pixels are reused, including successive Horizon missions. When both compact and detailed compiled FPV player sprites are decoded, their unused source bitmap does not block preparation. An explicit player override takes precedence and remains required. Both variants are checked because a viewport rotation must not expose an unprepared fallback.

This is a selected-look correction for authored Journey. Historical packs, imported practice and the existing cosmetic `setLook` error/fallback contract are unchanged. Enemy acquisition remains its existing separate presentation path. This change does not claim complete theme revision pins, full cross-mode readiness, offline Journey originals, or production acceptance of authored candidate missions.

## Verification and prompt

Use the actual Solo app, simulation and BoardPainter for the contract checks. Hold a selected Retro source before initial Start and verify the ready attempt stays paused; cancel or fail decoding and prove its old picture/run remain owned. Release a valid image and verify one deliberate Start enters play. Earn a Horizon victory through legal movement, then verify Next reuses its accepted craft and directly starts the named successor. Pair this with a real compiled FPV snapshot: hold only the unused original and prove the exact compiled player image still draws. Loading text alone is not evidence of missing required pixels.

Suggested prompt: “Qualify required selected-look preparation. Exercise delayed load, decode rejection, cancellation, timeout and stale completion with the previous run/result/picture retained. Verify exact-source reuse, compiled compact/detailed coverage and explicit override priority. Use legal capture and Next, include reduced effects, and keep browser, modeled input, decoded-image identity and physical-device evidence separate. Preserve simulation checkpoints and original-picture ownership.”

Source tests and local browser evidence do not establish public deployment. The release owner must run the complete source/build/artifact gates against the integrated commit, freeze it, deploy, and verify the same journey publicly before marking delivery accepted.

The Field Kit effects dependency fingerprint includes `game/ui/look-preparation.mjs` alongside the painter. Source changes reopen that recipe review. Integration must review the final combined renderer, regenerate its approved production record and compile the resulting manifest; old approval digests and frozen assets are not rewritten by this source packet.
