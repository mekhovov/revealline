# Phaseworks spatial successor pair

Status: candidate editions; default-route enrolment and human balance review remain separate.

This sixth batch stacks on the five earlier spatial pairs. It revises only
`two-ways-home` and `dogleg-transfer`. Mission IDs, names, Journey order, objectives,
coverage, gameplay policy, enemy-role identities, modes and presentation remain unchanged.
Earlier factories and historical editions remain available without reinterpretation.

## Research and cultural boundary

The candidates use the current fair pressure contract: ordinary movement remains straight
between physical impacts; a trail pursuer warns, commits for a finite route, then recovers;
walls can block movement and sensing but never close a cut; only reclaimed surfaces do.

Two ways home uses an original alternating-W composition inspired by a textile in the
[Museum Fund of Ukraine's Buchach makata catalogue](https://museum.mincult.gov.ua/collections/makat-138313).
The entry describes narrow horizontal bands containing alternating geometric/plant motifs,
including triangles, trident-like forms and W-shaped elements, completed by a rhombic
border. The mission borrows only the rhythm of mirrored W openings and alternating bands.
It does not reproduce the textile's layout, colours, weave or cultural meaning.

Dogleg transfer is an original FPV-workshop abstraction. Its central reclaimed form suggests
a propeller-balancing hub and dogleg fixture; surrounding walls suggest stand rails and
screens. It is gameplay geometry, not an assembly instruction or literal equipment diagram.

## Two ways home

The previous board spawned the craft inside a sixteen-cell-wide landing with a second small
foundation directly above it. The new pursuer was distinctive, but selecting a return often
required little spatial planning.

Distinct decision: connect the short central landing and accept immediate frontier pressure,
or make a long exposed approach through one W-band opening to establish a side return before
the pursuer commits.

Two intended approaches:

1. **Center first:** establish the central landing, read the frontier patrol and choose the
   side opposite the pursuer's visible commitment.
2. **W opening first:** follow the outer rail, stay outside the slow thread and descend
   through the open side of a woven band to a remote landing.

Capture consequence: the center creates two symmetric but pressured follow-ups. A side
landing first creates a long alternate closure and makes the mirrored unused return valuable
after the frontier moves.

## Dogleg transfer

The previous map was a broad permanent dogleg connected to the left and right outer rails.
It described screening and chamber choices, but the return itself was already available
across most of the board.

Distinct decision: connect the short balancing hub and use its perpendicular elbow to screen
the pursuer, or establish the upper service pad first while preserving the lower lethal
pocket for an outside enclosure.

Two intended approaches:

1. **Hub first:** connect the center, turn around the reclaimed elbow after a warning and
   close at an edge perpendicular to the pursuer's committed route.
2. **Upper pad first:** travel along the outer rail and descend to the side service pad,
   leaving the hub as a later screened connection.

Capture consequence: the hub offers differently oriented emergency returns behind two wall
screens. A service-pad connection changes the approach order without neutralizing either
occupied field chamber. The original slow and lethal pockets retain their authored IDs,
materials and positions.

## Focused qualification

The deterministic test proves:

- copy-on-write preservation of all other missions, art, objectives, policies/catalogs,
  pressure-role IDs and authored campaign/pack order;
- the same one finite `trail-pursuit` actor per mission with unchanged warning, commitment
  and recovery timings on every preset;
- one retained field, deliberate disconnected returns and no unexpected topology errors;
- identical Solo and Versus preparation on Gentle, Standard and Expert;
- 720 safe idle ticks over two seeds on every preset;
- both first-closure approaches over two seeds, all presets and both steering policies.

The scripted approaches close before demonstrating an active pursuit impact. They prove
opening reachability and that two routes are initially viable, not complete-clear pacing,
screening usefulness, lethal-pocket mastery, human enjoyment or device readability. Both
candidates remain **balance review pending**.
