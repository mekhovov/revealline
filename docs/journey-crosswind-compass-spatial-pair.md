# Journey spatial successors: Compass and outer-loop pair

Status: candidate-only successor revisions; balance review pending.

This tenth copy-on-write batch changes only **Compass array** and **Outer loop**. It preserves mission IDs, names, reveal pictures, actors, directional-speed zones, policy/catalog identities, coverage targets, modes, campaign order and every earlier edition. It does not enroll these candidates in the default Journey or change a version or release.

## Route decisions

| Mission       | First viable approach                                                           | Contrasting viable approach                                                                              | Capture consequence                                                                                                                            |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Compass array | Use the favourable north neck for a short fast closure under frontier pressure. | Cross the longer unmarked west arm to establish a quiet side return before using either neck.            | Capturing a neck neutralizes that directional field; the side arm instead creates an ordinary-speed departure for a later enclosure.           |
| Outer loop    | Take the nearby with-arrow lane directly to the western inner post.             | Reposition safely along the upper U, then make a short against-arrow dogleg onto the eastern outer post. | The inner closure creates a short central escape and expands reclaimed-ground pressure; the outer closure preserves separated post approaches. |

Walls block movement and never close a cut. Directional zones retain their exact authored cells and directions. They change movement speed only while unclaimed and never force a turn or drift. Reclaimed ground remains traversable but can still be threatened by the existing roamer role.

## Spatial vocabulary

Compass array places staggered baffles between the six marked bands, keeping the north/south necks and west/east arms readable as different route classes instead of one open rectangle.

Outer loop uses alternating shelf baffles above and below the inner posts. The nearby approach rewards a quick with-arrow commitment, while the outer approach begins with safe U-shaped repositioning before a compact dogleg through the marked column. The geometry is original and functional.

## Automated evidence and limits

Focused checks cover copy-on-write preservation, exact speed-zone preservation, geometry/topology diagnostics, initial retaining actors, idle-opening safety and two distinct first closures for every Gentle/Standard/Expert and immediate/grid-center combination. Route checks confirm the intended closure, stop-on-capture behavior and unchanged paired-board manifests.

These checks do **not** prove full mission clears, final balance, enjoyment, physical keyboard/controller/touch behavior, compact-layout legibility or human willingness to retry. Existing whole-Journey, historical and release acceptance remains outside this candidate PR.
