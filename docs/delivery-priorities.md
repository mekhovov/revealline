## Contracts to preserve

- **Arcade:** steer, close cuts and collect authored benefits. Generic Scan, Supply and Boost stay absent; difficulty comes from readable threats and route decisions.
- **Tactical:** abilities, payloads, objectives, interference and other abstract interactions are explicitly authored by the level/class. They are not silently added to Arcade.
- **Rewards:** record the earned full image before optional, skippable story playback. Coverage, first-earned results and repeated Collection playback remain truthful.
- **Controls:** keep useful touch controls outside the arena, with an explicit side choice. Keyboard/controller menus retain visible focus and consistent Back behavior. Returning focus never resumes a paused flight.
- **Recovery:** reading a stored original does not certify that the selected profile earned it, create executable campaign authority or constitute a complete backup. No writer stealing, adoption, database upgrade, silent deletion or cap increase is included in P5.READ.
