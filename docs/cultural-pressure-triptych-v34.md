# Ukrainian cultural pressure triptych · v34 candidate

This bounded candidate revises three existing Journey identities without deleting or
rewriting their v33 editions:

| Mission        | Spatial decision                                                                                                 | Cultural design source                                          |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Garden refuges | Short lethal-row enclosure versus a longer outer route that establishes the far refuge first                     | Reshetylivka whitework: alternating open and solid spacing only |
| Broken yard    | Bank the west escape window or neutralize the east pocket before the reclaimed-ground roamer controls its return | Kosiv ceramics: unequal framed-field organization only          |
| Island reserve | Accept the slow west approach or bank the longer clear east reserve before erosion reaches an earned link        | Crimean Tatar Örnek: broad grouped geometric organization only  |

The layouts are original gameplay abstractions. They do not copy source ornaments,
symbols, coordinates, palettes, meanings, objects or artwork. The source records and
adaptation boundaries remain in the compiled design record.

## Compatibility boundary

- `whole-spatial-v34` is a new opt-in edition with its own profile and suspended-flight
  key.
- v33 and every older route remain byte-pinned and selectable.
- Only the three selected maps gain wall geometry and updated design evidence.
- Actors, movement policy, difficulty preparation, objectives, bonuses, timed bonuses,
  foundations, terrain, spawns, reveal artwork and presentation pins remain exact.
- Solo and Versus resolve identical prepared boards. Team content is unchanged.
- All three wall fields retain one communicating field region and a safe stationary
  opening on Gentle, Standard and Expert.

This is a technically verified balance candidate, not human proof that either route is
enjoyable or optimally tuned. Human route-choice and retry testing remains required
before making v34 the default Journey.
