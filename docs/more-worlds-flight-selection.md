# More worlds: Download & play

The Solo chapter browser provides one **Download & play** action for an available
chapter and **Play** for its verified installed copy. The same button remains
the return destination as a download becomes installed. Playing an installed
chapter does not download it again. Manual exact-pair recovery remains
installation-only; it never starts a recovered flight.

The action authenticates the selected published bytes and original pictures,
then prepares the new run, recorder and decoded picture before replacing the
current attempt. The current field or result remains available until adoption.
A failed or cancelled preparation retains it. A completed installation stays
installed even if the subsequent launch is cancelled; Play retries preparation.
Installation success means the store's commit has completed, not merely that an
individual write request succeeded.

An unfinished flight requires **Stay** or **Replace & play**. The dialog reports
a verified save only after replay verification and storage readback succeed.
Stay retains the paused flight and restores its current Play button. Early Stay
does not wait for an obsolete storage callback. A changed saved slot requires a
new explicit replacement decision. After confirmed replacement and successful
preparation, the accepted attempt starts without a second Start action.

After Stay, the chapter browser reports that the flight is kept paused and that
Play remains available. It must not keep instructions for the closed replacement
dialog. This ready status belongs to the returning operation; an old decision or
late preparation callback cannot overwrite a newer operation's status.

Play is an explicit request for a fresh attempt even when the chapter is already
selected. The current difficulty and shared mission access choose the chapter's
next available mission; records and awards remain difficulty-specific. A pack's
first authored campaign remains the card destination; Missions retains access
to its other campaigns. Complete community/campaign/missions catalogue
consolidation is still separate P06 work.

The activation lease starts before download. Closing the browser, cancelling,
changing page/filter, losing foreground, opening another host surface or starting
newer work retires automatic launch. A delayed callback may finish a durable
install but cannot replace a newer attempt, close its menu or steal focus.
After adoption, callbacks are checked against the committed run, recorder,
picture owner and generation before Resume. An error after adoption is reported
as a changed field, not a successful rollback.

The requested pack object and authored campaign identity travel together.
Difficulty and result continuation preserve that pack's presentation metadata.
Historical picture identities and earned originals remain unchanged. If two
installed owners share a campaign/theme identity but have different artwork,
fresh Play reports a conflict before changing the run: historical media
assignments cannot prove which edition owns that identity. It never silently
substitutes the first pack's image. Authors must give distinct artwork editions
distinct campaign identities until complete presentation ownership is qualified.

The pinned browsing label **Chapter in view** denotes the displayed card, not
the active flight. Legacy panel callers that supply only Choose hooks preserve
their selection-only contract; ordinary Library and setup navigation are
unchanged.

## Verification and maintenance

The new panel lifetime suite covers the original activation, exact descriptor,
explicit host completion, cancellation, foreground retirement, stable focus and
deferred replacement. The real-host suite authenticates ordinary published pack
bytes and uses managed media through the actual app, with modeled DOM, storage
and image boundaries. Existing mission replacement and result-continuation
suites remain required. Native rendering, physical controls, full source gates
and public-release journeys are separate acceptance evidence.

Maintenance prompt: “Extend Download & play through the existing exact download
and committed-install authorities. Capture one activation before awaiting
network/storage. Hold the previous run, result and decoded picture until the
candidate is ready. Exercise install failure, delayed/failed decode, cancellation
after commit, early Stay, changed saved bytes, storage-read reentry and obsolete
disposal callbacks. Verify that Stay replaces the closed dialog's instruction
with a current paused-flight status, even before old preparation settles, and
that late callbacks cannot overwrite newer status. Prove one accepted start with
no new award, correct exact pack/difficulty, no duplicate download on Play, and
truthful post-adoption error copy. Preserve manual recovery and legacy Choose behavior. Record modeled,
native and deployed checks separately.”

This candidate is not evidence of full P06 acceptance or a public release.
