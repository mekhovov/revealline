# Journey spatial successors: Nested relays and watchposts

Status: candidate-only successor revisions; balance review pending.

This ninth copy-on-write batch changes only **Nested relays** and **Watchpost exchange**. It preserves mission IDs, names, reveal pictures, objectives, relay links and gates, policy/catalog identities, coverage targets, modes, campaign order and every earlier edition. It does not enroll these candidates in the default Journey or change a version or release.

## Route decisions

| Mission            | First viable approach                                                                          | Contrasting viable approach                                                                                         | Capture consequence                                                                                                           |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Nested relays      | Climb through the slowed upper-ring lane, capture `upper-relay`, and open the long inner link. | Move across the inner shelf and drop through `inner-relay` to open the lower bridge first.                          | Each relay removes a different return detour; neither moves a keeper or performs a hidden second fill.                        |
| Watchpost exchange | Drop directly through the nearby northwest lane to open `west-junction`.                       | Travel safely around the outer perimeter, then cut upward through the southeast lane to open `east-junction` first. | The junctions assemble the cross-board reclaimed route independently while each occupied watch sector keeps its own pressure. |

Walls block movement and never close a cut. Reclaimed foundations remain the authored return network. Slow terrain affects the player only while unclaimed. Field keepers continue straight between physical impacts under the shared policy.

## Spatial vocabulary

Nested relays adds offset baffles outside and between the two existing safe rings. The upper approach is short but slowed, while the inner drop is longer and exposed beside the inner keeper.

Watchpost exchange uses four asymmetric L-shaped watch structures around the existing hub. The two required relays deliberately face opposite directions: one begins near the spawn, while the other rewards a safe perimeter transfer before an upward cut. The structures are original functional geometry, not copied artwork.

## Automated evidence and limits

Focused checks cover copy-on-write preservation, geometry/topology diagnostics, initial retaining actors, idle-opening safety, and two distinct first closures for every Gentle/Standard/Expert and immediate/grid-center combination. Signature-route checks capture the intended objective, open only its matching gate and finish without damage under the shared runtime resolver.

These checks do **not** prove full mission clears, final balance, enjoyment, physical keyboard/controller/touch behavior, compact-layout legibility or human willingness to retry. Existing whole-Journey, historical and release acceptance remains outside this candidate PR.
