## Expansion rule

P3 names Tropical, Railway, Coaster and Hillside first. Source intake and bounded authoring studies may proceed independently, but default player admission must wait for the ownership, art and package gates above. A new preparation must deliver a different flight activity and composition, not a recolour or duplicated district. Preserve older course identities and proofs; moving subjects require explicit runtime support rather than pretending static models animate.

The next Railway source set is intentionally stylized. Its proposed trestle, switching yard and freight lanes will be reviewed as an authored region study, not as a realistic or released environment. The proposed 4 km dense-region target remains separate from any bounded preparation.
