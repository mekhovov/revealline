Prepared 14 October 2026. Source reviewed: `5f126d6105be5708948dbaff6907ca5cd7e15baf`.

**Recommendation: add 96 genuinely new Team levels and 72 purpose-built Duel levels in twelve chapter families, delivered in small, complete batches.** Use Solo's learning progression, enemies, materials and presentation as the foundation, with cooperative layouts for Team and racing decisions for Duel. Make existing supplementary campaigns easier to discover as a separate improvement.

## Implementation review and improvements

The second review keeps the 96 Team / 72 Duel target. It changes execution in five ways:

1. **Give each chapter a small playable entry point.** Teach its distinguishing action in the opening two stages, then combine it with previous lessons. Nintendo's [Splatoon 3 developer interview, part 4](https://www.nintendo.com/us/whatsnew/ask-the-developer-vol-7-splatoon-3-part-4/) describes early stages tuned for comfortable controls and lessons players can immediately try. Application here: every chapter remains approachable from the library, without assuming completion of Solo.
2. **Vary the decision, not just the scenery.** Hazelight's [Split Fiction overview](https://hazelight.se/games/split-fiction) describes varied cooperative activities across different worlds. Our adaptation is an authored decision inventory: each level records two approaches, the consequence of the first capture, and a particular partner benefit or racing tradeoff. A geometric fingerprint also compares reflections and rotations; review near duplicates before counting them.
3. **Pair visual warnings with other cues.** [Xbox Accessibility Guideline 103](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/103) recommends additional sensory channels and color-independent signifiers. Every new danger keeps a visible shape/state, a text explanation and the existing sound-event path. Reduced motion may remove decoration, never the warning interval. Muted and mono reviews are separate checks.
4. **Separate code completion from external acceptance.** Each phase has source, automated, browser and external-review columns. Human-pair feedback, physical-controller/device testing and publisher adoption require their own evidence. Continue all available implementation work while those checks remain outstanding; never mark them passed because a simulation passes.
5. **Make the chapter the portable unit.** Store mode-specific editable sources, stable identities, exact compiled rules and media references together. Reuse verified environment/actor/sound resources when appropriate, and report that reuse honestly. The first eight levels must round-trip through the real compiler and Studio before scaling. Keep old routes callable and old saved progress intact.

Research above was accessed 14 October 2026. Applications are design decisions for this project, not claims that these games use our architecture. Public publication remains a final deployment step after concrete chapters and evidence exist; source implementation proceeds now.

## Content target and variety rules

The proposed first programme contains **twelve families × eight Team levels = 96**, and **twelve families × six Duel levels = 72**. The small pilot below is included in these totals.

- Team's curated Journey grows from 12 to 108; retaining both Classic arenas gives at least 110 library choices before separately admitting existing supplementary content.
- Duel retains its 91 shared Journey levels and adds 72 dedicated races, giving 163 in those two groups. Keeping all current library rows would give 312 displayed choices, but that is not an installed-content or unique-geometry guarantee.
- No recolor, mirrored copy, difficulty preset, historical revision, re-listed Pursuit stage or four-theme reskin counts as a new level. Each of the 168 must have distinct authored topology and a documented new route/cooperation decision.
- Use geometry/actor/objective signatures to flag duplicates and near-duplicates, followed by designer review. Hash differences alone do not establish variety.
- A Team chapter has two teaching levels, three combinations, two coordination tests and one finale. A Duel chapter has two sprints, two route-choice races, one sustained-pressure race and one finale. These are design roles within existing win contracts.
- Each level needs at least two useful strategic choices, readable counterplay, a recovery opportunity and a specific memorable moment. Team must demonstrate how one player's action benefits the other. Do not impose artificial waits just to require two people.
- Gentle/Standard/Expert remain presets, not additional levels. Keep both Immediate and Grid + buffer steering. Set initial time-to-clear targets during the pilot; avoid solving tedium by raising every enemy's speed or coverage quota.

## Fairness, verification and delivery gates

Team exposes immediate direction input, not Solo’s alternate grid-center turning policy. For every Team level, run three difficulties × two seat/role assignments: **six supported combinations**, or 576 for 96 levels. Save replayable ordinary-input evidence and include early/late rescue, one pilot downed, simultaneous cuts, contested shared bonuses and exposed stronghold transitions where applicable. Add seed coverage only for genuinely seeded behavior. Test joint action ordering explicitly; do not require a timed “synchronized press” unless its input-window contract has been designed and taught.

For every Duel level, run three difficulties × two turning policies × two seat assignments: **12 baseline combinations**, or 864 for 72 levels, plus equal-input draws, simultaneous final clears, one/both losses, Pause/Resume, rematch and Next. Match identical options/seeds, difficulty, art readability and warning opportunities. Draws must be judged after both boards receive the same simulation tick. Preserve legacy timed outcomes. Avoid hidden catch-up rules.

These numbers are planned coverage counts, not results. Replays must bind source/rules/presentation identities. Current focused tests can anchor new coverage: `content-project`, `content-team-export`, `team-campaign-export`, `team-cultural-specialist-v2-originals`, `coop-live-rescue`, `pursuit-campaigns`, `multiplayer`, `multiplayer-untimed`, `multiplayer-encounter` and `multiplayer-recording` under `game/test/`. Add tests for new behavior and failures rather than repeating static layout literals.

For visuals and usability, sample each chapter on desktop, compact landscape and portrait layouts, then qualify actual touch/controllers and handheld/native targets separately.
