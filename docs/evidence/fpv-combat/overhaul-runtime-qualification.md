# Combat runtime and replay qualification

## Implemented contracts

- New definitions carry immutable `crowdModel: squad-lanes-v1`: distinct approach lanes, UAV altitude bands, full-body spacing and four staggered contact cohorts. Contact actors warn for half a second, then receive up to 9.5 seconds to approach. A slow 0.6 m/s rover reaches real contact from 3.2 metres even under the shield-front speed modifier. Older v2 definitions without this tag retain their exact historical movement and identity.
- Historical encounter v1 remains separately dispatched. New attempts opt into v2 definitions, accepted commands, recovery and recording identities.
- Four weapons, two equipped slots, three payload types, ammunition presets, reload progress, component damage, repairs, ammunition supplies and service pads use accepted simulation ticks.
- Continuous, Hunting, Elimination, Strike, six-minute Survival and Endless Survival use the same encounter authority. The fixed final enemy is reserved before Survival begins.
- Cancellation and rejected aircraft or resident-geometry intervals preserve encounter state, ammunition, random state and pending commands.
- Hunting replacements wait 200–400 accepted ticks and select another admitted route point when one exists.
- Actor identities and collision generations support 128 slots. Military appearances are resolved into the immutable definition.
- Surface impacts and defeats have separate bounded 1,024-record ledgers. Unsupported airborne pickups remain pending until a valid landing surface exists.
- v2 admission requires a clear firing line from the reachable launch position to at least one point on the fully supported actor route, within the equipped weapon range. This is conservative firing-position evidence, not global navigation.
- Service pads are immutable supported positions. Admission checks a landing patch, body clearance and a clear approach from the actual launch. Airborne regional restarts include the verified supporting floor. Tactical is unavailable without a validated pad.
- Regional shots and falling ordnance sweep their covered boundary segment before expiring. They do not query missing neighboring cells or invent a wall at the encounter boundary. Uncovered muzzle/release points block only the weapon and consume no ammunition; the accepted flight interval continues.
- Projectile collision centres remain separate from actual surface-contact positions, placing persistent marks on walls. Non-shot explosions never create shot-accuracy IDs; blocked-rocket splash counts once. Impact self-damage uses actual inflicted health, including overkill.
- Physical destruction overrides ordinary combat victory. Strike may resolve already-deployed ordnance for twenty accepted seconds; surviving targets retain their last accepted collider poses during that aftermath.
- Playback reconstructs accepted commands in the exact fresh Standard/Advanced backend and checks the final encounter and accepted physical dynamics. It does not substitute a restored final checkpoint for replay.
- v2 retains twelve complete one-minute recording segments plus its current partial segment. Historical v1 retains three. A six-minute attempt retains its beginning. Combat-specific IndexedDB archives retain sealed accepted command segments separately, so complete exported/finished archives can replay beyond live-ring rollover. A truncated recovery alone still cannot claim complete verification.
- Internal `frameSnapshot()`/`stepFrame()` views omit private actor routes and combat bookkeeping, copy and freeze presentation data, and share one view per accepted state. Public snapshots, ordinary step results and recovery retain their full defensive-copy contract. New-rule contact attacks preserve two seconds of spawn grace, then use their incoming-damage cooldown independently of the player’s same-target impact lock.
- Optional independently bounded launch metadata stores configuration, flight mode and original regional starting fields. Invalid optional metadata does not invalidate valid gameplay; exact backend identity still controls admission.

## Limits of this evidence

Short catalogue starts establish admission and identity compatibility. They are not proof that every authored objective can be completed, that every possible player route is reachable, or that gameplay is enjoyable. Survival pursuit uses local supported motion and separation checks; a complete global navigation solution is not claimed. Complete finished/exported combat archives retain the required chain beyond the recent ring. Interrupted open archives without a final paired boundary remain explicitly incomplete; truncated recovery files without an archive cannot be replayed or verified. See `overhaul-combat-archive-contract.md` for storage limits and lifecycle semantics. Strike aftermath freezes targets after the aircraft is destroyed because the existing physical backends have no versioned actor-only accepted transaction.

GPU cost, frame pacing, complete renderer population, visual quality, physical-radio behavior, six-minute human play and the five-player satisfaction gate are separate qualification work owned by the application/browser reports. This document does not claim those gates passed.

The autosave tests use a transactional IndexedDB implementation in Node; actual large-save browser recovery is recorded separately. Browser storage remains subject to quota and eviction. Autosave ordering is qualified within a host instance, not across simultaneous tabs. The dedicated latest recovery is separate from the bounded command archive and retains the complete gameplay, presentation and launch envelope.
