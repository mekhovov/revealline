## Evidence boundaries

- **Model:** separately recorded executed tests must name their source revision and finite DOM/canvas/audio boundaries. Merely finding a test is not a pass.

- **Listening:** not performed. F13 remains a distinct listening review. Speaker/headphone output, mix intelligibility and mobile interruption behavior cannot be inferred from oscillator calls or audio doubles.
- **AT/device:** no new screen-reader, switch-control, iPhone/PWA, controller or physical-device evidence. F18 and F57 retain these gaps independently.

The inventory concerns information needed to start/stop safely, avoid hazards, understand damage/recovery, use limited resources, make objective progress and recognize an outcome. Ambient motors, footsteps and decorative dialogue are not automatically critical warnings. Imported content and authored specialist successors require their own admitted scenarios; their availability is not proof that every public/default mission uses them.

## Decisions before F07b

No blanket “missing visual cues” change is justified by this source pass. Existing caption, HUD, telegraph and recovery paths should remain authoritative. Candidate work must begin with a specific admitted scenario and an observed failure: missing information, incorrect ownership, unreadable collision, insufficient dwell, or information dependent only on a sensory channel.

Highest-value browser checks are C02/C03, T01/T03, S01/S03, O01/O02 and F01/F02. For each, record normal and reduced effects, master mute, reaction captions on/off where relevant, and a bounded busy-state comparison. A normal browser screenshot does not verify audio output or assistive technology. Absence of a browser or listening receipt is an open evidence requirement, not proof that the cue is absent.

F03 owns broad art/readability changes; F09 owns scaling; F31 owns caption dwell/history; F13 owns listening/mix. F07b should add only the missing functional alternative or fix demonstrated cue loss, with the exact triggering scenario rerun.
