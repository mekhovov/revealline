## Cultural source boundaries

- The [Ivan Honchar Museum Podillia woven towel record](https://honchar.org.ua/collections/detail/2200)
  documents geometric ornament, squares of varying sizes arranged into schematic quatrefoils and
  terminal bands. First link borrows grouped-square and terminal-band organization only. It does
  not copy the towel, ornament, weave draft, palette, meaning or source coordinates.
- The [Museum Fund of Ukraine kilim record 62650](https://museum.mincult.gov.ua/collections/kilim-62650)
  documents vertical symmetry around a center, alternating smooth and ornamented horizontal bands
  and curved-down band edges. Second approach borrows bilateral and alternating-band organization
  only. It does not copy the kilim, ornament, palette, meaning or source coordinates.
- The [Museum Fund of Ukraine kilim record 56857](https://museum.mincult.gov.ua/collections/kilim-56857)
  documents a central field and border, alternating branch direction by row and a winding
  right-angle border. Three compounds borrows alternating branch cadence and right-angle border
  organization only. It does not copy the kilim, ornament, palette, meaning or source coordinates.

These records support cultural context and broad compositional vocabulary. They do not establish
gameplay balance, permission to copy an object or universal symbolic meanings.
