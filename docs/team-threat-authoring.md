# Team threat overlays

Asset Studio → Prepare Team threat slots adds a separate, exact three-slot family to the selected theme. It does not change the earlier four feedback contracts. Initial bindings retain existing procedural cues and source-stage provenance; registration is not art approval.

| Slot                          | Frame | Runtime meaning and placement                                                    | Earned Relay Yard specimen |
| ----------------------------- | ----- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| `team.threat.emitter-warning` | 32×32 | Exact targeted cut cell; keep core-to-cell ray and target square                 | Emitter warning            |
| `team.threat.spark`           | 16×16 | Actual moving sub-cell impact position, not its last grid cell or a nearby badge | Travelling spark           |
| `team.threat.shield`          | 64×64 | Shielded, undefeated core at its real position; capture anchors first            | Initial field              |

Use transparent centered overlays, an open middle and distinct silhouettes. Runtime paints shields below core bodies and warning/spark accents above decorative bodies; true contact markers, warning paths and labels remain above all overlays. The spark stays compact; no new hit area is implied. Board edges clip artwork rather than shifting its source. Declared images must prepare with exact dimensions, centered pivots and revisions before any snapshot is adopted. Invalid required images fail visibly while the existing snapshot stays usable. Old collections without these slots keep their previous appearance.

Warning timing remains simulation-owned, including pause and reduced effects. Shield artwork disappears for exposed or defeated cores. Spark artwork follows a valid existing impact with an active cutting player, retained unclaimed trail cell and undefeated owner. Cell-only historical inputs can supply a valid cell fallback only when both coordinates are absent; malformed or partially missing coordinates are not mixed into a fabricated position. These checks read state without modifying the simulation.

Native size inspects uploaded pixels; procedural defaults explain their existing cue and point to the actual scene. Upload, crop, single-layer editing, exact history, Undo/Redo, save/reload, bundle import/export and generated theme prompts remain in the local authoring workflow. Imports require all three exact contracts. Publishing any selected role requires all three reviewed declarations and meaningful evidence.

Qualification must prove image visibility, not merely draw calls. Inspect warning art against the ray/reticle, spark pixels around the true center, and shield pixels around shared core artwork over light/dark pictures at phone/desktop sizes. Check native uploaded images, malformed assets, atomic rejection, pause/reduced effects, multi-core ownership, cleared/intercepted impacts, edge clipping, and unchanged started simulation. Distinguish synthetic layout stress, earned traces, native browser, physical devices and public release evidence. Joint-capture and full edition art remain separate work.
