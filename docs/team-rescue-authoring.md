# Editable Team rescue and recovery

`team.rescue.progress` and `team.player.recovery` accept centered 32×32 transparent images or the registered `team.rescue.v1` recipe. The contact-rescue decoration appears under the rescuer. The recovery decoration appears during the existing post-revival grace interval. These are distinct from Support and shield pickups.

Use **Add Team presentation slots** to append missing contracts to an older Studio workspace without changing its accepted assets or historical revisions. Search the slot, select **Field context → Couch Team → Relay Yard**, then inspect **Rescuing player 1/2** or **Player 1/2 recovered**. The scenarios come from public controls on the unchanged arena; no simulation fields are invented. Both arenas support the bindings, but only Relay Yard currently provides recorded rescue specimens. An inactive scene explicitly reports no active rescue or grace.

Upload sparse corner decoration with an empty center and center pivot (0.5,0.5). Record actual provenance; validate and stage. Inspect native size, both players, normal and reduced effects, paused and playing states. Save, export the actual `.rltheme`, re-import and verify history before adopting a release. The reduced-effects variant lowers decoration opacity without changing timer or geometry.

Decoration is painted before every actor body and fixed identity cue. The engine retains player numbers/shapes and the dashed recovery ring. Registered rescue presentation adds the actual contact-rescue percentage to the fixed target-number label; its one-second timer comes from existing simulation state, with no invented loading progress. Real Pause releases contact rescue; Studio Paused holds its isolated command-earned specimen. Artwork cannot alter rescue eligibility, duration, cancellation, reserves, grace, collision or input-release behavior.

Malformed advertised frames fail before replacing a painter snapshot. Historical themes without the slots keep their original cues. Production art approval, full P04/P08 acceptance, physical devices and exact committed public release remain separate requirements. Uploaded geometric QA fixtures only exercise authoring and byte preservation.
