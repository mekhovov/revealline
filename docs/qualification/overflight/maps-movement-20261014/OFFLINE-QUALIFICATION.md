## Remaining anomaly and limits

After the first successful `3bb → 4dab` update on 8936, the custom mission's exact URL reported that it was not installed. Reimport was necessary for the next Creator check. The [read-only investigation](native-package-persistence-investigation.md) and [source hashes](native-package-persistence-source.json) found no intended version migration, session-only storage or updater database-deletion path. No cause is established and no speculative storage patch was applied. The second `4dab → e914` retention check passed, as did the independent repeat of `3bb → 4dab` on 8940. The latter explicitly saved the draft and closed/reopened Studio before updating. These are recorded sequence differences, not established causes or fixes. Saving a draft remains separate from installing a package. The original observation remains unexplained and unreproduced; one-package checks do not establish general preservation across every library and browser profile.

These checks do not qualify every layout offline, every optional package, complete sorties, OS installation, physical airplane mode or the waived external devices. They do not establish audio quality or performance. Existing worker/closure tests supplement the native actions rather than replace them.
