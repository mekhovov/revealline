## Verification and final review fixes

- The combined Overflight/Raid/offline unit run passed **359 tests** in 109.5 seconds; its complete output is preserved in `unit-suite.log.gz`. This includes the existing dense simulation checks. That run preceded the final presentation-only fixes below.
- Final renderer review found the Sweeper's follow-sector guide pointing forward despite its rear-facing attack. The guide now uses the same rear axis as simulation; follow-sector boundaries are hidden during Focus approach/engagement, where they do not constrain targeting. The renderer suite passed **18/18** after the correction.
- V3 Survivor/Riy supply instructions now describe the stored Overdrive choice, fresh-boost activation, six-second duration and no stacking/refresh. Legacy and Raid instructions stay unchanged.
- Diagnostics review playback accepts `reviewStopAt=120..240` only with a valid `reviewBuild`. At that simulation time all movement, boost and Focus inputs stop until Retry, including after airframe replacement. Earned upgrades still resolve normally. The stop is disclosed in the review label and exported review identity. This supports reproducible native active-versus-unattended review without granting a build or mutating simulation state.
- Host and presentation suites passed **31/31** after those final changes. Scoped ESLint passed for the changed runtime and test files. The new focused suites are registered in `publishing/industrial-feature-tests.json`.

The final independent review confirmed the renderer cue mismatch above; it did not establish another high-impact simulation, versioning or record-partition defect. This is a review result, not proof that all defects or balance issues are absent.

## Supported burst speed qualification

A minimal V3 real-step check measured a flank sprinter at **144 units/s without support** and **155.52 units/s with the existing radar support bonus (+8%)**, with a unit heading and no knockback. The plan's 144 value describes the base burst; radar support remains a visible priority-target benefit. Cell-centroid separation is explicitly disabled during a burst, so dense crowds do not amplify that burst vector. Knockback remains a separate displacement. No tuning changed for this qualification check.

## Evidence boundaries and remaining review

Implementation and local qualification are recorded in [the final report](README.md): native active/unattended clips, three restarted performance trials, and Creator export/import/native/offline checks. Balance acceptance remains partial: the preserved Echo + Pulse attempts do not establish a completion route, late upgrade gaps need review, and some crowd peaks exceed the intended composition envelope. Captures preserve master mute and therefore do not qualify audio quality. Earlier soak/retry/context evidence predates the final boundary/menu correction and is identified as such.

The unavailable Iris Xe/Windows reference laptop, M1 Safari, physical controller/phone and five-player formative sessions were explicitly waived by the user. They remain **skipped, not passed**. Automated pilots and state-reading route matrices establish reachability and regression behavior, not human enjoyment, a calibrated difficulty gradient or valid medal timings. Time Attack defaults remain provisional. The preserved Raid Marathon failure and interrupted/superseded balance receipts remain available rather than being excluded from the report.

## Latest-main integration

Merging main after the parent PRs landed exposed two inherited operation-menu integration issues: Settings reparented the operation cards out of Home, and identical Left/Right fallback lists trapped horizontal navigation between the first two of five choices. The shared app now leaves operation cards on Home; each card routes to the next or previous operation cyclically. A production-configuration host test preserves Home ownership through Settings initialization, and keyboard/controller tests traverse all five operations in both directions from every selected operation. Simulation rules are unchanged by these menu corrections.
