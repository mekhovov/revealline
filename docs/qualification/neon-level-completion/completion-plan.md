## Research translated into acceptance

- [Microsoft XAG 102](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/gaming/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/102): inspect important marks against the lowest-contrast part of a variable background. Examples include Hyperdot's light/dark contrast modes and configurable colors in Battlefield. Review trail core/outline, enemy silhouettes and objective separation across every painting, at desktop and narrow arena sizes. Keep authored images recognizable; do not regenerate a painting merely to conceal a renderer issue.
- [WebKit Clipboard API](https://webkit.org/blog/10855/async-clipboard-api/): writes are asynchronous and browser policies differ. Qualify the actual user gesture plus an exact manually selectable link when writes fail; retain the exact map and edition. Do not read a player's unrelated clipboard to verify success.
- [Service-worker lifecycle](https://web.dev/articles/service-worker-lifecycle): old and new clients/caches can coexist. Preserve reviewed migration and installation boundaries, test an interrupted chapter transfer separately from runtime preparation, then verify retry and offline play.
- [NN/g progress indicators](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/progress-indicators/): visible feedback reduces uncertainty. Pending creator/image work must expose cancellation; cancellation or newer input must prevent stale preview replacement. A loading indicator does not establish successful completion.

- [Ukrainian Institute of America — Pysanka](https://pysanka.ukrainianinstitute.org/): distinguish egg decoration and textile-inspired terrain from fantasy animal paintings. Its curator describes kalyna, wheat and periwinkle in the context of a particular installation; do not present one interpretation as a universal dictionary. [UNESCO Petrykivka](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/petrykivka-decorative-painting-as-a-phenomenon-of-the-ukrainian-ornamental-folk-art-00893) provides a living floral tradition as a reference for branching rhythm and local flora/fauna. Keep Crimean Tatar attribution for Ornek. Existing art remains an interpretation, not a claim of museum reproduction.
