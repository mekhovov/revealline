**Historical scope:** the 15 October table below records composition and static-layout review. Its “in-game acceptance pending” cells describe that earlier pass. The authorized unlock and 16 October painter review below supersede the password limitation. Physical-device and human balance approval are not claimed.

The 16 screenshot reconstructions retain their recognizable arrangement and enemy types. Their documented projection uses a 72 × 36 board and continuous safe outer rail; screenshot border segments are not a pixel-for-pixel physics specification. The 38 word/symbol layouts retain readable motifs, varied side refuges, gates, slow fields and lower layouts. No new geometry change was needed to obtain the recorded complete Standard wins. Static uniqueness and successful routes do not, by themselves, establish enjoyable variety.

All cultural pictures are artistic interpretations. In particular the horse, deer and fish paintings are naturalistic/fantasy animals paired with pysanka-inspired terrain, not reproductions of traditional painted eggs. Ornek is identified as Crimean Tatar; no universal symbolism is asserted.
