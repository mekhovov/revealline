## Player journey

For the exact approved built-in Relay Rescue starter pack, an earned First Connection result offers **Next: Relay Yard**, Retry, View picture and Choose arena. One Next action prepares the successor and starts it with the accepted difficulty, teamwork policy and existing input assignments. Hidden setup values cannot change that recipe. Preparation immediately announces progress without invented percentages; Cancel and Back keep the result and exact earned original. Choosing another action, backgrounding or leaving the page revokes the pending start. Returning focus alone never revives it.

An earned Relay Yard result says **Final arena in this pack** and prioritizes Choose arena. It does not claim the player completed both arenas: direct entry remains possible and this feature adds no campaign-completion ledger. Validated imported Team packs follow their authored levels array within the accepted pack snapshot. Next names and starts the following arena; the final entry offers Choose arena and Retry without claiming whole-pack completion. Matching IDs or later source changes cannot replace the retained itinerary.

## Ownership and recovery

The pending successor owns a fresh core and, when required, a separate picture lease. The completed run, accepted recipe, picture and result remain current through download, decode, cancellation and failed preparation. Starter-namespace content must confirm its exact approved picture binding before adoption, including an exact imported copy. Other validated historical imports use the explicit approved generic artwork policy and the same owned picture preparation path; see [Team import artwork](team-import-artwork.md). A first-paint failure retains the result; an immediate publication paint failure rolls back while the same foreground action still owns the transition. A newer dialog, lifecycle or explicit action is never overwritten by rollback.

After successful adoption, the previous picture is released and setup and Help reflect the successor. Only the literal built-in source owner updates the built-in arena bookmark or in-visit selection; imports preserve both, even when their levels reuse built-in IDs. Retry uses the accepted successor recipe and picture. The simulation begins only at adoption; preparation does not advance its clock. This feature preserves existing instantaneous launch timing. Countdown/motion qualification remains P08-B work.

No Solo progress, earned-picture ownership, media database or persistent Team flight record is created. The existing small arena preference records only an explicit successful built-in destination.

Historical pack and level schemas stay unchanged. Custom-art envelopes, further theme collections, the campaign-completion ledger and complete P07/P08 qualification remain separate work.
