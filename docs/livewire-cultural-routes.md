## Cultural source boundaries

- The [Ivan Honchar Museum men's woven belt record](https://honchar.org.ua/collections/detail/2593)
  documents a long narrow format, geometric ornament and twill/slit-tapestry techniques. Read the
  lock borrows narrow-band cadence only. It does not copy the belt, ornament, weave draft, palette,
  meaning or source coordinates.
- The [museum's woven curtain towel record](https://honchar.org.ua/collections/detail/1984)
  documents geometric ornament, several weaving techniques and separated woven bands. Switchyard
  borrows separated-band organization only. It does not copy the towel, ornament, band sequence,
  palette, meaning or source coordinates.
- The [Museum Fund of Ukraine gerdan record](https://museum.mincult.gov.ua/collections/250333)
  documents a narrow woven-bead construction and repeated geometric composition. Split junction
  borrows paired branching cadence only. It does not copy the necklace, bead pattern, palette,
  meaning or source coordinates.

These records support cultural context and broad compositional vocabulary. They do not establish
gameplay balance, permission to copy an object or universal symbolic meanings.
