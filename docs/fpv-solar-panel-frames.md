# Solar panel face assemblies

This bounded D2 increment corrects the existing panel banks' all-face photovoltaic wrapping. Upper faces show a coherent 10×3 framed module field with fine cell divisions; sides and backs use a quiet opaque finish. It preserves all sixteen 11×0.3×7 m closed boxes, their 15° tilt, original vertices/normals/indices/transforms, supports, collision, sightlines, courses and retained recordings. It adds no geometry, triangles, draw batches, textures, materials or other GPU owners. This is a surface assembly improvement, not whole-world artistic completion.

Only authored Operations with linear filtering and the complete sixteen unique `solar-panel-[0-3]-[0-3]` canonical boxes receives the atlas. Exact dimensions, finite coordinates, undefined shape type and the original rotation quaternion are required. A resized, renamed, typed, extra, duplicate or differently rotated panel makes the complete scene use its original finish; this also avoids allocating a second map trio in mixed custom scenes. Same-size translated panels remain supported. Pixel, all shared material collections, other authored themes and other worlds remain unchanged.

The existing albedo/normal/roughness-AO trio is reused. The positive local-Y face maps to the module field; all other faces map to the backing strip. The material retains metalness 0.35 and uses roughness 0.8 with the atlas's glass/trim/back variation. The packed blue channel is not bound as a metalness map. Every pixel remains opaque; module divisions are paint and shading, not new flight gaps.
