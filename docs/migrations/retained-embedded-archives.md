# Retained embedded historical records

The archives listed in `retained-embedded-archives.json` and `retained-additional-embedded-archives.json` are derived records after a content ownership migration. Their historical filenames may contain “originals,” but their current bytes are not the original artifacts. Exact original archives are preserved separately, and both original and derived hashes are recorded in the receipts.

Each changed archive contains a scope notice. Historical review claims, source revisions, approvals, and measurements inside retained excerpts describe the original evidence only; they do not qualify the derived archive or its current source. These archives must not be presented as the original verified release, and original checksums must not be used for their current bytes.

The additional audit covers 21 archives, 354 projected embedded records, and 16,505 byte-identical retained records. It checks bounded recursive contents, every changed record receipt, and the known exclusive media hashes. No archive members were extracted or executed during projection.
