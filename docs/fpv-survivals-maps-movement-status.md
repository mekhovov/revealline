The remaining work is primarily qualification and review. Any concrete defect it reveals still requires a scoped implementation fix. The delivered V2 layouts do not need another terrain implementation phase. Broader reuse should build on existing native animation adapters: Solo/Team already sample warning/recovery poses, Snake shares humanoid states and modular structures, and SIM uses native 3D phases. An actual-fired-event pose is a possible later improvement, not a newly invented mandatory release task. Solid scenery may be reused only where it matches host-owned collision or lies outside playable space.

**Skipped by the user, not passed:** Iris Xe Windows, M1 Safari, physical controller/phone and five-player trials. These are not included in the remaining local work or claimed from M4 Pro results.

**Human review judgments:** visual appeal, perceived fairness, audible quality, ordinary-player difficulty, replay interest and medal calibration. Automation can expose defects and demonstrate possible routes; it cannot certify these judgments. The skipped five-player study does not turn them into measured successes.

Research coverage is retained in the dated plan: Crimsonland, Vampire Survivors Moonspell/Adventures, Nova Drift dynamic waves and entrance readability, Dead Cells authored structure, DRG Survivor, NIMRODS and Valve locomotion/behavior separation. Those sources support specific design choices; they are not proof of our balance or of another game's undisclosed steering algorithm. No new research claim was added in this completion audit.
