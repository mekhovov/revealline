# Cultural timed opportunity pressure · v33

Status: **prepared candidate; not merged, released or publicly accepted**.

This bounded successor extends the already-shipped timed-bonus contract from the
early Border missions into three later spatial problems. It does not change
movement physics, enemy steering, objectives, pictures, route order or previous
editions.

| Mission | Existing pressure | Optional timed decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Two ways home | finite pursuer commitment and two offset braid returns | detour toward a relocating enemy-slow window or keep the safer closure |
| Read the lock | telegraphed row lock and central/lower return choice | spend a lock cycle reaching a relocating freeze window or bank the return |
| Returning light | lane emitter, roamer activation and stepped returns | replace the fixed slow pickup with a warned, expiring three-anchor window |

Every schedule announces before appearing, expires, relocates after cooldown,
has three maximum appearances and may be collected once. Collection is never an
objective or completion dependency. `whole-spatial-v32` remains immutable and
selectable; the candidate is registered separately as `whole-spatial-v33`.

## Focused evidence

- Source compiler accepts every anchor and all Gentle/Standard/Expert Solo and
  Versus preparations.
- Historical actors, goals, objectives, rules, line impacts, maps, assets and
  mission order remain exact.
- A missed Returning light window expires and reappears at a different anchor;
  its deterministic replay verifies.
- All three Standard openings reach their first announce/appearance without
  unavoidable damage while stationary.
- Paired Standard boards keep separate timed state and identical authoritative
  histories for identical inputs.
- Synchronous and selective asynchronous route loading agree, the edition hash
  is pinned, and browser bootstrap/navigation recognize the new route.

The repository-wide suite remains waived by policy and was not run. The sparse
local checkout cannot perform the complete localization validation because it
does not contain the 483 MiB optional authoring library. Hosted exact-head
validation and a built/public player check remain release gates. Human balance
review is still required before the candidate can be called fun or final.
