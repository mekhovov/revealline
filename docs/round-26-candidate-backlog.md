# Round 26 candidate — learn the capture loop by playing

**Recommendation: add one optional, repeatable First Flight course with three short, guided practice lessons.** It should teach closing a vulnerable line, choosing an enemy-free region and continuing after a failed cut through the actual game. This is a player-facing learning feature, not another settings option or a claim that the full requested game is finished.

Research and read-only source audit: 12 September 2026, maintained source `8eef7e2ada51dbd6ebec0fe134aeef667d047c6c` during v0.15.0 release preservation. This document proposes work only. The parent's release and browser records establish that version's evidence separately; no new gameplay, hardware or retention observation was performed for this proposal.

## Primary-source basis

Microsoft's [XAG 109 — Objective clarity](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/109) recommends available-on-demand mechanics tutorials using interactive play or demonstrated gameplay, and distinguishes them from static control screens. It also supports reviewable objectives. This directly supports a repeatable course; it does not show that our proposed lessons improve retention or establish accessibility conformance.

[XAG 116 — Time limits](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/116) addresses reading and UI interaction deadlines, with an explicit distinction from core gameplay timers. Keep lesson instructions reviewable without a countdown. Choosing training maps without mission/cut deadlines is a separate teaching decision; the guideline is not a reason to remove existing challenge clocks.

The publisher's [XPOSED Reloaded listing](https://store.playstation.com/en-us/concept/10002881) emphasizes revealing distinctive backgrounds, increasing skill and opening further packs. That supports retaining a real picture reveal at the end of the course and a clear route into the existing game. It does not document Reloaded's tutorial internals or prove that its store ratings measure this game's likely appeal.

## One bounded course

Offer **Learn by playing** beside the first ready screen and inside How to play. Existing Start remains immediately available; the course never becomes a prerequisite. Every lesson can be selected again, retried, skipped explicitly or left. Use the existing FPV presentation, animated craft, readable terrain and sound initially; no new art-production dependency is needed.

| Lesson                     | Player action                                                                                                            | What the game demonstrates                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Close the line**         | Leave the marked safe edge and return to safe ground on a simple board with one distant field enemy.                     | The player opens a real live cut, closes it and sees actual claimed territory. Mark start/return areas with a shape and short label, not color alone.                                                                                     |
| **Choose the empty side**  | Make a suggested separator between two field enemies, then make a second cut from the secured line into an empty region. | The first legal closure can secure only a line; that line is useful safe ground for the next route. The second closure demonstrates the larger fill. Use a deliberately legible, proven layout, not the existing small-capture heuristic. |
| **Bring the picture home** | Complete a small coverage/objective mission with a slowly moving threat and reduced guidance.                            | Transfer the lesson into ordinary play and a real full-picture finale. If a cut fails, show that the unfinished line disappears while previous captures remain. Deliberately losing a life is never required to complete the course.      |

Use a fixed known class whose ability is optional and no new weapon behavior. Teach directions/Stop and current remapped prompts first; equipment lessons, bosses, a full assist system, adaptive difficulty and ghost racing stay outside this increment. The seven existing classes and deeper Fieldcraft/Homeward goals remain the next things to explore after the basics.

Show one short current task and a reviewable list of completed lesson steps. Advance step feedback from actual simulation outcomes. Do not pause unexpectedly in the middle of a live cut, inject movement, teleport actors, claim cells or replay an invisible solution on the player's behalf. Retain explicit Pause/Resume. Between lessons, Next lesson requires a fresh action; reading, Done, held Confirm and an ended attempt cannot auto-start the next flight. A skipped lesson stays distinguishable from a completed one.

Course completion shows the actual final practice picture and an explicit **Return to the game** action. It awards no campaign clears, gallery records, scores, seals or appearances. Existing collected progress and any suspended real attempt stay intact. The player can then start or resume the ordinary game deliberately.

## Small implementation boundary to refine before coding

- Add a finite built-in lesson registry and pure observation module, separate from core rules and imported packs. Three validated ordinary level-v1 scenarios and bounded lesson metadata are sufficient; no arbitrary author script, new predicate language or simulation version is required.
- Resolve a trusted course entry into an immutable practice context **before** [the app](../game/app.mjs) acquires its profile writer. Reuse the current game renderer, input adapters, pause/recovery logic and replay recorder. A dedicated read-only lesson view can derive small owned facts after fixed steps; it must not mutate the run or use wall time to infer success.
- Define closure evidence carefully: `cut.closed.cells` is total newly secured cells, not the live-line length. `cells.claimed.indices`, controlled map markers and before/after ownership can demonstrate the authored separator. Do not copy `eventFeedback`'s `< 50` explanatory heuristic as a lesson-completion rule. Account for cells entered on the closure tick and prove alternate/off-route inputs cannot produce a false “both sides contain enemies” explanation.
- Keep the course's finite observer state in memory. Reload can return to a clearly labeled lesson start; no library, save, replay, mastery or pack format change is needed. A replay still describes normal inputs on an exact lesson map, not an award certificate.
- Scope selection and lesson navigation cannot turn practice into an awarding run. The existing [practice navigation guard](../game/ui/practice-navigation.mjs) blocks ordinary links, so design the explicit return route deliberately; do not remove that guard globally. Returning mid-course must pause/release inputs, preserve the real save slot and enter a fresh normal page without starting or replacing a saved attempt.
- Reuse current controller readers, configured menu actions, input-neutral boundaries and the new [flight-control layout](flight-controls.md). Prompts must follow actual bindings. The production artifact/offline inventory must include the lessons and their local links; do not require an external tutorial video or authoring server.

Expected scope is a small lesson registry/observer/view plus explicit app/Help entry and exit, three training scenarios and new focused proofs. Review the exact host lifecycle contract before implementation. No change to the existing 29 map identities, historic progress, real-game difficulty or frozen release files is justified by this feature.

## Acceptance that can be established locally

1. Prove every lesson with normal recorded inputs in **both steering modes**. Assert actual capture sets, the separator-only outcome followed by a larger fill, required-objective completion and final replay equality. Add a legal failure/recovery route, off-route controls and skip/retry cases; text snapshots alone are insufficient.
2. Exercise the visible course entry, all lessons, repeat/skip/exit and return to a real mission through the browser. Retained real scores, pictures, preferences and suspended attempt must compare unchanged after course play, including its wins and losses. No practice-to-campaign selection escape may award progress.
3. Use keyboard, visible direction buttons in Hold/Tap and remapped Controller Lab input. Test a held Next/Confirm boundary, reader exit, Stop/Boost, blur, disconnect and recovery with no unintended movement or automatic restart. Existing command/checkpoint behavior must remain unchanged when the lesson observer is absent or reading the same run.
4. Check 320×640, 390×844, 844×390, 844×501 and 1280×720 actual game viewports with normal and enlarged instruction content. The board, task and actions must remain reachable; allow honest vertical scrolling. Observe pointer/media capabilities rather than inferring physical touch from size.
5. Verify the exact saved browser artifact offline, with no missing lesson files, and retain earlier release bytes. The completion record may say the guided course works under these tested conditions. Comprehension, comfort, human enjoyment and voluntary replay remain **unmeasured** until corresponding observations exist; they are not blockers requiring the user to answer another question.

This is the highest-value bounded next step because it connects existing depth to a player's first successful decisions. Authored cultural/retro/business picture chapters remain substantial unfinished work afterward. Physical/native/host/network work has separate evidence and service requirements; it should not be renamed complete or used to delay this locally achievable learning feature.
