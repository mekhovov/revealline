# Soundtrack core-style review batches — 29 September 2026

These shortlists use public canonical-archive recordings only. They do not approve
the music or add it to a standard game playlist. Complete-track listening decides
which recordings advance; rejected recordings remain preserved and can move to
review-only without deleting their bytes.

Score every recording from 1–5 for hook, rhythmic coordination, sustained gameplay
energy, arrangement development and replay value. Record **accept**, **backup** or
**review-only**. An accepted batch may be smaller than six.
