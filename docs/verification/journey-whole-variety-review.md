## What changes

The71 core missions and12 stand-alone Remixes from the timed-bonus v3 edition
retain their exact mission, map and picture definitions. Eight existing original-
picture studies now have direct host access in two optional four-mission arcs:

| Optional sequence           | Missions                                                                     | Authored bands |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------- |
| Ukrainian-inspired ornament | Cross-stitch crossings → Rushnyk bands → Pysanka sections → Dnipro crossings | 4,4,5,5        |
| FPV workshop                | Four motor landings → Circuit lanes → Twin lens chambers → Toolbench weave   | 4,4,5,5        |

These are integrations of existing candidates, not eight newly generated maps
or pictures. Dnipro and Four motor select their explicitly reviewed spatial-2
editions; the other six preserve original geometry. Their existing enemy roles,
coverage, picture pins, optional mastery and pressure-v2 difficulty remain exact.
No new engine, combat or Team behavior is introduced.

The original donor presentation uses Horizon/Rover base themes, while the core
uses campaign-material successors. V4 explicitly includes independently copied
original themes as well as those successors. Old routes keep their original
theme arrays; no renamed theme or silent substitution hides a missing dependency.
Whole-set visual consistency still needs human review.

The arcs are entered through the optional chooser, never automatically after the
Apex finale. Next and two-activation Skip stay inside the selected arc; its fourth
mission offers finding another mission, replay or exit. Remixes remain separate.
Navigation groups optional campaigns by source, pack and campaign, so reusing a
campaign in another pack cannot connect otherwise unrelated sequences.

The ending says **End of this optional sequence**, not that the campaign is
complete: players may jump to its fourth mission, skip earlier ones or lose the
race. Only real successful mission outcomes award completion receipts.

V4 uses profile `journey-whole-spatial-v4` and suspended-attempt key
`revealline.suspended.journey-whole-spatial.v4`. These do not contain a release
version. Other edition receipts/backups cannot satisfy this review edition;
Solo/Versus remain separate. Defaults and historical URLs are unchanged.
