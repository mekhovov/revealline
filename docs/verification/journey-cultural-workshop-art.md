## Visual observations and boundaries

All eight were visually inspected. Continuous2:1 compositions have recognizable
large subjects, separated discoveries and no text/UI/watermarks. Pixel-cluster
styling is consistent across the warm linen/wood ornament set and navy/teal/wood
workshop set; these are detailed illustrations, not strict low-resolution sprites.
The embroidery pictures have dense fabric texture; copper circuit traces and dark
goggles on dark mats need particular partial-reveal/gameplay contrast checks.

The [Ivan Honchar Museum collection](https://honchar.org.ua/collections/view/rusniki)
informs the distinction among textile objects; the [culture ministry's pysanka
account](https://mincult.gov.ua/en/news/the-ukrainian-tradition-and-art-of-egg-decoration-recognized-as-the-cultural-heritage-of-humanity/)
informs egg-decoration attribution. Both primary pages were rechecked21September.
The motifs are original and inspired, not certified regional patterns or invented
claims about each symbol. Dnipro is a geographic inspiration, not historical
reconstruction. Workshop objects are inactive decorative still lifes, not working
circuitry, assembly instructions or weapons. Music remains explicitly paused.
