## Corrections and boundaries

- **Observer connection recovery:** native unexpected-close notifications retire the cached handle. A closing connection can also race acquisition without delivering that event; one retry is permitted only when transaction creation throws `InvalidStateError`. Started, aborted, committed, quota-failed or timed-out transactions are never replayed. Disposal permanently prevents reconnect. This addresses the reproduced stale-handle failure, not a claim to know why the earlier browser closed its database.
- **Finite observation ownership:** child navigation immediately finalizes an invalid report. Leaving the demo also ends its observation, rather than counting time in a different game/session. Neither action is blocked and no new observation or game session is started automatically. Passive capture retains bounded button IDs, navigation-key identifiers, trusted-event flags and pathnames; typed text, field values and URL queries are excluded. This provides evidence for a future exit; the earlier Couch navigation's cause remains unknown.
- **Useful preparation search:** a fully simulated win is accepted at its actual terminal tick. Opening/recovery decisions may stop after at least eight evaluated candidates when three useful safe alternatives and a bent route already exist. Difficult searches and hazard-overreach decisions retain their allowance. Seeded variety, sustained real-hazard losses and exact independent reconstruction remain required; no direction reversal or artificial death is introduced.
- **Worker retirement across scenes:** asynchronous termination acknowledgements are tracked before a later live source creates its thread, including late/cancelled preparations. The adapter and host retain their disposal acknowledgement. Browser Workers still use their native void termination; no service or extra Worker is added. A failed acknowledgement prevents silently creating another thread. The one-second planning watchdog, 15-second loading deadline and 16 / 1,200 / 12,000 simulation limits remain unchanged.

No physics, progression, rewards, picture permissions, replay format, community identity, automatic unmute or release policy changes are included.
