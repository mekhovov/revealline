# Sentinel receiver spatial successor

Two explicit geometry/placement successors for Twin Receivers and Relay Perimeter.
They use pressure-v2 and the unchanged sole-anchor shield-relay encounter, controls,
quota, warning/release windows and legal isolation rule. No new engine, Team support,
automatic enrollment, final artwork, human-balance or deployment claim.

## Changed decisions

Twin replaces convenient near-core returns with a remote start, upper/lower
approach landings and open receiver-gallery baffles. Walls never close cuts.
Players can establish an approach before choosing which receiver to enclose.
Relay uses staggered platforms and asymmetric three-receiver approaches. Capturing
the western shield opens a permanent non-scoring transfer corner; taking the
shortcut is optional, and its mastery route actually traverses both segments
before the final shield. Frontier and reclaimed-ground threats retain their
existing roles and tiers; placement follows the new valid topology.

Studio has a separate **Inspect Sentinel receiver greybox successor** action.
Original pictured candidates remain available. Inspection does not change the
active draft until Apply. This uses the same compiler, inspection and engine
preview, not a parallel map-management path.

## Remaining acceptance

- Twin's21.35second optimized route and sub45second Relay routes remain human
  pacing concerns; do not equate a longer fixture with greater enjoyment.
- Opposite preset timing order does not mean harder enemies are easier for people:
  routes differ and enemy movement can open different capture windows. Test real
  failure causes, decisions and retry intent, not clear duration alone.
- Broader seeds/delays, physical controls, accessibility, native full clears and
  genuine human comparison with old editions remain required.
- Original artwork and whole-host successor adoption follow greybox acceptance.
  AI soundtrack generation remains paused.
- Reviewed integration, allocated immutable release and exact GitHub Pages proof
  remain release-owner gates. The public/frozen release has not been changed.
