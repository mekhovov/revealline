The test-only observer distinguishes physical visits, mutual foundation linkage,
perimeter linkage and the initial retained regions traversed by completed cuts.
Capturing around a landing must not be reported as visiting it. With one island,
mutual linkage is vacuously true; perimeter linkage is recorded separately. These
are observations, **not a new optional-mastery evaluator or game award system**.

Allowing a risky broad enclosure instead of an island route is an intended choice,
so bypassing a landing alone is not a defect. Review whether the advertised lesson
is discoverable, the optional route is useful, and the large-cut risk pays for its
speed. Do not add compulsory visits or quota cleanup just to invalidate efficient
play. The especially short courtyard/outpost/second-landing routes and the Remix's
unused upper platform are spatial review priorities, alongside earlier late-game
shortcuts. Increasing speed alone does not address these geometry questions.

Human pacing, optional mastery, native/controller/touch play, campaign-boundary
tension, integrated host qualification, reviewed promotion and Pages remain open.
