# Home Signal: ordinary-field finale alternative

2026-09-21. P12 design study, **balance pending**, not a released or accepted
finale. Eight public-input no-loss clears replay and finish equal paired-board
races. They establish feasibility, not human enjoyment or increasing difficulty.

## Why a different encounter

The prior [contested-return study](journey-apex-home-spatial.md) still has
15.05–21.75-second optimized boss clears. Adding keepers to that boss is not a
valid small balance edit: the Sentinel contract requires its sole field seed.
This alternative instead removes the encounter explicitly (`encounter: null`)
and uses the established ordinary enemy-seeded capture rule. No engine, capture,
collision, physics, schema or bonus contract changes.

`createApexFieldCandidates()` creates `home-field-2` with a distinct project and
mission revision. Historical boss editions and the other four Apex runtimes stay
exact. All map geometry, foundations, 1921-cell earned denominator, 24 reserved
dock cells, 85% quota, optional freeze, original asset pins and pressure-v2 presets
remain unchanged. The unchanged map retains its existing revision.

Two measured keepers occupy the upper western approach and the eastern field.
The existing frontier crawler and ground rover remain: three familiar threat
roles, not a new mandatory rule. Four visible ordinary objectives replace the
shield/core set; the north objective sits in the western keeper's corridor.
The near dock relay still opens the permanent non-scoring connector. Capturing
all four and meeting coverage wins; there is no core opening or full-board release.
Optional mastery now explicitly requires visiting a connected western wing and
a **far** eastern platform, not merely the inner relay landing.

## Verification and review

The 21 new focused checks and combined 154-test cohort pass on Node 20.19.5 and
22.22.2. The cohort includes historical Apex/spatial routes, capture components,
relay semantics, project/Studio/preview/persistence and mounted paused details.
ESLint, formatting and diff whitespace checks pass. Sparse-checkout content tests
use the existing read-only exact `daaef1fa` fallback, never a replacement for a
present working file. No downloaded asset or generated source is silently edited.

Independent review reproduced all eight routes and equal races, then separately
ran the 21 focused tests on both Node versions. Its hardening recommendations
are applied: pin exact actor/objective/route inventories; require every recorded
wait to start stopped on reclaimed ground with no live trail; describe dock use
without claiming necessity or a full crossing. Passing negative-route assertions
are regression tests, not six additional successful clears.

## Remaining acceptance gates

Do not promote this study merely because its first routes took longer. Resolve
the 28.75-second shortcut's actual challenge, long idle windows, Gentle's quota
tail, frontier relevance, late-roamer bypasses, optional mastery across presets,
adapted starts/seeds and dock usefulness. Preserve satisfying large enclosures;
do not impose waits or smaller captures only to inflate duration. Compare this
alternative with the boss study in human sessions before choosing the finale.
Original-art fit, native full play, touch/controllers, human failure prediction,
voluntary retry, host enrollment and reviewed versioned release/Pages remain open.

Rechecked 2026-09-21: [AirXonix's developer rules](https://www.axysoft.com/airxonix/)
separate field enemies from threats on filled ground. [Xposed Switched's publisher
description](https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/products/xposed-switched-switch/)
describes a compact variety of terrain, enemies and powerups. The design inference
is to combine understandable domains and change return-route decisions, not add
unexplained speed or infer undocumented Reloaded timing. Neither source proves
that this alternative is fun or guarantees engagement.
