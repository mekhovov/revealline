# Remaining catalogue editorial review

This completes the **184 missions** not covered by the earlier
[56-mission pacing review](editorial-pacing-review.md). Together the two documents
cover the current 240 official missions exactly once. This is an authored review
of accepted geometry, target policies, objective order and adjacent lessons. It
is not a player study, and “Keep” means a defensible present purpose, not a claim
that a mission is proven enjoyable. The catalogue trace review supplies repeatable
witnesses; its quiet-interval flags exclude ordinary movement and cannot measure
boredom on their own.

Four introductory Field recipes change from six catches to three. Each previously
repeated one unchanged policy with no supplies, staged objectives, acceleration or
second enemy family. Three catches preserve a first read, correction and longer
cable confirmation before the following eight-catch mixed-policy mastery mission.
This follows the existing three-catch Oval Intercept rhythm. The change deliberately
reduces endurance in these introductions; it does not claim that three is a
research-established optimum. No new mechanics or geometry are introduced.

All other quotas remain: longer foundations exercise actual growing-cable routing;
supply missions need enough catches to offer their later supplies; operation
quotas are tied to ordered deliveries and exits. A lower witness length alone is
not a reason to shorten any of those missions.

Each row below records the decision, concrete recipe evidence, the pressure or
choice that earns the mission, and the open route/recovery that prevents that
pressure becoming compulsory waiting. IDs are exact, including legacy titles.
The changed missions receive new recipe identities and six new Solo/Team/pace
journals each in `editorial-proofs.json`; predecessors remain explicit archives.
No other mode's progress or creator content is changed.

## Bounded pacing changes and verification

The old and revised reference pilots use level seed 17. At each supported pace,
these move counts are identical within each mode; timing still follows the
selected pace. They are winning witnesses, not optimal routes or enjoyment scores.

| Contour Orbit | 66 → 36             | 43 → 17             | Contour Transfer: eight inner/outer catches         |
| Wake Watch    | 83 → 52             | 63 → 36             | Wake Courtyard: eight rover/perimeter catches       |

All 24 new journals reproduce the accepted revised recipes in Solo and Team at
normal, slow and fast pace. No target speed, random stream, wall, warning or
movement rule changed. Old six-catch journals still use their archived recipes;
fresh official records use the revised recipe. The shorter final cable is an
intentional introduction trade-off, while the longer mixed mastery stays intact.

## Follow-up observations and limits

The four shortened introductions are an editorial decision, not measured player
preference. Longer foundation quotas intentionally remain because the accepted
mechanic there is increasing cable occupancy; the 14-catch wrapping boards
already interrupt the 18/19/20-catch final endurance sequence. Legacy internal
IDs such as `last-shedding-bank` and `garden-crossfire` are not evidence that those
v1 missions have Reel or projectiles. Their displayed titles and descriptions
must accurately communicate their present routing task.

Several operation layouts intentionally reuse a taught junction or divider.
They earn their later place through different staged destinations, cargo timing,
source coverage or enemy commitment. Reuse alone is not a defect, but a future
player study should check whether those distinctions are noticed without reading
these notes. The study remains optional as requested; no universal enjoyment,
optimal tuning or higher-star calibration is asserted here.
