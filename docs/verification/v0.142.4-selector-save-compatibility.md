# Selector star results and historical save compatibility

Reviewed 29 September 2026 on the v0.142.4 selector candidate. PR #776 originally
added `bestStars` to completion receipts without changing the v1 record format.
The published v0.142.3 reader rejected those records on both read and write.

## Storage contract

- Keep database `revealline-journey-v1`, IndexedDB version 1, and
  `revealline-journey-profile.v1` unchanged. Completion receipts still contain
  exactly `runId`, `gameplayId`, and `difficulty`.
- Store optional grades in `revealline-journey-stars.v1` at
  `${profileKey}:stars.v1` in the existing `profiles` store. Profile, picture and
  star writes share one transaction. A failed companion write rolls back every
  write, while session progress remains exportable and retryable.
- Merge the highest verified grade per mission and mode. Older tabs can read and
  update their known profile without replacing the separate grades. A missing
  completion hides and prunes orphan grades when the current reader next loads
  the profile. Star data grants no score, reward or replay authority.
- Validate finite star records with a 2 MiB/30,000-node budget and exact fields.
  Own-property lookups preserve legal mission IDs such as `toString` and
  `valueOf` without inheriting Object values.

## Backup contract

Exports without grades retain their existing v1, scoped v2, or picture v3
envelope. Exports containing grades use explicit
`revealline-journey-backup.v4`, carrying the unchanged v1 profile, its scoped
picture ledger, and the star sidecar. The envelope keeps the 16 MiB byte budget
and allows 230,020 nodes to include the bounded sidecar. Grade entries require a
completion in that same profile. Wrong-edition, malformed and orphan imports
fail before mutation.

Current readers accept earlier backup formats without removing existing grades.
Previous readers reject v4 cleanly; they are not expected to understand new
backup metadata. Live shared v1 saves remain usable by those previous readers.
