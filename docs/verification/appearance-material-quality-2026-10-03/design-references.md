# Material quality direction — 2026-10-03

This is design research and a proposed art direction, not a rendering acceptance report. It reviews the 15 normal-theme captures and four high-contrast captures in `/tmp/appearance-material-depth-final/`, alongside the material CSS at `fa905ef13`. The document was added after the integration worktree advanced to `658e38623`. Those JPEG captures help compare overall hierarchy; they cannot establish exact native-pixel sharpness or numeric contrast. Recommendations below are original interpretations. No reference textures, icons, symbols, or game artwork are copied.

The common weakness is material construction, not insufficient decoration. Most themes still present similarly outlined rectangles with a different gradient or fine repeat. Industrial has the strongest depth, but its many small dashes look like generalized wear. The quieter themes often lose their identity once the palette is removed. Several scene motifs are mostly covered by the specimen's first content row. Increasing their opacity would not solve the placement problem.

## Shared construction rules

1. **Establish three different physical roles.** A panel is a substantial backing with a rim or seam; a button is a smaller raised or seated part; an input is a recessed, quiet reading well. Preserve these differences in primary, selected, destructive, and pressed states. Recoloring a state must not erase its material, and decoration must not replace semantic focus or error cues.
2. **Build material at three scales.** Use broad, low-contrast body variation; medium features that identify the material, such as a folded edge, stepped chip, weave crossing, or interrupted fiber; and a sparse fine finish. As an initial authoring target, use roughly 24–96 px body structure, 4–16 px edge features, and 1–2 px detail at the intended UI scale. These are starting values for visual review, not measured requirements. Long reading areas should have the lowest detail contrast.
3. **Draw pixel clusters instead of distributing isolated specks.** Deterministic SVG can encode original integer-aligned rectangles and stepped contours just as an atlas can. Give adjacent marks a material relationship: a dark pit with one lit lip, a scratch that terminates at a seam, or a thread alternating over and under another. Avoid blanket turbulence filters, regular micro-dash fields, and one generic noise layer tinted for every material.
4. **Keep structural edges sharp.** Separate corner/edge artwork from the repeating center. Do not stretch rivets, stitch crossings, chips, or pixel patterns across arbitrary widths. Review at actual 1× and 2× presentation, narrow layouts, and enlarged text; inspect lossless captures at native size. Browser-scaled JPEG contact sheets are insufficient. Keep text rendering independently readable.
5. **Give ornament a real place.** Reserve a header band, corner pocket, or outer rail with layout clearance. Use one coherent motif at that level, rather than attaching a loud symbol to every control. Large text and narrow layouts may simplify it. Off and high contrast must still remove decorative material reliably.
6. **Use a consistent lighting direction per family.** Derive three to five deliberate light/body/shadow tones from semantic colors. A pressed control changes edge depth; hover strengthens a rim, hem, or status strip. Neither needs a moving shine or animated grain. Keep the material phase stable while scrolling and resizing.

Factorio's developers use a common widget specimen and tileset to evaluate contrast, type, scaling, and the whole GUI together. That is a useful production model for this work, rather than treating individual buttons as isolated art. [Factorio GUI tileset](https://factorio.com/blog/post/fff-243). Nine-slice rendering can retain corners and tile the edges/center without stretching the motif. [Godot's nine-slice contract](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.5/classes/class_ninepatchrect.html).

The cultural treatments should remain specific and restrained. A Ukrainian museum's documented porcelain dish uses a blue rim and a thin gray lower rule; its embroidered blouse places botanical work on the front and sleeves. These are useful examples of decoration organized around an object's structure, not evidence that every Ukrainian object shares one pattern. [Porcelain dish](https://museum.mincult.gov.ua/collections/blyudo-ovalne), [embroidered blouse](https://museum.mincult.gov.ua/collections/bluza-zhinocha-vishita-88685). The Met's Nabeshima dish distinguishes pigment below glaze from enamel above it, a useful material distinction for an original Sakura treatment. [Cherry-blossom porcelain](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/52281).

## Recommended order of work

1. **First prove a material construction on three different families:** Industrial's steel/rubber, Vyshyvanka's cloth/stitches, and Dnipro's glaze/rim. Review an actual settings page, a compact dialog, a primary action, a native input, and Studio at normal and enlarged text sizes. These test different optical behaviors and expose whether the framework merely recolors a generic finish.
2. **Extend the construction to every textured family with its own recipe.** The table provides the material-specific features. Check active surfaces as well as default ones; current bright controls reveal repetition and uniformity more readily than dark panels. Preserve current texture-free families and accessibility overrides.
3. **Review native pixels and live behavior.** Capture lossless 1×/2× views, scrolling, focus, pressed and disabled combinations, narrow layouts, and content with long labels. Recalculate text/control contrast over the final painted regions. Verify material identity remains distinct with ornament disabled, and ornament does not collide with content when enabled.
4. **Qualify cost and provenance.** Keep generated texture sources deterministic, assets original, old pinned definitions unchanged, and payload growth within current limits. SVG containing thousands of marks is not automatically cheaper than a compact bitmap; compare actual output size and runtime paint cost. Any renderer or SIM lighting extension needs separate measurement and acceptance.

The reviewed high-contrast captures visibly suppress decorative material, which is the intended direction. This is an observation about those four captured specimens, not a whole-game accessibility certification or a measured performance result.
