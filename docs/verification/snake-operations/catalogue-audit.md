# Original Snake catalogue: 144-mission review

Reviewed all original missions for the route decision, repeated stationary encounters, target variety, quota, supplies and teaching order. This is a content review; automated wins do not establish player enjoyment.

**96 kept; 48 revised.** Keep decisions below are intentional: quiet cable-routing levels, obstacle-free Open Loop and recent finite field encounters still serve different lessons. The 36 pursuit and 12 living-route missions had long repeated quotas; they now end after the lesson has developed, with reverse-return patrols and mixed sprint encounters where appropriate. Geometry, IDs, supply availability and chapter order remain intact.

All 48 previous recipes are retained verbatim in the official archive. New records use revision 2 and completion stars until calibrated; older verified grades and recordings remain attached to their original recipe.
