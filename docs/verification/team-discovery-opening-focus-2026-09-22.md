Opening **Browse Team arenas** after choosing Plain styling and Large display text could focus **Play First Connection** while its lower half remained outside the modal. At the observed 1280×720 viewport, the action began at y683.906 with height 47; the panel ended at y708.

Two paths caused clipping. Native `showModal()` could already focus the selected action, bypassing the code that assigned focus with `preventScroll`. Separately, artwork teasers began hidden and added their canvas height after asynchronous preparation.

The opening handoff now checks the full selected action against the dialog client scrollport and viewport even when native focus is already correct. It scrolls only a clipped action, using nearest instant scrolling, and confirms the same visit still owns visible foreground focus after geometry reads. A scoped 2:1 thumbnail frame reserves the eventual canvas area from initial population. The canvas remains hidden until actual pixels are painted; no synthetic picture is displayed. This avoids delayed scrolling after the player moves elsewhere.
