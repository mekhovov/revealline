## Accounting and uncertainty

The unchanged raw application metric spans PRE_STEP through POST_RENDER. The meter separately retains Float64 endpoints of invoked journal hooks. Paused export intersects the intervals, counts work inside the application span once, and assigns outside hook pieces to the next same-phase work window. Active terminal tails are added to the final active sample and remain in aggregate/maxima; initial, non-active and mixed-phase tails remain explicitly reported. Any observed hook, tail or active envelope of at least 100 ms prevents a scoped pass and requires investigation. Non-active/mixed tails are outside the active-percentile comparison, not silently credited to gameplay.

The existing fixed numeric capacities remain 16,384 application windows and 65,536 hooks (3,145,728 active-buffer bytes). Overflow, open/nested spans, invalid clock/origin, extra documents, failed raw budgets or replay, and missing positive active coverage invalidate an arm. The matched lane requires at least 600 stable active windows spanning 29 seconds. This meter is restricted to the declared short workload; it does not claim 40-minute coverage.

Export also retains the number of clock endpoints contributing to each sample and tail. Quantized-zero hooks retain an allowance: a measured zero is not proof of zero work. The maximum contributing count times the independently observed same-browser clock quantum bounds each arm's p95 sensitivity under the stated quantization assumption. Integer/count bounds and interval/tail conservation are verified again after the owned child closes.

Analysis uses all ten paired log p95 ratios. A two-sided 95% Student-t interval (9 degrees of freedom, rounded-up multiplier 2.263) is expanded by the worst endpoint sensitivity, including its possible effect on the sample deviation. Upper bound ≤ 5% passes the scoped envelope; lower bound > 5% fails it; crossing 5%, nonpositive uncertainty-adjusted denominators or missing pairs remain unresolved. Frames are not treated as independent experimental repetitions. There is no trimming, successful-subset pooling or optional stopping.

The [High Resolution Time specification](https://www.w3.org/TR/hr-time-3/#coarsen-time) permits timer coarsening and jitter. The measured quantum is a sensitivity assumption, not a guaranteed bound on privacy jitter. [NIST's paired-observation guidance](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section3/prc311.htm) supports the matched analysis; the endpoint allowance is an additional conservative engineering check.
