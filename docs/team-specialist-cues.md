# Team specialist cues

Status: implemented candidate; public-device and human readability review pending.

The specialist successor now carries role identity through both the HUD and the in-board Support pulse:

- Interceptor status uses the visible word “Interceptor,” an `I` marker and a long-dash inner ring.
- Disruptor status uses the visible word “Disruptor,” a `D` marker and a short-dash inner ring.
- Text and geometry remain present when reduced effects are enabled; colour is supplementary.
- Hybrid historical Support keeps its existing appearance.
- The simulation records the role on the cosmetic pulse event, but role art never changes radius, cooldown or collision.

This is a presentation child of the versioned specialist runtime. It does not promote the candidate to the default Team route or claim human validation.
