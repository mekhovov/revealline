## Completed Legacy campaigns

An earned won result for a fully completed Legacy, non-practice campaign names campaign completion on that same result, alongside the final coverage, score, time, picture and existing save warning. Its primary action is **Browse campaigns**, which opens the existing Missions chooser as a child of the result. Back or cancel returns to the same won result and Browse campaigns focus. Retry and View picture remain available; browsing does not prepare a new attempt, release its picture, resume play or record another award. Replaying an already-cleared mission may truthfully show campaign completion without introducing a new completion award.

Cold/reloaded completed campaigns without an explicit replay bookmark retain the existing overview and Collection/replay entry points because no live final result is being retained. An explicit saved mission selection still opens its replay briefing. Incomplete Next keeps its cancellable destination preparation and direct Start. Journey ordering/content, practice and course behavior are unchanged.

## Mission selection feedback

A successful selection notice belongs to that campaign revision and mission revision. Accepting a different prepared mission retires the old notice, so returning to Missions after Next cannot describe the previous mission as ready to deploy. The selected mission and deploy controls remain the source of current availability. Pending, cancelled or failed Next keeps the accepted result and its notice; same-mission Retry does not retire it. Unrelated import, install, backup and error feedback is not cleared by this rule.
