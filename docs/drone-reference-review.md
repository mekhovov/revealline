# Drone proportions and machine reference review

28 September 2026. Supersedes the visual proportion conclusion of the first C1-A motion fixture. That fixture proved frame changes and stable paused poses; the user's review correctly identified undersized propellers. Motion-test success is not art approval.

## Implemented candidate

The opt-in `reference-v2` construction produces original native 32/64-pixel bodies for seven FPV classes and the border-patrol drone. The old default construction and compiled asset bytes remain intact. [Compare actual decoded candidates](verification/rotor-motion/proportions.html) using the shared player and actor painters.

Quad radii increase from 0.12 to 0.205 of the image frame, approximately 71%. Their motor footprint decreases from five to three pixels on the 32-pixel grid. The centre frame becomes slimmer, with distinct battery, camera, reel and equipment arrangements. Six-rotor carrier centres move to a separate layout with 0.15 radii, two blades per motor, and explicit alternating directions around the perimeter. Diagonally opposing quad motors share a spin direction. These are visual coordinates, not physical design specifications or collision geometry.

Do not enlarge all rotors with a global draw multiplier: it can overlap carrier sweeps, clip an edge or duplicate blades on an uploaded original. Adopt a reviewed body and its matching rig as a new presentation revision. Preserve manual/original overrides and retained attempts. The candidate is not a replacement for every existing enemy, a published release, or a complete C1/C3 acceptance.

### Rotor direction and motion evidence

For a conventional quad viewed **from above with the nose pointing up**, both patterns below are valid. [Betaflight's Motors Tab](https://betaflight.com/docs/wiki/app/motors-tab) defines props-in and props-out; its [reversed-direction guide](https://betaflight.com/docs/wiki/guides/current/Reversed-motor-direction) explicitly identifies the outward pattern's front-left and front-right directions. Its [prop-direction documentation](https://betaflight.com/docs/wiki/guides/current/Multirotor-Spazzing-Out#prop-direction) states that diagonally opposing corners share a direction.

| Quad pattern | Front-left       | Front-right      | Rear-left        | Rear-right       |
| ------------ | ---------------- | ---------------- | ---------------- | ---------------- |
| Props-in     | Clockwise        | Counterclockwise | Counterclockwise | Clockwise        |
| Props-out    | Counterclockwise | Clockwise        | Clockwise        | Counterclockwise |

These are frame/configuration conventions, not a national pattern. This review establishes no single rotor direction for all Ukrainian FPVs or for a named real model without model-specific evidence. A still photograph or illustration does not establish rotation direction; apparent blade angle is also separate from animation phase. The table is an authoring reference, not a claim that every source machine uses one particular pattern. Confirm the painter's angle-sign convention before translating clockwise/counterclockwise into rig `direction` values.

| Visual family                 | Appropriate original-art motion and evidence boundary                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Flat six-rotor craft          | Alternate directions around the six-position perimeter for the standard fictional carrier convention; see [ArduPilot's hexa frame diagrams](https://ardupilot.org/copter/docs/connect-escs-and-motors.html#hexa-frames). This does not establish Vampire's specific rig.                                                                                                                  |
| Coaxial craft                 | Keep stacked rotors distinct from separate arms. [PX4's coaxial example](https://docs.px4.io/main/en/airframes/airframe_reference#hexarotor-coaxial) explicitly pairs upper clockwise and lower counterclockwise rotors. Do not generalize to every frame: [ArduPilot also documents corotating frames](https://ardupilot.org/copter/docs/connect-escs-and-motors.html#octo-quad-frames). |
| Aircraft nose/rear propellers | Preserve the propeller's rotation plane and nose/pusher location; propose an edge-on or elliptical animation in top-down art rather than overhead quad disks. [PX4 distinguishes vertical hover motors from forward-facing pushers](https://docs.px4.io/v1.17/en/config/actuators#motor-geometry-standard-vtol). Exact blade count and rotation remain model-specific.                    |
| Wheeled/tracked vehicles      | Propose wheel roll or a scrolling tread pattern consistent with the depicted movement. Chassis selection requires visual evidence; no aerial rotor animation.                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |

Do not invent visible deck rotors or exposed propulsion absent a reviewed reference.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |

## Cohorts and acceptance

1. **Now: proportion correction.** Seven FPV bodies plus prepared patrol, native 32/64px, four headings, actual raster motion/held-state checks, bright/dark views, matching body/rig export. Inspect the carrier separately. Do not adopt until body detail survives Team markers and board-edge insets remain correct.
2. **Next: machinery roster.** Original fixed-wing/pusher, jet-delta, wheeled/tracked and projectile candidates using the taxonomy above. Give each one dominant silhouette and an explicit existing role or scene-only designation. A beautiful image is not a reason to invent a new attack.
3. **Then: state and mode integration.** Use the actual warning/committed/recovery and Support/downed/rescue data; pause/reduced effects retain warnings and freeze decoration. Test full boards, exact originals on Retry/replay, and input independence.
4. **Finally: community and authoring coverage.** Reference/licence records, native exports, anchors, occupied bounds, preview states, exact revisions and prompts enter Studio/Motion Lab; C2/C3/C5/C7 retain their outstanding playability, performance and whole-content gates.

The user approved the generated [roster concept sheet](../authoring/library/fpv-proportion-candidates/roster-concept-v1.png), which explores sixteen visual families, as the **visual direction** for further original artwork. This is **not approval of a native runtime asset or rotor rig**. The sheet remains concept-only: it contains baked blades, many fine colours, inconsistent nose directions and some incorrect requested blade counts. Do not crop/downsample it into production sprites or infer rig geometry from it. Reinterpret the approved style on native 32/64-pixel grids, giving larger scene props sufficient canvas to retain their defining silhouette. The deterministic 32/64-pixel exports are the playable-size candidate cohort; neither is a published default.

## Native body-detail and contrast follow-ups

The source-only [reference-v3 Scout/Carrier study](../authoring/library/fpv-body-detail-candidates/manifest.json) reconstructs the richer body treatment directly at native 32/64px. The [reference-v4 Scout contrast study](../authoring/library/fpv-body-contrast-candidates/manifest.json) composes that immutable native recipe and changes only its central battery-face colours: a coherent amber sleeve, dark straps and shaded side. It preserves every silhouette pixel, camera, antenna, frame, motor, occupied bound and reference-v2 rotor parameter. Neither study samples a photograph, generated sheet or PNG; moving blades remain procedural.

V4's two PNGs total 838 bytes. The [playable comparison](../authoring/playable-benchmark/) verifies each explicitly selected cohort's manifest, source fingerprints and exact image bytes before replacing only the second view's Scout images. V3 has three construction-source dependencies; v4 adds the contrast module as a fourth. Both retain separate source-candidate identities and produced status, without production registration or artwork approval.

The current full-board comparison shows a brighter amber face, but the central Scout silhouette remains weak at small play sizes. Further actual-size, heading, light/dark artwork and motion review is required before adoption. Measured pixel luminance and local two-painter CPU timings do not establish visual quality, whole-game performance or player preference.

## Procedural company quad follow-through

The six existing company FPV bodies now follow the shared alias-safe rotor clock
and the same generic props-in diagonal convention. The previous body-phase
multiplier could visually reverse three-blade props at degraded frame rates;
row-major direction parity also mismatched rear motors. The source correction
preserves body/motor dimensions, phase offsets and caller contact cues while
mirroring the existing blade profile. [Batch22](verification/actor-batch-22/README.md)
records painted-geometry checks and bounded native DroneAid play. This is a
renderer correction, not approval of new art or a named real drone configuration.
