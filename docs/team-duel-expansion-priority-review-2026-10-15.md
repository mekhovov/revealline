**R8 — Verify the published experience and distributable package**

**Remaining work.** First bind a review to one deployed revision. Check the expedition and Hunt entries, correct mode ownership and expected assets; follow select → launch → finish → Next → Retry. Cover ordinary chapters and each distinct dependency/mechanic family in the 24 offline groups. Test an existing cached installation updating, a clean profile installing, server-unavailable reopen and restored Studio/media backups. Verify that the previous playable content survives failed or cancelled preparation. Separately, when a downloadable release is scheduled, produce and verify its exact package, inventory and clean-install/update paths through the canonical publisher.

**Why / benefit.** The live site is now built, but a successful deployment cannot establish that player progression, cached chapters and custom assets work together on that artifact. This connects the completed source to a recoverable player experience and closes the practical creator-compatibility promise on the selected distribution.

**If deferred.** No deployment defect is established. The uncertainty is stale or incomplete offline content, broken continuation, or missing custom media in the delivered installation. People downloading the older immutable release will not receive this expansion.

**Done means.** A recorded source/build identity, a chapter/dependency coverage matrix, successful published player/creator journeys, and explicit tested/untested targets. A downloadable-release claim additionally needs the exact published artifact and clean-install/update results. Do not repeat the entire suite solely to recreate evidence already provided by the rolling build.

**Effort/dependencies.** Medium for a bounded public-browser pass; variable for packaging and defects. R8a can proceed independently. R8b follows the release owner's schedule and is lower priority if browser play is the only immediate distribution goal. The earlier local ZIP/offline receipt remains useful history, not final-source qualification.

**R9 — Actual controls, device lifecycle and assisted navigation**

**Remaining work.** Choose a small, named support matrix first. For couch play, exercise two physical controllers and mixed keyboard/controller play, joining, player ownership, disconnect/reconnect, shared pause and resume. For touch/handheld targets, check actual touch areas, orientation, safe areas and foreground/background transitions. Carry one simple and one demanding Team/Duel chapter through the relevant flows. Include the supported keyboard/assistive-navigation paths in the library, briefing, Field Details, pause, result and Studio recovery; check announcements, focus restoration and usable large text on the actual target. This does not imply that the action gameplay already supports complete nonvisual play.

**Why / benefit.** Synthetic input and resized desktop views cannot establish hardware ownership or platform lifecycle behavior. Real-target coverage prevents one player controlling the wrong seat, losing a session after a disconnect, or being unable to reach a required control. The accessibility remainder is explicit here, rather than hidden inside the completed 48-screen sample.

**If deferred.** Desktop/source evidence remains valid, but couch/mobile and assistive-use reliability remain unqualified. A single device-specific failure can make the whole expanded catalogue inaccessible to an affected player. This is a risk, not a discovered defect.

**Done means.** A named device/browser/input/assistive-technology matrix, reproducible outcomes for both modes, fixes for observed failures and clearly stated unsupported combinations. Do not pursue “every device” as an unbounded completion condition.

**Effort/dependencies.** Medium per small target matrix; requires the relevant hardware and assistive environment. Highest priority for an imminent controller/mobile promise; deferrable for a narrowly supported desktop-browser release. Input-path and orientation considerations are consistent with [Xbox Accessibility Guideline 107](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/107).

**R10 — Performance, memory and storage on chosen targets**

**Remaining work.** Measure cold launch, chapter changes, frame-time consistency during two active cuts and overlapping effects, peak decoded-media memory, downloads/cache size and recovery when storage is constrained. Begin with advanced finales and the heaviest dependency sets, on the same targets as R9. Compare against existing budgets, then address measured bottlenecks; avoid reducing artwork or raising budgets without evidence.

**Why / benefit.** The earlier 68.3 MiB offline core fit its 80 MiB budget, but compressed/download size does not establish memory use or consistent animation and input response. Target measurements show whether the new variety remains responsive and installable.

**If deferred.** Lower-end players may encounter stutter, slow switching, memory-related interruptions or failed downloads despite correct gameplay logic. No such target failure has been established by this review; the measurements are missing.

**Done means.** Reproducible measurements for the named targets, explicit acceptable thresholds, fixes or disclosed limits for failures, and a safe low-storage recovery path. Hardware-specific evidence is not replaced by desktop emulation.

**Effort/dependencies.** Medium for the initial measurement pass; optimization scope depends on findings. Pair with R9 to reuse setups. Promote above cosmetic polish for mobile/handheld or lower-end support.

**R11 — Audible cue clarity and the two-player mix**

**Remaining work.** Listen to lane/erosion warnings, support/rescue, relay openings, stage changes and finish cues, including overlapping events and both Duel boards. Cover music on/off, small speakers, headphones and mono. Check distinguishability, volume balance, repeated-event fatigue and unwanted duplicate playback. Verify relevant Sound Studio substitutions while preserving visible/text alternatives.

**Why / benefit.** Event and byte checks prove that the correct sound is bound; previous browser sessions were muted. They cannot prove that a warning is audible in time or distinguishable from another event. Good cues make dangers easier to read when attention is divided between partners or boards. Multiple sensory channels and color-independent information follow [Xbox Accessibility Guideline 103](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/103); this is design guidance, not certification.

**If deferred.** Music may mask a danger, overlapping cues may be confusing, or sound repetition may become tiring even with correct event dispatch. These are unverified possibilities, not observed audio defects.

**Done means.** A recorded listening matrix for the shipped cues, targeted adjustments where necessary and preserved visual alternatives. Actual audible evaluation is required; another muted automated run would not close it.

**Effort/dependencies.** Small-to-medium, dependent on audible access. Recommended early because the scope is bounded and the benefit spans many levels. This is technical audio qualification, separate from the waived paired enjoyment study.

**Broader strategy and progression evidence — a bounded source-only next step**

The original plan called for useful choices, recovery and partner benefit in every mission. Source briefs describe those choices, every mission has a successful route, all layouts pass exact/reflected/rotated duplicate checks, and both strategies are demonstrated for eight pilots. That does not independently establish useful alternatives or progression quality across all 168 missions.

If the priority remains source work while devices/listening/release review stay deferred, extend the R5/R2 audit to the 24 chapter finales first: different capture orders, both Team pilots' contribution, recovery after a mistake and competing Duel strategies with seat reversal. Then inspect the shortest solutions and closest layout pairs. Expand coverage where those checks expose weak decisions. Revise geometry or instructions only for a demonstrated problem; do not add forced waits to hit a duration target.

This would bring stronger evidence of variety and cooperation. Deferral leaves dominant shortcuts, superficial alternatives or uneven difficulty unexamined beyond the current sample. It is **remaining depth of qualification**, not evidence that R5's agreed pilot follow-up failed or that new missions are missing. A complete claim that every mission has two demonstrated useful strategies would require a per-mission matrix; a finale sample must stay labelled as a sample. Initial scope is medium; catalogue-wide coverage is larger. The original 2–5 minute Team and 45–150 second Duel pacing goals remain unvalidated human hypotheses while R12 is waived.

**Decision recommended**

For a browser-first delivery: R8a → R11 → targeted finale/strategy coverage, with R8b when a downloadable release is scheduled. For a couch/mobile launch: R8a → R9 and R10 on the named targets → R11 before target signoff. For continued source-only work under the current deferral: the bounded finale/strategy audit is the useful next item; keep R8–R11 deferred and R12 waived. No blanket new level count or all-platform promise is recommended.
