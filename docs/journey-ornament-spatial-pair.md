# Journey ornament spatial pair

Status: candidate successor, balance review pending. This does not replace or
reinterpret any historical edition.

## Cultural design boundary

The two maps use original rectilinear abstractions rather than copying a museum
object. “Garden refuges” borrows the symmetric trunk-and-branch composition of
the Tree of Life found on Ukrainian rushnyky. “Four quarters” borrows the radial
rhythm of the widespread eight-pointed star/rosette. The Museum of the Ukrainian
Rushnyk describes Tree-of-Life and nature/cosmos motifs in its collection, while
the Encyclopedia of Ukraine identifies the Tree of Life, rhomboids and rosettes
as recurring Ukrainian ornament vocabulary:

- https://discover.ua/en/locations/muzej-ukrainskogo-rusnika
- https://www.encyclopediaofukraine.com/display.asp?linkpath=pages%5CO%5CR%5COrnament.htm

The cultural names describe the compositional inspiration only. Walls,
foundations and terrain retain Reveal Line’s established gameplay meanings.

## Garden refuges

Distinctive decision: connect the central trunk early to gain several short
branch returns, or first circle a stepped wall branch to neutralize a side
lethal bed.

Two intended approaches:

1. **Trunk first:** make a short closure from the north perimeter to the crown,
   descend the reclaimed trunk, then take alternating short branch enclosures.
2. **Blossom first:** travel around the outside boundary, enclose a side lethal
   bed from its outer edge, then connect to the lower root and work upward.

Three field keepers retain separate pressure around the crown and side beds. A
frontier patrol pressures departures on the connected tree without introducing
a new enemy role. Capturing a lethal bed neutralizes it; walls never close a cut.

## Four quarters

Distinctive decision: connect the near point of the central eight-point-star
return network, or circle an outer stepped ray for a larger first enclosure.

Two intended approaches:

1. **Center first:** make the short north connection, reposition through the
   star, and take small alternating quadrant cuts away from the frontier patrol.
2. **Outer ray first:** move along the perimeter into one corner, circle the end
   of a broken wall ray, then connect to the horizontal star arm from the side.

Each quarter starts with its own keeper, so a large line cannot erase every
quadrant. Two slow-thread patches make opposite approaches asymmetric and are
neutralized by capture. The existing frontier role, movement policy, quota,
picture and modes are unchanged.

## Qualification boundary

Focused tests cover copy-on-write preservation, all presets and Solo/Versus
parity, spawn safety, initial retention, connected playable field, distinct
approach topology and deterministic idle seeds. They do not prove 45–150 second
human clears, enjoyment, visual authenticity, or final balance. Both missions
remain explicitly **balance review pending** until human playtesting verifies
the two approaches and willingness to retry.
