### Fresh research decisions

Microsoft's objective-clarity guidance, updated 4 March 2026, calls for reviewable goals, explicit next steps and on-demand demonstrated or interactive tutorials. A static control list does not count as a tutorial. Apply this to named campaign successors and qualify existing First Flight/practice with actual capture and relevant Team rescue/Support instruction; the improved Help reader alone cannot satisfy P16 learning acceptance. [XAG 109](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/109)

Microsoft also recommends multiple cue channels and alternatives to colour-only meaning. Apply this to numbered/shaped players, distinct warning/core/rescue cues, meaningful accessible action labels and muted/reduced-effects checks. Multiple visual shapes alone do not establish blind-player accessibility, and simulated colour filters do not replace player testing. [XAG 103](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/103)

Valve's current handheld/controller guidance requires all content reachable with the default controller configuration and glyphs matching the active input. Its compatibility page now covers both Deck and Steam Machine. Use that as a review model for the entire launch/catalogue/recovery path; a working in-flight pad is insufficient. Native Steam integration and certification remain deferred. [Steamworks compatibility guidance](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/steamhardware/compat)

Celeste's changelog records removal of a hard-coded quick-restart chord when it conflicted with rebinding, plus retained loading feedback after skipping an intro. Apply those lessons by deriving Retry prompts from bindings and keeping preparation status visible when presentation is skipped; do not borrow another game's shortcut or timer rules. [Celeste changelog](https://www.celestegame.com/changelog.html)

### Navigation and progression

Use a short title menu: primary Start/Continue with the destination shown, clear Single player / Couch Versus / Couch Team choices, Missions, Collection, Settings and Workshop. Keep quick Sound available on title, lobbies, HUD and pause. Future modes receive no nonfunctional menu actions.

Present content as edition → campaign → ordered mission cards. Keep the current campaign and selected mission visible, with fast previous/next campaign navigation. The same gallery exposes installed and downloadable content, compatibility, size, progress and a single Play or Download & play action. Use six cards per desktop page, two columns on tablet and one on narrow phones. Preserve filters, page, selection and focus on return. Retain mission access without medal locks.

Results prioritize artwork and earned progress, with Next primary, Retry secondary and Missions always available. On the final mission, replace Next with the named next campaign, Download & play when needed, or Choose campaign when the edition is complete. Failed Next preserves the completed result. Optional briefings and result animations never become repeated confirmation screens. This adopts the quick-retry and skippable-feedback approach demonstrated in [Celeste's shipped changes](https://www.celestegame.com/changelog.html).

P07 deliberately changes the installed-content candidate's ordinary Next/Rematch policy from Ready-plus-Start to one-action launch. A fresh activation records a launch intent tied to the exact operation, target, owner and navigation context. Start after preparation only while that intent is current, the game remains foreground and the input-release/neutral gate passes. Backgrounding, cancellation, departure or a superseding command invalidates the intent; completed preparation then stays at Ready and requires a fresh Start. Failed preparation retains Results. This bounded exception preserves explicit player control and must be tested with delayed installed content, not only bundled missions.

D-pad, stick, keyboard and touch share action meanings; prompts follow actual bindings. Spatial focus follows the visible responsive grid, scrolls into view, and survives dialogs and rotation. Closing a screen restores focus without resuming flight. Keep menu actions separate from flight actions and retain neutral-input/reconnect protections. These requirements align with [Microsoft's consistent navigation guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/112) and [Valve's controller-accessible launch and UI criteria](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/steamhardware/compat).

Every delayed action acknowledges before awaiting work. Use truthful phases and measured progress where available; retain Cancel/Back/Retry and stale-operation protection. Target visible acknowledgement within 100 ms on qualified devices. Count active menus and confirmations in test journeys: Start/Continue, Retry and Next each require one fresh activation; joining/assigning Couch players is measured separately from mode-plus-Start.

### Level variety and fair challenge

The [completed reference review](research/xposed-adaptation-ledger.md) and [machine-readable ledger](research/xposed-adaptation-ledger.json) now cover all 64 supplied files, including original SHA-256, dimensions, visual observations, proposed adaptations and outstanding acceptance. The 48 numbered references map to the four FPV campaigns in their supplied pack/level order; the other 16 map to screens and feedback without increasing the 132-mission budget. This completes the design mapping, not production of those levels. Screenshots do not establish undocumented AI, timing or collision behavior; sampled video/manual findings retain their limits.

Build the three-mission benchmark at introductory, intermediate and advanced positions within the planned campaigns, not as extra mission count. Prove an approachable opening, a route-choice problem, and a telegraphed encounter combination. Each twelve-mission campaign opens with an approachable lesson or refresher, alternates exploration with combinations, and finishes with mastery of established rules. The per-mission lesson labels are explicit; a rigid three-level grouping must not force a new mechanic before players can understand the previous one. Introduce one unfamiliar role at a time, no shooters in the first three missions, and at most two shooter missions per campaign.

Vary chamber geometry, safe approaches, slow/lethal terrain, boundary traffic, revealed-ground threats, capture opportunities and existing abilities. Every mission specifies its learning goal, signature route choice, intended counterplay, art landmark and difficulty variants. Avoid producing novelty solely by increasing speed or enemy count.

Retain the approved deterministic auxiliary limits, domains, fixed-aim warnings, cooldowns, projectile limits, contact/capture defeat ordering and bounded rewards. Present warning → commitment → recovery clearly. Optional remains are a separate overlay; clean original artwork survives. Friendly/support roles use assistance or collection outcomes rather than combat imagery. Cosmetic settings never change collision or score authority.

Expose Gentle/Standard/Hard and encounters on/off beside preparation and Retry. Changes apply at the next explicit attempt boundary, preserve unlocked content and are recorded with the attempt. Use consistent visible difficulty names while retaining historical readers. Keep reduced effects independent of difficulty. After repeated failure, offer a quiet route hint and difficulty shortcut without forcing a modal. This adapts [Microsoft's difficulty guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/108).

Edition treatments retain a shared visual grammar while changing setting and meaning:

- **FPV:** orchards, field routes, industrial yards and radio infrastructure; readable craft, patrols and deliberate threat windows.
- **DroneAid:** workshops, community logistics, reconnaissance and communications; add wildfire-response-inspired artwork within the existing Support allocation. Current [German](https://drone-aid.de/), [Dutch](https://drone-aid.nl/en) and [Portuguese wildfire](https://drone-aid.pt/pt/open-call/wildfires) pages support these themes. Keep regional claims distinct and use the supplied brand sources for identity, optimized logo motion and a static poster.
- **Living Atlas:** regional landscapes, architectural and textile motifs with cultural review; avoid reducing the edition to flag recolours.
- **After School Arcade:** cassette loops, playground routes and CRT-era colour, with optional scanlines away from critical text.
- **Spend in Motion:** supplier routes, approval bottlenecks and reconciliation; resolve exceptions with clear noncombat feedback.

Do not silently rewrite old owned maps. Classify existing content as Curated, Training, Legacy or Imported; retain old identities and publish redesigned editions as new revisions. Every new campaign has complete required artwork, compatible-mode bindings and actual play review before promotion.

## 4. Test, fix and release loop

Repeat in a real paused panel across rotation and Large/Plain text. Measure against the actual clipping container as well as the window, keep native scroll defaults, and prove that focus restoration does not resume play. Keep announcements in one visible status location through entry and exit. These requirements came from reproduced Couch failures, not only static style review.
