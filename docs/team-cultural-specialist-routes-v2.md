## Cultural source boundaries

- [Museum Fund record 73059](https://museum.mincult.gov.ua/collections/rushnik-vesnyanki-73059)
  documents a wide central band, repeated rhomboid/cross-like forms and smaller forms building a
  larger focus. Crossed gardens borrows central emphasis, nested scale and band rhythm only.
- [Museum Fund record 62650](https://museum.mincult.gov.ua/collections/kilim-62650) documents
  vertical symmetry, alternating plain/ornamented horizontal bands and bent band edges. Split
  orchards borrows mirrored organization, alternating density and offset edges only.
- [Museum Fund record 157967](https://museum.mincult.gov.ua/collections/kilim-157967) documents
  bands of different widths, elongated rhomboid figures and thin separators. Weaver crossing
  borrows variable-width lanes, an elongated central opening and separator rhythm only.

No object, motif, stitch/weave chart, element count, palette, meaning or source coordinate is
copied. These official descriptions constrain broad composition; they do not establish cultural
approval or gameplay balance.
