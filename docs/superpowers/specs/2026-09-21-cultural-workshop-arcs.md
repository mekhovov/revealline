# Ornament and workshop spatial arcs — slice C1

User-authorized continuation of the pressure/variety programme. This increment
authors eight greyboxes using the shared map compiler, not a new engine or a
replacement for the original 95 candidates. Final artwork follows route and
readability qualification. No release enrollment is implied by a Studio button.

## References and interpretation

Checked 2026-09-21: the [Ivan Honchar Museum rushnyk collection](https://honchar.org.ua/collections/view/rusniki)
documents distinct textile objects; the [Ukrainian culture ministry](https://mincult.gov.ua/en/news/the-ukrainian-tradition-and-art-of-egg-decoration-recognized-as-the-cultural-heritage-of-humanity/)
describes pysanka as a living egg-decoration tradition shared with Estonia in the
UNESCO nomination. UNESCO's direct pages were access-challenged on this check;
search indexing and the ministry support the general attribution, not an invented
meaning for each mark. [AirXonix's developer rules](https://www.axysoft.com/airxonix/)
support the distinction between field danger and threats on reclaimed territory.

Use original stepped diamonds, repeated textile bands and segmented egg-inspired
forms, never traced museum coordinates or copied artwork. Call these inspired
game geometries, not authentic regional embroidery patterns. Pysanka is not
embroidery. Dnipro crossings are a geographic motif, not an archaeological claim.
Workshop geometry uses component silhouettes only; no wiring/assembly/weapon
instructions. No human combat is introduced in this increment.

## Chosen approach

Two four-mission optional arcs, each 4–5 broad bands. They share the existing
continuous/grid controls, capture contract, permanent foundations and wall rules.
All levels use explicit `journey-difficulty-v2` pressure and registered tiers;
warning lengths remain unchanged. Ordinary play has no countdown and no required
pickup. Do not add timed pickups until baseline route qualification is complete.

Alternative rejected: picture-shaped solid walls everywhere. This creates isolated
auto-fill pockets and decorative maze cleanup without interesting choices.
Alternative rejected: eight recolours of one layout. Each map below has a distinct
topology, route decision and capture consequence. Decorative detail belongs in
future art, not collision cells that obscure walls versus valid return surfaces.

## Authored contracts and delivery

- Ordinary `ContentProjectV1`, `MapDesignV1`, `MissionDesignV1`, campaigns and packs;
  separate original IDs, immutable revisions, explicit Solo/Versus qualification.
  Team omitted until genuinely purpose-built; no automatic mode fallback.
- Every mission records decision, lesson, counterplay, capture consequences,
  memorable moment, mastery, practiced/combined mechanics, duration60–150s target
  and difficulty facets. Targets are not measured clear times.
- Prefer two or three threat roles, base standard keeper speed (4.48 Standard /
  5.6 Expert with V2), unforced departures, broad return landings, walls at least
  two cells thick and deliberate gaps at least three cells where practical.
- New UI inspection loads a candidate into Source only; explicit Apply remains
  required. Existing original/public projects and progress remain unchanged.
- Greybox thumbnails derive from exact compiled topology; no generated background
  masquerading as tested geometry. Final original compositions, cultural review,
  music and actor material work remain separately tracked.

## Gates

1. Strict compile for all eight × three presets × Solo/Versus; parity, no mutation,
   no duplicate map identities, interior spawn clearance, usable departures and
   return opportunities. Inspect four-connected components; reject unintended
   empty isolated chambers or blocked required objectives.
2. Real-input clear routes, exported replay, no-loss feasibility on both steering
   policies; multiple seeds and bonus-independent routes. If a route cannot be
   proved, retain greybox status and redesign, never lower a test assertion.
3. Native Studio maps and controls, then original art. Human test distinguishing
   each mission and its failures, pressure balance, no quota cleanup and optional
   mastery value. Automated solvers do not prove45–150s or enjoyable pacing.
4. Exact reviewed successor integration/release/Pages through release owner. No
   modification of frozen v0.77.0, silent catalogue replacement or legacy migration.
