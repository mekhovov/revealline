# Team roamer return-network successors

Status: explicit prototype implemented and independently reviewed; technical/native
evidence and limits in [the verification report](../../verification/journey-team-roamer-spatial.md).
Implements a scoped part of the remaining P14
spatial/teaching work identified by the whole-Team pressure audit; not a new rule,
published replacement or human balance claim.

## Evidence driving the change

All144 ordinary pressure configurations now have no-down command routes, but
Shared Lookout Expert clears without activating its only roamer. Both Twin Depots
stronger presets activate only one of two. Their optimized16–37-second paths and
largely alternating-seat workload do not establish the intended wake/escape lesson.
More speed alone cannot repair a placement that lets the central behavior remain
irrelevant. Preserve those old paths and recipes for comparison.

## Authored scope

Create a separate `ContentProjectV1` containing only Shared Lookout and Twin
Depots, explicit pressure-v2 and new map/mission/campaign/pack revisions. Use the
existing TeamMissionV3 compiler, terrain/foundation rules and claimed-rover domain.
Do not change the default catalogue, published pins, Team host, artwork revisions,
schema, player speed, coverage quotas, warning120 ticks, Support or joint rules.
No automatic enrollment. Import/Inspect/Apply and Team test-pack export remain
the shared authoring route; no new dedicated menu is needed.

### Shared Lookout: a changing return, not a distant sleeper

- Retain the two broad spawn islands. Add one central reclaimed landing and two
  staggered horizontal baffles. The landing provides a shorter subsequent bridge;
  the baffles make approach direction matter without sealing an empty chamber.
- Place the single dormant roamer on the short outward western return, away from
  both spawn footprints. Its initial heading follows that newly reclaimed return.
  Taking the quick outside route wakes a visible threat behind the craft; an
  inward connection can establish another exit first. The partner can improve the
  network without being forced to perform exactly half the work.
- Distribute two same-tier retainers with diagonal headings across the upper and
  lower approaches. This changes route timing, not the registered speed scale.
- Retain74% coverage, one introduced rule and the existing optional activation
  mastery. No new compulsory objective or required waiting for an animation.

### Twin Depots: outer pressure versus inner landings

- Keep the full-height central divider and both starting islands. Add one inner
  landing and a broad internal baffle per occupied chamber, with clear top/bottom
  passages. A craft can take a quick outer return or wrap a baffle toward a useful
  interior landing; walls still never bank a trail.
- Place the two dormant roamers on their respective outward return lanes. Add a
  second same-tier keeper in each chamber so a single long sweep is less likely
  to remove the interesting geometry before either wake-up matters.
- Retain76% coverage, two known threat roles, no timer and no required pickups.
  Increase the authored density facet to reflect the actual added retainers.
- Chamber order and assistance stay player choices. Do not require equal actions
  or a joint cut merely to satisfy a test. Both ordinary and optional routes must
  be distinguished from meaningful human cooperation.

## Acceptance and iteration

1. Strict compilation at all three presets, owned copies, distinct successor
   identities and exact old-edition fixtures. No error diagnostics, blocked spawn,
   unintended unoccupied field chamber, or geometry overlapping a spawn/actor.
2. Compare recorded original and pressure routes on the successor. Their failure
   is not required by itself: record what they capture, when roamers warn/activate
   and whether the old bypass still exists. Do not tailor a single trap to one log.
3. Fresh public-input no-down clears at every preset, joint on/off and relabelled
   seats. At least one purposeful route per preset exposes every authored roamer
   several seconds before victory, with subsequent self-returns and an available
   escape. Never insert idle waiting solely to satisfy an active-time metric.
4. Test outer and inner opening choices, exact120-tick wake warnings, no inevitable
   spawn contact, delayed departures and both chambers retained independently.
   Store real first-knockdown failures and incomplete mastery rather than easing
   the recipe or asserting every possible route must activate every roamer.
5. Exact-source native Studio/import/first-return and wake-state readability.
   Human route choice, voluntary cooperation, controller/touch, full picture
   readability, meaningful pacing and accepted-release adoption remain separate.

Geometry coordinates may be revised after bounded greybox probes, with recorded
reasons. An optimized clear under45 seconds is a review flag, not permission to
add filler, inflate coverage or fabricate human timing. No final balance signoff
from a solver or automatic restart.

Review caveat: an active horizontal roamer can remain confined to its first
one-cell return rail. A qualifying route must make a subsequent purposeful return
or escape decision around the active patrol, not merely accumulate warning or
activation counts. Twin's added retainers require explicit Expert pressure checks.
