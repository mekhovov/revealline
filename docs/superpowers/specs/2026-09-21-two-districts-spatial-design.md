# Two Districts: pressure-aware spatial successor

Status: scoped design for independent review. No human balance or release approval.
User-authorized automatic continuation replaces routine approval pauses. Existing
isolated worktree, compiler, pressure catalogue and release-owner boundaries remain.

## Evidence and intended decision

The unchanged pressure-v2 Two Districts clears in17.45–24.85seconds on the four
new Standard/Expert paths; all four also satisfy its existing optional mastery.
Its authored ordinary duration is95–150seconds. Expert's historical immediate
straight-left departure loses a life at tick353 in both controls; the new winning
route uses a different approach. These facts justify spatial review, not a claim
that a specific replacement will be enjoyable.

Preserve the identity: two occupied districts separated by a permanent center
return, western slow field, eastern lethal field, erosion and frontier pressure.
The intended choice is whether to bank a short island return, attempt a larger
enclosure, or switch districts when erosion makes defending a slice unattractive.
Both districts must remain materially different routes, not mirrored chores.

## Alternatives and selected approach

1. **Selected:** asymmetric return islands and open staggered baffles, plus one
   familiar field keeper in each district. More retained regions after cuts create
   a reason to plan several captures instead of one broad slice per district.
2. Higher quota, compulsory repair count or new objective gates: rejected for
   this study. They could prolong cleanup or force waiting for erosion without
   making the core enclosure choice better.
3. More speed, shorter warnings or a countdown: rejected. Shared pressure-v2 is
   unchanged and the observed problem includes a failing Expert opening already.

## Authored successor and bounds

- New copy-on-write candidate factory/project revision; preserve the original
  Fracture factory, pressure projection, identities, maps and recorded replays.
  One changed mission only, exposed as an explicit Studio inspect/Apply option.
- Retain the center spine,77% earned-coverage quota, no required objectives,
  three existing role families: two territory eroders, one frontier patrol,
  two added field keepers. No new actor schema, mechanic or extra speed tier.
- Add an offset small return island in each district, deliberately asymmetric
  in position/shape. Add up to four open baffles total. No completely sealed new
  chamber, automatic empty-region fill, dead-end spawn or sub-cell geometry.
  New obstacle/return positions must alter departure, return or herding decisions.
- At startup there must be exactly two retained field components, each seeded by
  an eroder and a keeper. New islands must have at least four legal departure
  cells; existing spine remains permanently protected from erosion.
- Tune fixed initial actor positions/headings and frontier edge assignment using
  native geometry/actual-input evidence. Do not tune by hidden seed adaptation,
  per-preset map changes, softened collisions, warning changes or replay mutation.
- Pick a documented immediate first return for each preset/control. Prefer the
  same direction/route in all six. A seed1 idle10second probe must be lossless.
  That is a start-pressure check, not a promise that reclaimed ground is immune.
- Keep the original optional goal: close a cut in both districts and neutralize
  the eastern hazard without a loss. Repairs remain optional and must not require
  deliberately waiting for erosion. New descriptions explain the keeper/eroder
  retention and island/large-cut choice without a mandatory dialogue.
- Greybox only, clearly labeled candidate. Preserve original picture assets but
  do not falsely mark the successor as final visually qualified content. No
  enrollment, default edition replacement, version allocation or public promotion
  in this isolated lane. Release owner integrates after scoped gates.

## Implementation sequence and acceptance

1. Create the additive factory and inspect its map/compiler/capture diagnostics.
2. Reproduce original four short input routes as historical controls, then show
   those exact inputs no longer yield a no-loss clear on the successor. A changed
   checkpoint alone is insufficient; a merely delayed equivalent shortcut is
   still a pacing concern to investigate.
3. Find and record new no-loss public-input clears in all six preset/control
   configurations, replay and independent equal paired-board races. Measure
   duration, closures, exposure, per-district capture, optional goal and repairs.
   Collect no bonus; never inject cells, actor state, objectives or completion.
4. Verify all six first-return and10second idle probes, plus six seed2 and six
   seed1/180tick-delayed clear samples. Successful alternate routes may differ;
   failed samples remain explicit review gates, not silently dropped.
5. Inspect the new layout and actual first returns in native Studio/game. Confirm
   old project state is untouched until Apply; Undo restores it. Run old Fracture,
   shared capture/foundation/pressure and Studio regression cohorts Node20/22.
6. Publish comparison evidence and remaining human/native/device/art/host/release
   gates. Do not equate slower automated clears with enjoyment or require a
   manufactured minimum wait. If optimized clears remain very short or the added
   density overwhelms the opening, revise geometry or hold the candidate back.
