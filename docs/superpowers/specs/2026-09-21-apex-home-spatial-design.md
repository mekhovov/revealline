# Home Signal — contested-return study

Scope: one explicit copy-on-write Apex Home Signal geometry/placement study.
Preserve historical editions, sole-anchor capture, pressure-v2, enemy roles and
recipes, controls, 85% quota, three shields, release windows, non-scoring permanent
connector and optional two-wing visit mastery. No new mandatory mechanic, timer,
larger lives allowance or artificial wait. Other four Apex missions remain exact.

## Evidence and intended change

The existing Standard/immediate legal route has two short connector closures at
ticks 2106 and 2382 which raise earned coverage from 22.0% to 97.46%. The last
shield then falls at 99.25%; core release completes at tick 3270. An older Expert
route reaches its third shield with only 40 field cells remaining. The problem
is not merely an optimized clear below the human-duration aspiration: convenient
near-core returns collapse the shield approaches into a tiny remaining seed box.

Use separated, open shield approaches with less starting reclaimed ground beside
the core. A western launch should offer an upper/lower investment, then a useful
non-scoring transfer route after the first shield. Walls are obstacles, never
return surfaces. A captured region without the Sentinel still fills: geometry
must not create initially empty remote chambers or a one-mouth seed pen that
trivially fills every remote shield.

This is a spatial experiment, not a new capture rule. Multiple shields in one
valid enclosure and legal core isolation remain allowed. No minimum clear time
is enforced, and slower chosen routes do not hide known faster counterexamples.

## Implementation and gates

1. Add an explicit successor factory and new revisions using the shared compiler.
   Retain original picture bindings as candidate artwork, not a new art approval.
   Validate one retained initial field, usable departures, frontier attachment,
   objective clearance and safe initial decision space across all presets.
2. Reproduce historical fast routes unchanged. Compare their outcomes on the new
   edition and retain negative observations without claiming universal hardness.
3. Qualify new legal-input clear/replay/equal-race routes across all three presets
   and both steering options. Inspect shield grouping, coverage and field size at
   transition, connector traversal, exposure, release cause and post-quota tail.
   Use optional mastery evidence separately from ordinary clears.
4. Make the successor inspectable through existing Studio Inspect/Apply/preview,
   without automatically replacing a saved draft, whole-Journey source or progress.
5. Independent review, Node20/22 regression, native exact-preview observations,
   evidence ledger and coordinated release-owner handoff. Human pacing, device
   and whole-host adoption remain explicit gates; no phase acceptance is inferred.

## Outcome: provisional, not finale pacing acceptance

The first baffle layout accidentally formed a small Sentinel pen; its two short
clears are retained as rejected controls. Moving receivers near the original
locations also left a five-cell isolation shortcut. A brisk tier and a nearer
roamer position did not contest the recorded returns and were not adopted.

The selected open-baffle layout moves only the measured-tier roamer to
42.5,22.5 relative to its frozen geometry control. Three old immediate-steering
logs now hit that roamer after its full warning. Alternate departure timing,
an upper return and the three grid-control routes remain available. Eleven
no-loss routes include all six preset/control combinations, two delayed seed-two
routes, an upper return, pre-transition dock traversal and a preserved fast
isolation control. Every clear includes a fresh cut after roamer activation.
Twelve additional fixed-log delay probes retain eight successes and four losses.

Optimized clears are still 15.05–21.75 seconds. Grouped shield capture remains
legal; the inherited optional mastery can be satisfied at the inner landing,
not necessarily a far wing. Dock traversal is demonstrated before transition,
not as a post-activation escape. These limits keep the original finale-pacing
ambition open. Do not automatically enroll this study or count it as P12 accepted.
