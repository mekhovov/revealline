## Apex v25 scope

### Crossing complete

- Decision: fast central close before the frontier turns, or a longer west close beside the
  lethal belt for a quieter next departure.
- Geometry: three stepped obstacle focuses, four return surfaces and symmetric side terrain.
- Cultural boundary: broad three-focus, nested-centre and rhythmic-edge organization only.

### Returning light

- Decision: exposed centre crossing before the lane warning, or an upper stepping return before
  reclaimed-ground pressure activates.
- Geometry: triangular side cadence, separated step returns and a contested middle landing.
- Cultural boundary: broad triangular side-band, scattered-centre and end-symmetry organization
  only.

### Home signal

- Decision: short central dock with immediate frontier pressure, or a longer south-wing return
  before choosing the four-relay order.
- Geometry: four-point wall organization, a central dock and distinct relay approaches.
- Cultural boundary: broad centre-to-four-points and ornamental-perimeter organization only.

## Risks and non-claims

- Source PR completion is not public delivery.
- Automated routes establish legality, determinism and preserved ownership; they do not establish
  fun, cultural approval or final balance.
- The release stack is deliberately serialized because every successor preserves earlier editions
  and depends on their exact identities.
- Long suites, frozen build provenance, release hashes, Pages verification and physical-device
  checks remain release gates; skipped checks are never reported as passing.
- Original missions, Classic/custom content, uploaded media, settings, Studio projects and
  historical releases remain preserved.
