## Remaining production batches

1. **Campaign ownership and balance:** assign every selected wide map a stable ID, revision, authored Arcade/Tactical policy, difficulty recipes, mandatory goal, mastery targets, first-run introduction and legal route evidence. Preserve older revisions.
2. **Art coverage:** fill each selected map's four themes, then produce forty separately curated reserves. Record exact dimensions, SHA, provenance, prompt, replacement identity and inspection at partial and full reveal.
3. **Character coverage:** finish seven roles × four themes × two visual treatments. Inspect idle, movement heading, speed response, ability, warning, hit and recovery states. Sharing a rig is allowed; a recolor or configuration key is not an inspected finished set.
4. **Story coverage:** produce eleven further stories toward twelve total, three per theme. Verify poster choice, independent segment, win recorded once, Play/Pause/Skip, exact ending image, Collection and paired recovery.
5. **Soundtrack and SFX:** finish twenty-four tracks, predominantly synth with chiptune/rock/metal variety. Record full duration, provenance, listening, game mix and transition decisions. Map inspected effects to real events with separate music/effect/cinematic gains.
6. **Product art and qualification:** finish title, focus, chapter, terrain, pickup, achievement, failure, collection and promotional assets. Test contrast, reduced effects, localization/text length and actual devices.

Every row progresses through **planned → produced → inspected → source-integrated → browser-verified → released**. A prompt is not produced art; a source test is not browser playback; browser playback is not a physical-device or human-enjoyment assessment. The [shared AI workflow](feature-delivery-workflow.md) and [prompt examples](../authoring/prompts/media-presentation.md) apply to every batch.
