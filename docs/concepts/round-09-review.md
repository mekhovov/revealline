# Round 09 — original bodies and world-role artwork

Four new body concepts are bound to the motion/ability lab. One additional world-role sheet is displayed in the reference atlas. All five were generated with the built-in image tool, visually inspected, copied into the project and recorded with their full effective prompts in [the generation record](round-09-generated-prompts.json). Originals remain preserved; no programmatic image editing was used.

Read-only Pillow inspection verified body dimensions and alpha. Small gold hub regions were measured read-only to estimate normalized motor centers, then supplied to the renderer author. Heavy and scout rigs use different anchors; motor count remains independent of blades per motor. The source record includes hashes and durable project paths.

All four bodies are **original concepts**, not verified replicas of DJI, Vampire, STING or another named model. Manufacturer and official references inform categories and broad identity; they do not establish exact geometry, measurements or flight performance for this artwork. The heavy image is a six-motor example; the broader reference catalog also contains four- and eight-motor families.

The world-role sheet shows a tank, wheeled armour, cargo and medical support vehicles, radar and communications trucks, artillery and box launcher, an engineer, a medic, a tracked supply robot and an uncrewed boat. It uses a three-quarter frontal view, rather than the exact north-up overhead camera required for playable units. All bear friendly Ukrainian-themed accents where shown. There are no Z markings or graphic injury scenes. Enemy/support allegiance and separate actor behavior remain mission-data decisions.

The sheet deliberately remains a reference. Individual sprite exports, transparent backgrounds, pivots, directional frames, enemy variants and actual actor binding have not been created from it. It must not be registered as twelve ready production sprites.

The supplied body PNGs are high-resolution source concepts. Small-size pixel cleanup, production atlases, cargo/spool/gimbal component artwork, exact rotor alignment and physical-device readability remain further art work. The current lab supplies procedural moving attachments and ability cues; [preview evidence](../../authoring/evaluations/round-09-preview-checks.md) records what was actually exercised.
