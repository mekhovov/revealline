## Effective prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one polished original gameplay art-direction study for FPV FRONT, a Ukrainian FPV-drone Xonix territory-reveal arcade. It should visibly communicate playable geometry and threats, inspired by the clarity of classic territory-capture games, with beautiful modern 1990s-style pixel art. Landscape image. One large flat rectangular arena taking almost all the image, a very slim readable status row above, and a small three-symbol legend below. Quiet dark navy frame with only two tiny Ukrainian embroidery corner accents. Title FPV FRONT, small subtitle DESIGN STUDY. No device mockup.
Arena: a coarse, deliberate rectilinear cell grid, crisp right-angle stepped capture boundaries. Most of the arena is genuinely near-black unrevealed space; about the lower-left third reveals a detailed but muted Ukrainian dawn illustration with wheat fields, a small rail siding, and clearly recognizable fictional invading Russian military trucks and armored vehicles. These vehicles are painted into the revealed artwork, lower contrast and without live-enemy outlines. Only the revealed portion shows the illustration; do not show scenery through the unrevealed area.
The board has an authored three-chamber spatial puzzle: two solid steel-blue tile wall bars separate chambers, each with a clear wide gap; two small patches of sparse indigo arrow tiles indicate player-slow terrain; two small patches of red crossed hatch tiles indicate lethal terrain. Wall blocks, slow tiles and lethal tiles must look unmistakably different by shape and density. Keep generous empty travel space and quiet negative space between them. All obstacle geometry is orthogonal, no isometric maze.
Player is a small but clearly recognizable blue-and-yellow quadcopter with four separated rotors and a bright ivory outline, at the endpoint of ONE thin luminous ivory-gold L-shaped exposed trail extending from the revealed lower-left region into a black chamber. Do not put a decorative glow over its route. Different hostile entities: a small red diamond scout inside unknown territory; an amber notched gear visibly on an INNER stepped capture boundary rather than only the outer rectangle; a small orange outlined wheel roaming on the REVEALED ground; a broader magenta mechanical diamond near a narrow captured strip, suggesting an advanced territory-eroding enemy. Keep player brightest, trail next, enemy silhouettes next, scenery subdued. No human figures, no gore.
One small collectible freeze icon sits behind a wall gap as an optional route incentive, clearly distinct from enemies. Bottom legend has exactly three simple samples with labels WALL, SLOW, DANGER. Slim top HUD contains life pips, AREA 36 / 80, a restrained timer and small score; no giant dashboard, no elaborate menus, no extra copy. Rich intentional pixel clusters, hard square sprite edges, minimal bloom, no smooth 3D plastic, no blurred watercolor gameplay sprites. Preserve Ukrainian blue/gold identity without turning the whole screen into a flag. This is an original game concept screenshot, not a reproduction of any existing game's art or layout.
