## Scope and overlap audit

This batch starts from protected `main` at `251f2e859df42e8fc8e9b3bc412982b925b93fdf` and changes exactly three existing mission identities:

- `stepping-stones`: an early foundation lesson with upper and lower route branches.
- `four-quarters`: an original asymmetric stepped-band wall study. The geometry is informed by the rhythm and directional changes of Ukrainian geometric craft, but it does not copy a chart, artwork, or named symbol.
- `survey-markers`: an original offset FPV workbench/trace study. It does not copy a real flight controller or antenna layout.

Open PR323 already owns `two-bays`, `neon-remix` (Three open circuits), `broken-yard`, `read-the-arrows`, `twin-receivers`, and `crossing-complete`. Open PR332 owns the eight optional ornament/workshop identities plus `two-districts`, `two-ways-home`, `second-approach`, and `windbreak-weave`. None of those 18 identities is modified or selected here.

## Evidence boundary and remaining human work

Focused automated checks validate exact selection/disposition, copy-on-write preservation, compilation in Solo and Versus, current preset scaling, connected field topology, usable departures, collision-role restriction, idle safety, two distinct no-loss opening approaches, and equal paired-board outcomes.

They do **not** prove that the full missions are fun, that either approach is equally attractive, that wall silhouettes read clearly against every picture/theme, that physical controller timing is comfortable, or that full clears avoid tedious cleanup across all presets and control policies. A human balance pass should specifically compare the lower Stepping Stones branch, the north-west and south-east Four Quarters band ends, and the marked versus unmarked Survey Markers approaches before any enrollment proposal.
