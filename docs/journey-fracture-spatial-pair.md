# Fractured Grid spatial successor pair

Status: candidate editions; default-route enrolment and human balance review remain separate.

This fifth batch stacks on the four earlier spatial pairs. It revises only
`bank-the-crossing` and `five-anchors` (`Five landings`). Their IDs, names, Journey order,
required objectives, coverage, gameplay policy, actor roles, modes and presentation stay
unchanged. Earlier source factories and editions remain available without reinterpretation.

## Research and cultural boundary

Both layouts use the existing capture contract. Walls obstruct movement but cannot close a
cut; foundations are permanent returns; slow field affects exposed craft; field-retaining
enemies determine what fills; captured anchors protect authored connections against erosion.

Five landings uses an original paired-curl composition inspired by the documented
**“Баранячі роги” (“Ram's horns”)** vocabulary. The
[Museum Fund of Ukraine catalogue entry](https://museum.mincult.gov.ua/collections/vustavka-baranyachi-rogi)
describes a late-nineteenth-century Hutsul/Pokuttia embroidered insert as a band of rhombi
with small ram's-horn forms, bordered by triangle bands with horns and a chain of small
rhombi and crossings. The mission borrows only the idea of repeated open curls and mirrored
bands. It does not copy the textile's stitches, coordinates, colours or asserted meaning.

Bank the crossing is an original FPV assembly abstraction: the central foundation suggests
a carbon-frame jig, side foundations suggest equipment pads, and broken walls suggest
clamps. It is gameplay geometry, not a literal or instructional equipment diagram.

## Bank the crossing

The previous map began on two broad horizontal banks and offered six large permanent
surfaces. That made the protected crossing anchor important in rules text but often
unnecessary for the first spatial decision.

Distinct decision: connect the short center of the frame jig, making the required crossing
anchor a nearby follow-up, or take a long route to the isolated equipment pad before joining
its reclaimed-ground roamer to the larger network.

Two intended approaches:

1. **Center jig first:** connect the central cross, bank the anchor through its lower arm,
   then use the protected path as a repair route while the cutter threatens adjacent gains.
2. **Equipment pad first:** cross the upper field to the side pad, keeping its roamer on a
   small island until a deliberate later connection to the jig.

Capture consequence: the central route makes anchor protection immediately useful and
offers four departure orientations. The equipment route establishes a separate reserve but
creates a larger roamer domain once joined.

## Five landings

The previous map used five similar plus-shaped foundations with no walls. The revised board
keeps exactly five functional landings—a central cross and four corner pads—but makes the
two anchor approaches spatially different.

Distinct decision: use the open face of the upper-west horn to bank the western anchor, or
secure an eastern landing first and approach the lower anchor from the central cross while
the frontier patrol moves around it.

Two intended approaches:

1. **Central cross first:** take the short direct connection, read the frontier patrol and
   enter an anchor chamber through its open wall end.
2. **West landing first:** travel along the outer rail and connect the upper-left pad before
   approaching the western anchor laterally.

Capture consequence: each anchor protects a different connection against two eroders.
Corner landings change approach order and provide reserves; they remain choices rather than
five mandatory visits.

## Focused qualification

The deterministic test proves:

- copy-on-write preservation of all other missions, art, policy/catalog identities,
  objectives and authored campaign/pack order;
- both required anchor cells remain unclaimed, reachable field cells;
- one connected retained field, deliberate disconnected returns and no unexpected runtime
  topology diagnostics;
- unchanged eroder, keeper, roamer and frontier role counts on Gentle, Standard and Expert;
- identical Solo and Versus preparation on every preset;
- 720 safe idle ticks over two seeds on every preset;
- both first-closure approaches over two seeds, all presets and both steering policies.

The scripted closures do not capture either required anchor or complete a mission. They
prove opening reachability and safety, not whole-clear pacing, repair usefulness, enjoyment,
device readability or player understanding. Both candidates remain **balance review pending**.
