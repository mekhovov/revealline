## Decisions informed by research

XPOSED Reloaded's [official description](https://store.playstation.com/en-ie/concept/10002881) emphasizes simple controls, demanding capture challenges and picture/pack rewards. Keep that structure with original art and meaningful routes. Exact AI, button release and soundtrack claims require their own observed evidence; screenshots do not establish them.

[Xbox input guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/107) supports digital and analog operation throughout menus, alternatives to sustained holds and single-button actions. Keep tap-to-steer, explicit Resume and a complete title-to-Collection journey. The [round-46 research](research/round-46-campaign-input-and-reward.md) also proposes an optional active-play screen wake lock; it is not implemented or device-certified.

The original-byte external installer avoids inflating embedded JSON with image data URLs. Keep the 48 MiB embedded library, 256 MiB managed media and 64 MiB core bounds, explicit downloads, rollback and no automatic eviction. Original art and a compact chapter record are separate backup inventories. Current candidate adapters preserve exact saved/first-earned identities; migration of existing embedded editions and ordinary external removal remain separate work. [Native recovery evidence](verification/round-46/external-backup-native.md).

For music, [Holizna's Retro Wave collection](https://opengameart.org/content/retro-wave-collection) and [Kenney Digital Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/digital-audio) are credited CC0 candidates. The thirteen MP3s have codec and short native-playback evidence; their two candidate exports are replacement libraries, not additive packs. Full listening, game fit, loudness balance and transitions remain production work. Measured RMS varies substantially, but RMS is not perceptual loudness; follow [EBU loudness guidance](https://tech.ebu.ch/publications/tech3343) before setting reviewed gains. Keep original bytes unchanged.

Actual controller-only navigation and legibility at supported resolutions matter to [Steam Deck compatibility](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/steamhardware/compat). Browser success does not certify a native store build.

All thirteen project skills link to the [shared delivery, recovery and focus contract](feature-delivery-workflow.md), with [prompt examples](../authoring/prompts/media-presentation.md). Prompts and CLI examples support production; they are not finished assets or test results. Each delivered feature follows **commit → exact-source verification → frozen version → artifact/browser verification → PR/CI/merge → Pages/public verification → receipt**.
