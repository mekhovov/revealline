# v0.132.2 Team picture revision compatibility

## Player-visible problem

The v0.132.1 presentation compiler produced FPV theme revision 88 while Team's
two starter-arena picture bindings still treated revision 86 as current. The
strict presentation lease therefore rejected otherwise unchanged approved
artwork before **First Connection** or **Relay Yard** could start.

## Correction

- Bind the two reviewed Team originals to exact FPV revision 88.
- Retain revisions 58 through 87 for already accepted attempts.
- Extend the finite historical-import policy capacity for those two added
  retained revisions.
- Keep the image asset IDs, dimensions, byte counts, SHA-256 values and content
  identities unchanged. No latest-version alias or silent artwork fallback is
  introduced.

## Verification boundary

Focused binding, historical-import, successor-picture and Team host tests cover
the current and retained revision sets. The long automated suite remains
waived by the repository's temporary fast-release policy and must be reported
as skipped. Physical controller, touch and complete offline qualification remain
separate evidence.
