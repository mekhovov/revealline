## Exact qualification and failure rules

**Supply Line:** collect each named pad successfully at least once; perform the named successful hangar switch; and close a live cut containing at least two distinct suppressed endpoint cells in each named region. Each region needs its threshold in **one** closed cut; the two regions may be satisfied by different cuts. Keep each region's best closed-cut count, not a sum of fragments. This clarification is supported by the measured four-cell and three-cell crossings.

For crossing credit, require a running live cut, half-open rectangle membership (`x >= left`, `x < right`, likewise y), and `suppressedUntil > time + EPS`, using core's signal expiry tolerance (`1e-9`). Preserve v1's existing observer tolerance separately. Count floor-cell identity once. Commit the pending set on closure in original event order; discard pending cells on failed/shield-aborted/redeployed cuts. Never count a safe-ground walk, recovery teleport, stationary dwell twice, an expired field or an unsuppressed resistant crossing. Like Steady Signal, sample fixed-tick endpoints and do not invent sub-tick visits on the closing tick.

Supply Line does **not** add a clean-win condition. A later lost life does not undo successful pickups, switches or earlier closed cuts in the same run; unfinished cuts still discard their pending cells. A restarted/new run begins empty. The UI should not promise “no lives lost” for this goal or silently impose a mandatory pickup/switch order beyond the actual events.

**Safe Return:** before a successful impact use, the craft is running and cutting with at least three trail cells. The same tick creates a pulse overlapping the named actor using core's circle test, with that actor actually stunned and the craft redeployed with no life loss. Retain that candidate until its later `player.respawned` event; require running, no live trail and unchanged lives. A failure or unrelated recovery invalidates the pending candidate. Then require a real winning closure and no lost life anywhere in the run.

The initial exposed cut is deliberately abandoned; requiring that cut to close would contradict the ability. Conversely, a pulse on safe ground, a two-cell cut, a pulse missing the named actor, a different stun field, a rejected/cooldown action, a death respawn or a pulse without completed recovery cannot qualify. A previously stunned actor may still be overlapped by the new pulse; do not claim a new rescue or counterfactual necessity. One completed qualifying recovery is sufficient; repeated pulses do not multiply rewards.

Both previews remain preview-only, including caller-constructed fact tests. Only the existing installed-content, winning-replay/checkpoint verification path can yield the capability used for a local award. Continue preserving eligible-run/generation checks, queue capacity, abort behavior and cancellation before import/Undo backup snapshots. An optional observer error must disable its preview/check gracefully while normal gameplay, picture collection and progression continue.
