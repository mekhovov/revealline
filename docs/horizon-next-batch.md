The two Hutsul compositions are intentionally distinct. `island-outpost` uses small broken joins
and alternating return distance to study the Vyzhenka seam description. `long-way-home` uses broad,
offset shoulder groups and a lower gathered group to study the Verkhovyna construction description.
The geometry is original at game-grid scale: no artwork, stitch chart, garment outline, egg division
or museum coordinates are copied.

## Rules and qualification

- Foundations and negative space are the only changed gameplay geometry. These early Horizon
  missions receive no walls, terrain, speed zones or later Journey mechanics.
- Enemy roles, positions, counts, speeds, difficulty scaling, objectives, bonuses, time limits,
  coverage and presentation remain the exact v10 values.
- Each design has one connected claimable field, at least four departures from every safe component,
  a safe idle opening, and two executable authored approaches under all three presets, both control
  policies and two deterministic seeds.
- The successor uses `whole-spatial-v11` with isolated profile/session ownership. The three exact v10
  editions are manual-only Solo/Versus cards. The existing three v9 cards remain available as well.
  Prior cards launch their full receiving route and have no automatic Next; current v11 progression
  retains authored global order and skips all prior-edition cards.
- Prior-card metadata is compiled from two bounded three-mission projections, not either full
  historical 91-mission catalogue. Tests compare the projected v10 manifests with full v10 across
  all three missions, modes and presets.

## Evidence and remaining human work

Automated qualification covers exact copy-on-write scope, attribution separation, topology,
foundation/capture budgets, rule and enemy parity, preset/control/seed routes, safe openings,
replay stability, paired Versus state, route registration, selector identity, manifest parity and
Next behavior.

Automation does not establish whether either route is fun, whether the two choices remain equally
legible during a complete clear, or whether the cultural abstraction reads appropriately to people
familiar with these traditions. Required follow-up is a human cultural review and full Solo/Versus
clear on gentle, standard and expert, with special attention to keeper timing, route recovery and
whether the compact layouts are harder rather than merely different.
