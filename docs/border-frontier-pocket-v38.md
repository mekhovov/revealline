# Border frontier and pocket completion · v32 candidate

This bounded successor finishes the two remaining Border redesign dispositions without changing the
default Journey. `whole-spatial-v32` is an explicit balance-review route layered on v31; v25 stays
the default Solo and Versus experience, and every earlier edition remains addressable.

## Mission decisions

| Mission         | Distinct route decision                                                                                         | Attributed compositional input                                                                |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Turn the corner | Take the compact inner wave while the frontier leaves it, or commit around the longer outer shoulder            | Bukovyna pysanka geometric/floral sectioning and directional contrast, translated originally  |
| Return pocket   | Close the upper mouth first, or leave the side opening and stage a longer lower return under two patrol domains | Podillia rushnyk large-star and smaller-interstitial rhythm, translated into separated masses |

The maps reuse only large-scale rhythm and spacing. They do not copy a pysanka division, rushnyk,
symbol, stitch chart, palette, claimed meaning, or source coordinate. Their people, animals, and
meaning-bearing figures are never converted into targets or obstacle caricatures.

`behind-the-patrol` remains open because the accepted disposition preserves a readable first
perimeter-patrol lesson. `side-door-bays` remains open because its fifteen permanent-foundation
pieces already create the campaign's intentional contour puzzle. The audit therefore closes with
two deliberate open/plain boards, not an artificial zero count.

## Preserved contracts

- Current pressure actors, spawns, foundations, objectives, bonuses, timed-bonus behavior, reveal
  art, movement rules, difficulty presets, and Next ordering are unchanged.
- Each successor map retains one connected field and its intended disconnected return topology.
- Walls block movement and never become reclaimed return ground.
- Original editions, receipts, replays, progress, Classic/custom content, and user media are not
  reinterpreted or deleted.

## Verification boundary

The focused candidate test covers Gentle, Standard and Expert; Immediate and Grid + Buffer; seeds
1 and 2; two authored closures per mission; idle-safe starts; exact pressure preservation;
deterministic replay; and paired Versus equality. The full route-loader snapshot and bounded import
chain also cover the new v32 owner.

This is technical candidate evidence only. Human balance, cultural, physical-device, hosted
exact-head, production-build, release, and public Pages acceptance remain pending.
