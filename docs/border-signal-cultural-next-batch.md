# Border and Signal Ukrainian spatial successor draft

Status: implementation prepared on top of `whole-spatial-v12`; route registration,
edition cards, default enrollment, two-route closure evidence and release version are
intentionally deferred until the preceding Border batch is accepted.

## Scope

This copy-on-write draft redesigns three existing identities. It changes starting
foundation geometry and route guidance only. It preserves names, art, actors,
movement, collision rules, objectives, coverage, terrain, bonuses, timed schedules,
campaign order and mode qualification.

| Mission         | Authored decision                                                                                                        | Cultural design input                                                                   |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `border-remix`  | Establish the flexible central stem first, or cross mixed patrol pressure to create the far offset return first.         | Poltava-region vertical sleeve bands, staggered fields and plant/geometric composition. |
| `dry-spine`     | Work from the upper shoulder around the western slow bed, or descend to the lower shoulder beside the eastern bed first. | Bukovyna pysanka geometric/floral sectioning and directional contrast.                  |
| `wide-approach` | Use the west hook and intermediate link, or take the wider approach to the east hook first.                              | Slobozhanshchyna rushnyk long-field proportion and chain-stitch linkage.                |

The layouts do not increase permanent reclaimed area. Slow terrain remains exactly
authored: 488 rectangular source cells in `dry-spine` and 432 in `wide-approach`.
The `border-remix` frontier patrol retains its valid authored contour seed.

## Sources and limits

- The M. F. Sumtsov Kharkiv Historical Museum study documents Poltava women’s
  shirts, including vertical and staggered sleeve arrangements and plant,
  geometric and geometricized motifs such as branches and oak leaves:
  <https://museum.kh.ua/academic/publications.html?n=929>.
- The Ivan Honchar Museum catalogues a Bukovyna pysanka as geometric/floral and
  names cross, rhomb, pine, ox’s-eye and wave elements:
  <https://honchar.org.ua/en/collections/detail/1188>.
- The Ivan Honchar Museum catalogues a Slobozhanshchyna rushnyk from Okhtyrka
  district as hand chain-stitch embroidery on homespun hemp cloth with plant and
  zoomorphic ornament: <https://honchar.org.ua/collections/detail/2006>.

These records support attribution and broad compositional vocabulary. They do not
authorize copying an object, chart, palette or coordinates, and they do not support
assigning a universal meaning to an element. The game layouts are original,
rectilinear abstractions. People and animals are never translated into targets.
