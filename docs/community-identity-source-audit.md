# Community identity source review

Reviewed 28 September 2026 against the current official public pages and the game’s
selected brand catalogue. **Neither community’s official mark is cleared for inclusion
as a game character or installation icon by the evidence found.** Source discovery is
complete for this review; permission remains unresolved. This is a publication decision
for this project, not a claim that every possible use of a mark is prohibited.

## Current implementation

`social-drone-ua` and `victory-drones` both have `logoAssetId: null` and
`iconAssetId: null` in the generated catalogue. Their actor sets are the existing
original community markers. Their admitted home and mission scenes are original
fictional illustrations, not community-owned identity assets or photographs of events.
The independent-edition descriptions remain appropriate. No official logo bytes were
added to the source inventory, runtime catalogue, release output or PWA icon slots by
this review.

The icon dimensions above are declarations in official HTML, not an assertion that
these files satisfy our raster, padding or maskable-icon requirements. The current
headers and icon references were rechecked in live HTML; no image bytes were imported
or visually approved during this review. A CDN URL, a donation, a shop purchase,
source attribution or public course availability does not supply the missing grant.

Victory Drones’ notice is relevant even for a free educational game: permission to
share a complete unchanged informational item does not establish permission for the
different character/icon treatment proposed here. It also does not clear third-party
photos, partner marks or translated authors’ illustrations. This review inspected the
notice; it did not reproduce its technical training materials.

## Evidence needed before admission

Obtain an applicable public brand licence or an authorised rights-holder response
covering the exact mark file and these intended uses:

1. Distribution inside downloadable web/PWA games, offline caches and public GitHub
   release/source archives, with the required credit and any audience restrictions.
2. Supported raster derivatives, 192/512-pixel installation icons, padding/background
   treatment, and the requested character rotation or other presentation changes.
3. Clear wording distinguishing an independent game from an official service,
   endorsement or certification. A permission to use an image must not be recorded as
   a claim that the organisation endorses the game.

The [Victory Drones contact page](https://victory-drones.com/contact) and Social Drone’s
[official site](https://www.socialdrone.com.ua/) provide routes to their organisations;
the shop operator’s sales role alone does not establish ownership of the mark. No
outreach was sent and no permission was inferred from silence.

After clearance, use the existing source-review and exact-asset pipeline: retain the
applicable terms/permission record, identify the actual rights holder, hash the approved
original, review supported raster derivatives and their size/padding, register the
immutable revisions, and retain old presentation references. The catalogue already
validates a 512-pixel install derivative and its matching 192-pixel dependency. SVG
source discovery is not a reason to add runtime SVG support.

Original neutral installation artwork can be prepared separately without depicting an
uncleared mark. That would complete a presentation option, not fulfil official-logo
clearance. **Human artwork review, physical-device installation checks and any claim
of organisational endorsement remain uncompleted.**
