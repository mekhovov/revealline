# Remix readable-pressure pair

Status: candidate only; human balance and public integration pending.

This stacked successor replaces one generic keeper in two adjacent Remix missions after pursuit and
interception have already been taught. It preserves every earlier candidate and does not register a
route, change a default, bump a version or alter a historical edition.

| Mission        | Replacement                           | Two intended approaches                                                                                                                                                                       |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Phase remix    | East keeper → heading interceptor     | Use short closures from disconnected fallback landings and turn after lock; or wait through commitment on reclaimed ground, then connect the central circuit before the frontier returns.     |
| Livewire remix | Former carrier/keeper → trail pursuer | Bank a short refuge closure before the longer cross-emitter connection; or wait through an emitter cycle, draw pursuit away from the return and close the broad route before the second lane. |

Both replacements retain field and reuse the `journey-actors-v9` 90-tick warning, 144-tick finite
commitment and preset-specific recovery. A committed target never follows a later turn. Actor IDs,
counts, tiers, positions and headings remain exact. Maps, foundations, terrain, objectives, lane
emitters, frontier and reclaimed-ground rules, coverage, pictures, campaign order, defaults and
history are unchanged.

## Focused evidence

The focused checks compare this source directly with PR 461's preceding candidate, resolve all
three presets in Solo and Versus, and verify retained-field semantics and fixed committed targets.
Two distinct existing legal-input routes per mission replay through the new simulation:

- Phase remix Gentle/immediate: 11.25-second no-loss clear, five cuts and one cancelled committed
  intercept.
- Phase remix Expert/Grid + Buffer: 34.45-second no-loss clear, four cuts, intentional waits and one
  cancelled committed intercept.
- Livewire remix Standard/immediate: 50.25-second no-loss clear, ten cuts, five commitments, three
  closure cancellations and two completed recoveries.
- Livewire remix Standard/Grid + Buffer: 63.25-second no-loss clear, ten cuts, four commitments, two
  closure cancellations and two completed recoveries.

No route collects a bonus. Every checkpoint reproduces and each exact input finishes an equal
independent paired-board race. This proves two distinct deterministic implementations per mission
across both steering policies and all three preset samples; it does not establish that each route
perfectly represents the written human strategy or that every preset/control/seed combination is
viable. Native/device readability, broad seeds, mastery quality, fairness and fun remain **balance
review pending**.
