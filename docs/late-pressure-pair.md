# Late Journey readable-pressure pair

Status: candidate only; human balance and public integration pending.

This bounded successor studies two late missions after the established Phaseworks pursuit and
interception arcs. It does not register a new route, change defaults, bump a version or modify a
historical edition.

| Mission         | Replacement                             | Distinct decision                                                                                                                                                                    |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Long wave       | West field keeper → heading interceptor | Use alternating short directional links and feint toward the central landing, or wait on reclaimed ground and take the wider enclosure during the independent lane-emitter recovery. |
| Returning light | Field keeper → trail pursuer            | Use the fast eastbound link before pursuit commits, or close first at the upper stepping platform and preserve a separate reclaimed-ground escape before the roamer wakes.           |

Each replacement preserves the actor ID, count, tier, initial position, heading and field-retaining
contract. Maps, speed zones, foundations, lane emitters, the reclaimed-ground roamer, objectives,
coverage, artwork, campaign order and every unselected mission remain exact. The shared
`journey-actors-v9` recipe continues to provide a 90-tick/0.75-second warning, 144-tick/1.2-second
locked commitment and 300-tick/2.5-second Standard recovery. Gentle and Expert retain the existing
registered recovery factors. No actor retargets in mid-commitment.

## Focused evidence

The source checks every preset in Solo and Versus, verifies that exactly one ordinary keeper is
replaced, and compares the candidate to the current `whole-spatial-v9` source. Controlled pressure
checks establish the fixed target and finite waypoint after a course change.

Two previously recorded legal-input routes were replayed against the new candidate rather than
injecting state:

- Long wave, Standard/immediate: no-loss clear, twelve cuts, 95.65 seconds, three visible warnings
  and three commitments; no bonus collected.
- Returning light, Gentle/immediate: no-loss clear, twelve cuts, 49.85 seconds, four visible warnings
  and two commitments; no bonus collected.

Both routes reproduce their candidate checkpoints and finish equal independent paired-board races.
This proves bounded deterministic feasibility for one route per mission. It does **not** prove both
documented approaches, all preset/control combinations, native or physical-device readability,
human fairness, fun, mastery, broad seeds or final late-campaign pacing. The pair remains **balance
review pending** and must be explicitly integrated into a future versioned route before players can
select it.
