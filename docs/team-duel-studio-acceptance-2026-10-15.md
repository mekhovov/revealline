## Defect corrected

An uploaded gameplay cue could survive Studio save/export/import correctly and still be ignored in play. `Soundscape.event()` honored the published capture/failure/pickup choice, but the normal `FeedbackDirector` path selected its decoded built-in bank without consulting that choice. A replacement might therefore be heard only when the built-in recording was missing. New Team and Duel tests failed before the fix with **“cells.claimed must use the recovered original”** even though the exact restored WAV had reached the decoder.

The director now checks the supported published cue before its native recording. Capture, player failure, collected pickup/powerup and Team defeat honor their restored selection. Team victory retains its existing published owner. Both Duel boards retain separate ownership and family cooldowns. Shared mute/zero-volume, gameplay pause, feedback priority and the existing sixteen-feedback-voice reservation remain in force. If the custom cue is not ready, native feedback remains immediate; completion of a delayed decode never replays the past event. The existing material-contact accent remains independent of the main capture sound.

The regression fixture transports existing project WAV originals through two independent Studio stores, `.rltheme` export/import, `.rlruntime` export/import, the real presentation compiler and host, then the real published-audio and event-dispatch modules. It compares every original byte at the decoder boundary and the identity of the scheduled buffer. It covers ready and missing built-in banks, repeat/cooldown suppression, both Duel seats, zero-volume expiry, Team defeat/victory and a cold replacement becoming available only for the next event. Native decoding and perceived sound are explicitly outside that modeled boundary.

Independent review then reproduced a second issue: long custom routine cues could overlap after their cooldown, and a successfully decoded replacement could leave its previous native tail playing. The published owner now retires only the same board's matching semantic family after the new source actually starts. Budget rejection preserves the predecessor and never schedules fallback. Both before-fix regressions and the budget-denial case are retained in the Studio audio test file.
