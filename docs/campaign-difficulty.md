# Standard and Gentle campaign play

This guide describes the working same-map difficulty implementation, currently under verification. It is not a release, completed browser-acceptance or physical-device claim. The [accepted plan](round-30-gentle-difficulty-plan.md) defines the scope: solo authored campaigns, including installed expansions. Couch race, dated Challenges, First Flight and arbitrary Practice scenarios keep their own rules.

## Choose a preset

The flight deck's **Campaign difficulty** select offers two presets:

| Preset       | What to expect                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Standard** | The original mission's lives, hazards and limits. Earn Standard results and eligible optional equipment seals. Older profiles default to this mode.                                                                                                                                                |
| **Gentle**   | The same map and picture, at least five starting lives, slower moving enemies, no mission/cut deadline or maximum line length, and longer boss warnings/openings where their existing limits allow. Pictures, mission access and chapter appearances count; scores and medals have their own mode. |

Open the adjacent rule details to compare the selected map's actual values. Gentle still requires its original coverage target and required objectives. Self-crossing, enemy contact, border patrols and active boss lanes can still cost a life. Craft speed, steering, Boost and equipment behavior stay the same. This is neither invulnerability nor a guaranteed completion.

At **Ready**, changing difficulty prepares this map at zero elapsed time without starting it. During an unfinished flight, including a live cut, Pause or recovery, the choice applies **to the next fresh attempt**. The current flight retains its original rules and territory. **Resume** keeps that flight's mode. **Try again** explicitly starts from the beginning in the selected next mode, with a fresh run ID and the starting loadout; its current cut, reveal and resources reset. Previously collected rewards remain subject to the ordinary library rules.

The current-mode cue and reset note distinguish these cases. Changing the preference alone does not overwrite the suspended slot. Other actions retain their existing behavior: for example, opening Library pauses an unfinished flight and may autosave it. Keyboard, native touch controls and the existing controller select-preview/Confirm/Back behavior remain available. Changing a draft is not permission to resume; reading or leaving a result explanation never retries.

## Pictures, progress and results

A retained clear in either exact mode counts toward the same chapter's mission access and appearance milestones. Count each declared map once: clearing it in both modes does not earn two chapter clears. The first appearance tier needs one map; the final tier needs `min(4, chapter map count)`. Full chapter completion still requires every map. An already-cleared mission remains replayable after an out-of-order restore. First-clear, pathfinder and full-chapter achievements use shared access; clean/gold achievements remain labelled by mode.

Scores, medals and best-result statistics stay separate. A Gentle score does not replace a comparable Standard score or turn a Gentle medal into a Standard medal. **Optional equipment seals require Standard**, including a restored attempt: Gentle installs no Standard seal observer or award job. Existing seals retain their original definition and setup. The shared score history still has its existing **1,000-row cap**; ordinary recording and backup-merge retention can evict older rows from either mode. Separate identities do not promise permanent history retention.

The gallery groups recognized Standard/Gentle records for the same chapter, map and theme into one picture card while retaining their separate stored records. If both exist, the card says **Standard + Gentle** and Standard is the initial result; **Saved difficulty** selects another completed mode. **Replay mission** prepares a fresh mission in that selected result's mode; it is not a playback of recorded inputs. Use the flight deck to choose another difficulty for a fresh attempt. Replay Theater separately verifies and plays a supplied recording, grants no rewards and never converts its mode.

Gentle reveals the same original PNG or procedural scene and uses the same theme celebration. **Reduced effects** remains independent. An art-only pack update can refresh both modes' pictures without rewriting their gameplay records. Missing or changed campaign content leaves unmatched records archived; the app must not guess their mode from an ID prefix. A grouped card does not remove stored rows or increase existing profile/gallery budgets.

## Saves, transfers and long flights

A saved attempt keeps its exact execution campaign, level revisions, roster, steering and recorded commands. **Load** verifies that context, restores it paused and clears held input; the saved mode controls this attempt even when the next-attempt preference differs. Export preserves those actual bytes. Ready's **Export saved attempt** remains read-only; an unfinished current attempt has precedence, and an exhausted current recorder must not silently substitute an older saved flight. See [Library and packs](library-and-packs.md) for copy/download-request, cancellation and local versus portable limits.

When a pack is missing, replay-only rescue export can preserve the unfinished recording. Loading still requires the exact original campaign and roster from which its mode can be reconstructed. A similarly named pack, a different physics revision or an invalid supplied context cannot downgrade that check. Complete backup and Undo retain both mode branches and the preference through their existing transaction rules; included pack content must actually resolve the saved flight. Export a backup before replacing data you want to keep.

Three limits have different effects:

| Existing boundary                                          | Player consequence                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **216,000 recorded fixed ticks: 30 minutes of simulation** | The recorder has a finite lifetime, with its existing byte/segment bounds as well. Once discarded, continued play cannot produce a recoverable current-attempt save or replay. A shorter recording may also hit a data limit.     |
| **7,200 seconds: two hours of simulation**                 | Progress, pictures and score validators reject a completion beyond this time. The current winning picture can open, but that completion cannot be retained in the collection. The result must say the collection was not updated. |
| **1,000 score rows and other existing library budgets**    | Ordinary bounded retention and capacity errors still apply across modes; grouping pictures changes presentation, not storage capacity.                                                                                            |

The recorder and collection boundaries are separate. A win after recorder exhaustion can still update local collection data within its limits; a later out-of-budget win cannot. Exporting the library preserves what it already contains, not an unretained completion or discarded recording. Pause when taking a break. These are simulation-time bounds, not promises about indefinite wall-clock storage; browser eviction and session-only ownership retain their existing limitations. No timer is clipped and no new punitive mission deadline is introduced.

## Maintain exact contexts

[campaign-difficulty.mjs](../game/campaign-difficulty.mjs) owns the strict `standard`/`gentle` enum and `gentle.v1` transformation. `createDifficultyContext(baseCampaign, mode = 'standard')` returns a frozen owned `{mode, policyVersion, baseCampaignKey, campaign, campaignKey}`. Standard is an exact owned copy, with no additional level defaults or changed identities. Gentle uses the validated original campaign and its actual filtered roster; it derives a campaign identity and per-level revisions from the original key and policy. The map IDs and display names remain the original ones.

The finite Gentle transformation uses existing normalized core fields:

| Field                                               | `gentle.v1` policy                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Starting lives                                      | `max(original, 5)`                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Mission deadline, cut deadline, maximum trail cells | `0`, their existing unlimited values                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Bouncer velocity components; border-patrol speed    | Multiply by `0.6`; absent patrol speed uses the existing default `4` first                                                                                                                                                      |
| Legacy lane-boss timing                             | With existing defaults `warning=1.5`, `active=0.7`, `period=6`: new warning is `min(10, 2*warning)`; new rest is `min(60-newWarning-active, 2*(period-warning-active))`; new period is their sum with unchanged active duration |
| Sentinel timing                                     | Double shielded warning/rest and exposed warning/open ticks, each capped at the existing 7,200-tick field limit                                                                                                                 |

Coverage, objectives, topology, actor geometry/count, seed, supplies, hangars, equipment, recovery, score formula, medal thresholds and other encounter timing remain unchanged. The ordinary level-v1/core-v2/replay-v3 and staged level-v2/core-v3/replay-v4 pairs retain their existing engines and checkpoint formats. Slower hazards do not alter `FIXED_DT`, controls, presentation clocks, music or reduced-effects policy. Revalidate the derived recipe; never mutate a live run or add a difficulty state flag to the core.

Treat **`gentle.v1` as immutable for retained attempts**. Changed tuning needs a separately versioned derivation and support for reconstructing older saves; it must not silently reinterpret an existing key. Reuse [createExecutionCatalog](../game/campaign-contexts.mjs) for trusted resolved wrappers. Its frozen entries carry `campaign`, `baseCampaign`, `baseCampaignKey`, `difficulty`, `policyVersion` and `executionKey`, while preserving visual metadata. `find(executionKey)` and `select(baseCampaignKey, mode)` resolve exact entries. A key claimed by different base/mode owners rejects even if the execution data is identical; do not install a derived campaign as a second authored owner to bypass this check. Same-owner duplicates retain the first wrapper. Pack/Level selectors continue to list authored content once.

[difficultyAccess](../game/difficulty-access.mjs) produces a separate frozen access projection from the two exact progress branches; it does not fabricate award-shaped Standard records. Ignore undeclared map IDs and unrelated/archived contexts. Cache whole-campaign derivation outside per-frame UI refresh. `newDifficultyAppearanceBodies` belongs around a real eligible before/after completion; imports, reloads, gallery visits and Undo do not manufacture unlock notices. The trusted `expandDifficultyCampaigns` backup hook applies the same ownership checks to original campaigns plus included-pack originals before session resolution.

`preferences.campaignDifficulty` is additive within library v2: only omission migrates to Standard; present invalid values reject. Adopt the actual merged value, preserve ordinary session-only/Undo behavior and keep next-attempt preferences distinct from restored execution context. Old strict profile readers may reject new exports even though core, replay, session and pack formats did not change. This is forward transfer compatibility, not universal backward import support.

## Verify the player paths

Use the [five workflow prompts](../authoring/prompts/round-30-gentle-difficulty.md) and [Runtime Maintainer](../authoring/skills/xonix-runtime-maintainer/SKILL.md). Focused helper, access, registry, library and backup tests must preserve frozen Standard identities and use actual legal wins, recordings and restores for both steering modes and core/replay pairs. Test conflicting contexts, wrong supplied campaigns, both retention limits, mixed-mode cap/merge behavior and raw suspended-slot preservation during next-attempt changes. Keep old proof bytes unchanged.

Browser acceptance remains separate: use actual native/controller UI for current/next mode, explicit Retry, live-cut and recovery Load, grouped picture/replay selection, four theme celebrations and Reduced effects. Inspect readable mode/reset text and reachable controls at actual 320×640, 844×390 and 1280×720 CSS sizes, accepting necessary vertical scrolling. Practice previews cannot establish campaign awards or persistent saves. Software input and viewport simulation do not certify hardware, beginner comprehension or difficulty balance.

The rationale draws on [Microsoft XAG 108](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/108), rechecked on 12 September 2026: difficulty options should reduce barriers to the same content, explain their effects respectfully and support saving. That guidance also recommends a wider range of presets, individual adjustments and changing difficulty without losing in-progress play. This bounded two-preset, fresh-attempt design does not claim full conformance or measured accessibility outcomes.
