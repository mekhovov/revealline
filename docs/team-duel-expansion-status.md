## Automated gameplay evidence

- **Team: 576 complete runs** = 96 missions × three difficulties × two support-role assignments. Each pilot closes a cut; rehearsed routes lose no lives. This exchanges Interceptor/Disruptor roles, not spawn geometry or human participants.
- **Duel: 1,296 complete runs** = 72 missions × three difficulties × two steering policies × three outcomes: equal-input draw, seat 1 win and seat 2 win. The 432 configuration fixtures bind authored/tuned identities, options, ticks and native-state hashes. Both boards are independent, and equal-input states agree. Pause/resume preserves the final checkpoint; a fresh rematch reproduces the initial state.
- Team terminal recording export/replay verifies the new environment observation contract and rejects tampering. Historical contracts stay unchanged.
- Final focused suites passed: Team/recording regressions 22/22; compatibility 34/34; lifecycle/portability 15/15; Studio/content integration 35/35; Duel route/outcome suite 7/7. These suites overlap; their counts must not be added as unique cases.
- Route fixtures are retained under `game/test/fixtures/multiplayer-expansion/`. Environment behavior tests are in `game/test/coop-expedition-environment.test.mjs`. Run the focused suites directly with Node; full-repository tests were not claimed.
- The audit finds **168 distinct exact topologies**, including rotation/reflection comparisons, and distinct decision briefs. The [near-geometry review](research/multiplayer-near-geometry-review.json) records the most similar occupied-cell pairs and their mechanical differences. This does not establish perceived variety or enjoyment.

Team rehearsed completion times range from 4.4 to 86.8 seconds, with medians around 35–43 seconds depending on difficulty. Duel rehearsals range from 5.3 to 108.9 seconds, with configuration medians around 25–42 seconds. The plan's 2–5 minute Team and 45–150 second Duel targets were human pacing hypotheses, not measured outcomes. Fast known routes do not justify adding artificial timers or claiming those targets were met. Human-pair assessment of challenge, recovery, idle time and replay interest is skipped at the user’s explicit request; automated runs are not relabeled as human evidence.
