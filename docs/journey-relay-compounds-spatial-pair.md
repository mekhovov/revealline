# Journey spatial successors: Relay compounds pair

Status: candidate-only successor revisions; balance review pending.

This eighth copy-on-write batch changes only **Three compounds** and **Spiral stores**. It preserves mission IDs, names, reveal pictures, objectives, relay links and gates, policy/catalog identities, coverage targets, modes, campaign order and every earlier edition. It does not enroll these candidates in the default Journey or change a version or release.

## Route decisions

| Mission         | First viable approach                                                                                                      | Contrasting viable approach                                                                                       | Capture consequence                                                                                                            |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Three compounds | Ascend directly from the central landing, enclose `upper-relay`, and open `west-link` before enlarging the roamer network. | Cross the clear western channel first and establish an independent reserve before choosing a relay.               | Each relay opens only its authored connector. The unlinked outer compound remains a separate refuge until deliberately joined. |
| Spiral stores   | Cross the western mid-store opening, capture `west-relay`, and open the upper shortcut.                                    | Descend one safe step and cross the opposing eastern opening to capture `east-relay` and open the lower shortcut. | Each opposing broken spiral has a different timing window; its gate shortens only that side's later return.                    |

Walls block movement and never close a cut. Reclaimed foundations remain the authored return network. Slow terrain affects the player only while unclaimed. The ordinary enemy-seeded fill rule and existing actor domains are unchanged.

## Spatial vocabulary

Three compounds uses three visually separate archive yards around a central control pad. The asymmetric noise beds make the outer approaches materially unequal while leaving a clear deliberate route to each return.

Spiral stores replaces an unrestricted interior with two opposing broken-wall coils. Their openings are aligned to the existing relay objectives, so the player chooses a readable crossing window rather than tracing or mistaking the wall shape for safe ground. The geometry is original and functional, not copied from reference artwork.

## Automated evidence and limits

Focused checks cover copy-on-write preservation, geometry/topology diagnostics, initial retained regions, idle-opening safety, and two distinct first returns for every Gentle/Standard/Expert and immediate/grid-center combination. Signature-route checks capture the intended relay, open only its matching gate and verify the route closes without damage under the shared runtime resolver.

These checks do **not** prove full clears, final balance, enjoyment, physical keyboard/controller/touch behavior, small-screen legibility or human willingness to retry. Existing whole-Journey, historical and release acceptance remains outside this candidate PR.
