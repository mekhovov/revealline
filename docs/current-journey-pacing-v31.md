# Current Journey pacing and spatial-variety checkpoint

This checkpoint audits the exact default Solo/Versus source behind `whole-spatial-v25`. It is a
read-only preparation item for the remaining whole-Journey balance work; it does not change a
mission, publish a new edition or claim that automated counts establish fun.

## Exact-current inventory

- 91 missions exist in the current source: 71 core-route missions and 20 optional/preserved
  entries.
- The 71 core missions span 13 campaigns and authored challenge bands 1 through 12 without a band
  reset or a jump greater than one.
- Standard fresh attempts resolve the same 8.84-cell/second craft speed throughout the route.
- Every mission after the opening pack starts with at least three enabled field enemies after the
  normal runtime tuning path.
- No core mission currently uses a failing countdown, keeping timed content below the 15% ceiling.
- 44 of 71 core missions have no wall rectangles; 41 have no slow or lethal terrain; 21 missions
  after the opening pack have neither. These counts identify repeated spatial vocabulary, not
  automatic defects.

The reusable `JourneySpatialVarietyInspectionV1` report records map revision and geometry identity,
authored band and route decision, wall/foundation/terrain/gate/directional-zone counts, terrain
`kind`, runtime-prepared player speed and starting actor counts. It uses the same gameplay-tuning
adapter as a fresh attempt and applies tuning exactly once.
