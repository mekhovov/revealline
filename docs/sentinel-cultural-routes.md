## Cultural source boundaries

- The [Museum Fund carved chest record 241663](https://museum.mincult.gov.ua/collections/241663)
  documents a divided triangular lid, three-part front and geometric/floral carving. First relay
  borrows only tripartite, divided-ridge and ring-and-triangle organization.
- The [Museum Fund hand-woven runner record 235942](https://museum.mincult.gov.ua/collections/235942)
  documents five zones separated by bands, a concentric central rhomboid and mirrored outer zones.
  Relay perimeter borrows only five-zone, central-focus and mirrored-outer organization.
- The [Ivan Honchar Museum carved shelf record КН-2869](https://honchar.org.ua/collections/detail/1826)
  documents trapezoidal spacing and a symmetric centre balanced by paired side details. Crown
  audience borrows only trapezoidal, central-focus and paired-side organization.

No source object, pattern chart, carving, motif, palette, meaning or coordinates are copied. These
official museum records provide broad compositional vocabulary; they do not prove gameplay balance
or universal symbolism.
