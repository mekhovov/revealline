## Current engineering evidence

- The prior 36-mission batch passed **388 company/edition regression tests** and **216 deterministic route witnesses**. Candidate CI for commit `03084b22d` passed (GitHub Actions run `36381662552`). This does not qualify later changes.
- Expansion route checks cover three difficulties and two steering modes. **648/648 witnesses passed** after final expert-map adjustments, including no life loss and public replay agreement (79.1 seconds for the complete replay suite). They prove legal objective completion, not human pacing or enjoyment.
- Rich media passed **140 focused compatibility checks**; campaign-key descriptor/Studio/retention support passed **50 checks**. Twelve optional-practice tests cover all twelve drills, bounded replay, input neutralization, reproducible packaging and cache isolation.
- Exact old discovery presentations are retained for all four new editions. Adding permitted campaigns can restore an earlier subset; removing an allowed campaign, changing audience/brand/modes or substituting pinned assets is still rejected.
- Studio-preview wins, Retry and save attempts leave player storage unchanged. Preview sessions use temporary storage, open no player databases and acquire no player writer locks. Real browser inspection exercised two-card comparison and wrong/correct causal feedback in the aircraft atlas preview.
- Reward persistence checks cover timeouts, quota failures, corrupt saves, writer leases, exact-edition transfers and repeated open/close cycles. Finale checks remain locked with any required win missing. Audio/video requires explicit Play, exact source/caption/transcript hashes and existing mute/volume ownership; closing and blur release or pause playback.
- The company content generator currently emits **181 selected authoring exports**. New art uses JPEG/WebP/PNG support under unchanged image budgets. Selected derivatives carry their exact hash; original generation masters remain outside public packages.
- Earlier pilot builds were compiled twice with matching ZIP bytes: Coupa adventure approximately 25.1 MB; DroneAid workshop approximately 25.8 MB. Reproducible local ZIP caches were removed to recover disk space; manifests remain. Current editions need fresh qualification.
- Full local repository validation is limited by historical artwork intentionally absent from this sparse checkout (`authoring/library/fpv-role-presentations/originals` and one legacy orchard image). Full-checkout CI remains required; absent inputs are not a passing check.

No current evidence establishes the p95 frame-time target, physical-device behaviour, learning effectiveness, full art completion or publication. Human/device evidence remains deferred.
