One DRY framework covers Solo, Snake Solo, SIM and FPV Survival at every authored standard setup, including every allowed SIM flight mode. Complete genuine performances, current-mode entry after 60 eligible idle seconds, balanced random **Watch all modes**, isolated practice and unchanged player persistence/preferences remain required.

Team/Versus, Raid, prototypes, free flight, editors, custom combinations and new personal recordings outside Solo are excluded. Existing Solo recordings, bots, improvisation, artwork access and settings remain compatible. Standalone SIM retains ordinary flight and its existing size limit; shared demo actions are hidden where the shared corpus is absent. Demo entry never installs missing packages.

The user confirmed ordinary Solo eligibility: installed locked missions use isolated tick-0 practice; uninstalled missions open the normal chooser; eligible installed missions reach Ready through the departure guard. Watching and takeover cover every authored mission. Normal SIM launch remains disarmed. Human/device review was not waived; reviewer timing is unknown and automated work continues independently.
