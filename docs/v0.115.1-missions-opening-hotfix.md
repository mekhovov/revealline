## Correction

Solo and Versus retain the input-turn ownership established when the player
opens Missions. Before the installed-card catalogue performs its final owned
asynchronous refresh, each host snapshots the still-current opener and removes
the temporary focus observer while retaining the key, pointer, and click
observers until that refresh finishes. The existing revision, foreground,
dialog, run, pause, and gameplay-context checks also reject a genuinely newer
navigation or replaced game state after that refresh.

This prevents internal catalogue reconciliation from invalidating an otherwise
current Missions request. It does not weaken mission identity, installed-pack
validation, leave confirmation, or mission-launch authority, and it does not
change gameplay content or presentation assets.
