# Discovery curriculum: first six campaigns

This delivery implements six campaigns and 36 missions of the planned 18 campaigns and 108 missions. It does not implement the remaining 72 missions or the separate 12-drill simulator. Every mission has a distinct authored map and an English/Ukrainian discovery. Each finale explicitly requires all six exact mission wins. Discoveries contain optional reflection prompts; they are not verified Apply tasks or professional qualifications.

The second Social Drone UA and Victory Drones campaigns introduce the existing frontier patrol and reclaimed-ground roamer with distinct campaign presentation recipes. They preserve every first-slice mission, reward requirement and gameplay identity. Edition campaign lists and theme registries derive from the curriculum catalogue.

Four shared home illustrations are currently reused as mission backgrounds and reward pictures. They are four compositions, not 36 distinct mission images. Per-mission compositions, separate teaser artwork, richer interactive component/textile exhibits and recorded original music remain production work. Current campaign soundtrack presets use the existing synthesizer. The Ukraine finale additionally includes one exact public-domain Met painting with a source-comparison explanation. Honchar photographs remain source links because their individual licence is CC BY-NC.
