# Discovery curriculum: eighteen campaigns

This delivery authors all 18 planned campaigns and 108 missions. The separate 12-drill practice package and qualification status are tracked in the shared phase ledger. Every mission has a distinct authored map and an English/Ukrainian discovery. Each finale explicitly requires all six exact mission wins. Discoveries contain optional reflection prompts; they are not verified Apply tasks or professional qualifications.

The second Social Drone UA and Victory Drones campaigns introduce the existing frontier patrol and reclaimed-ground roamer with distinct campaign presentation recipes. The next two Ukraine campaigns continue those rules and introduce the existing territory eroder. The final three Ukraine campaigns use the existing warning actors, numbered relay connectors, directional fields and shield-relay finale. They preserve every first-slice mission, reward requirement and gameplay identity. Edition campaign lists and theme registries derive from the curriculum catalogue.

Four home scenes and reviewed campaign key illustrations establish the edition and campaign worlds. Fifteen missions currently have distinct original compositions, including all six Threads Across Ukraine and all six Colour, Clay and Spring missions. The other 93 missions reuse their campaign scene while production continues; this is not 108 distinct mission artwork. All six campaigns in the first published 36-mission preview retain their exact earlier presentation and advance their art/reward promises to revision 2. The remaining per-mission compositions, separate teaser artwork, richer exhibits and recorded original music remain production work. Current campaign soundtrack presets use the existing synthesizer. The Ukraine finale additionally includes one exact public-domain Met painting with a source-comparison explanation. Honchar photographs remain source links because their individual licence is CC BY-NC. Six separately cleared FPV reference photographs show a historical quadrotor, a motor, a controller and soldering objects. They appear with bilingual context, creator attribution, licence links and exact byte pins; they are object-comparison rewards, not additional mission background compositions or a recommendation to copy the depicted setup.

Two worked finales now contain an earned interactive pilot using the registered inspect/compare recipe: six aircraft-role cards and three documented textile-object cards, each with two optional bilingual predictions and corrective feedback. The cards reuse the authored sources, include no unlicensed museum photographs, and create no Journey win, learning completion or mastery receipt. A complete exploded aircraft diagram, textile lighting control and specialist-reviewed application fixtures remain future production work.
