**Read the lock** and **Cross the afterglow** provide two controlled exposures before
terrain combinations. The original first landing closed only17ticks after the
warning; it was moved farther down so the real initial closure follows89ticks
of visible warning and still precedes firing. This is a measurable engine window,
not evidence that a human perceived it. **Cooling loop** combines the known eroder
with permanent return choices. **Windows in the wire** is an optional known-rule
Remix with lanes, a selective-impact carrier and a reclaimed-ground roamer.

Primary-source recheck: [Fortix2's developer listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/45450/Fortix_2/)
describes maze-like territory capture with turrets and monsters. The useful design
inference is to vary approach geometry and threat interactions, not simply speed.
[AirXonix's developer rules](https://www.axysoft.com/airxonix/) distinguish threats
in the field from mines on filled territory and describe contact bonuses. That
supports explicit movement-domain teaching; it does not justify importing its
universal timer or claiming a guaranteed retention effect.

Our lane emitter is an existing, explicitly authored Reveal Line behavior. It
locks a row/column, warns, fires and rests. It retains field territory and is not
disabled by enclosing it. Permanent relay gates remain a separate later capability;
no decorative marker pretends to disable this enemy. Objective-ordering variants
still require an implemented causal effect and separate cleanup validation.

Bands8–9 preserve the Phaseworks handoff. Two three-mission arcs introduce only
locked-lane attacks in the first arc; the second introduces no mandatory rule.
All seven have distinct geometry and two or three threat roles, shared measured
tiers, no countdown, no required objective or random bonus. Duration90–150seconds
is authored intent, not demonstrated human pacing. Optimized routes are not a
substitute for playtests, and every reference disposition remains non-final.

All candidates remain asset-free greyboxes, explicitly inspected/applied in Studio.
Full preset/control routes, optional goals, native readability, original artwork,
Team, human and release/Pages gates remain. Merge or cut candidates if actual
play cannot distinguish their decisions; no minimum shipped count applies.
