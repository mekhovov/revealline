[CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) and [4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) allow commercial redistribution but impose ShareAlike conditions on adaptations; this research does not decide the scope for the game.
