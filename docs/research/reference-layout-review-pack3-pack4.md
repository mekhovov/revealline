## Cross-pack conclusions

The useful recurring vocabulary is: open field, split field, nearby island,
interrupted lane, nested chamber, ring corridor, and terrain that can be neutralized.
Our campaigns should change the route decision and capture consequences within
that vocabulary. They should not recreate each screenshot's density or order.

Candidate comparisons for explicit merge/cut review: pack1/8 versus 1/11; pack2/5
versus 2/10 and 2/11; pack2/4 versus 4/6; pack3/2 versus 4/9; pack3/8 versus 4/8;
pack1/6 versus 3/5. These are review pairs, not final deletions or claims of identical
runtime behavior. Final decision follows greybox comparison and a written reason.

Minimum candidate acceptance: two legible initial route choices where intended,
a specific change caused by capture, no unexplained empty-region fill, no forced
new rule in a finale, and no tedious quota remainder after the interesting part.
Published evidence must identify mode, preset, controls and whether observation
was automated, native browser, or human play; none substitutes for the others.
