The [JSON ledger](xposed-adaptation-ledger.json) stores original hashes, dimensions, evidence limits, target phases and acceptance requirements and empty implementation-evidence fields. The [production plan](../cross-mode-production-review.md) owns phase ordering and release gates.

All source files were visually inspected at full-frame scale; large frames were displayed at reduced resolution. The three AVIF files were converted locally to PNG for viewing after the image viewer rejected AVIF; their recorded hashes identify the unchanged AVIF originals. No native-size sprite, animation-timing, collision or input qualification is implied. Black concealed regions do not establish missing artwork. Reference images remain research material, not production assets.

## Authoring decisions

- Treat safe ground, walls, slow terrain and lethal terrain as different roles. Only pre-existing safe ground closes a cut. Five Masts therefore uses safe platforms around separately blocked foundations. Validate cell geometry before final artwork.
- Solo “drifter” resolves to an ordinary bouncer; a warned “hunter” uses a qualified existing Classic enemy-pressure policy on a bouncer. Team retains its own actor semantics. Never introduce a new actor type by renaming a sprite.
- Preserve the source layout motif while simplifying density: rows, chambers, openings, islands and alternate approaches matter more than reproducing every small tile. Keep cardinal movement and actual-size clearance.
- First Contact teaches short capture, safe return ground, warned enemies, blocking walls and then lethal terrain as distinct lessons. Later campaigns revisit familiar skills before combining them. Labels below describe intended learning, not measured difficulty.
- Optional auxiliary configurations wait for P09 qualification. Retain approved limits, no shooters in the first three positions, at most two shooter missions per campaign and complete encounters-off alternatives.
- Finales name the next campaign; Home Signal ends the FPV edition with Collection and Browse rather than automatic wrap or a forced download.

Source contracts: [core ground/capture rules](https://github.com/mekhovov/revealline/blob/c93019a344f6f4c6ac03940c77ea09c81122d911/game/core/README.md) and [existing pressure policy](https://github.com/mekhovov/revealline/blob/c93019a344f6f4c6ac03940c77ea09c81122d911/docs/enemy-pressure.md). These source audits are separate from observing the supplied game screenshots.
