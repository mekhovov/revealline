# Difficulty design research addendum

20 September 2026. Preparation only; the approved three presets and phase scope
remain unchanged. These recommendations do not themselves change runtime rules
or certify a release.

Microsoft's [Xbox Accessibility Guideline 108](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/gaming/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/108),
retrieved September 20, treats experienced difficulty as a relationship between
player ability and different gameplay demands. It recommends descriptive presets,
adjustable challenge dimensions and pause support in local modes.

Application to our approved plan (design inference, not an enjoyment guarantee):

- Keep planning, execution, density, time, mechanic load and coordination as separate
  Studio ratings. One aggregate band must not hide a demanding precision route.
- Explain Gentle/Standard/Expert changes, preserving the selected preset between
  missions. Avoid silently changing speed to compensate for a player's failures.
- Visual clarity, captions and reduced effects are preferences, not penalties or
  reasons to withhold a clear. Simulation-changing assists need explicit identity.
- The optional chooser and two-action Skip remain available when a particular
  challenge is a poor fit. No stars or purchases are required to try another level.
- Pause must remain usable in Solo and both couch modes. Fast continuation never
  removes the player's ability to stop.

The guideline suggests a wider preset range than this plan. That suggestion alone
does not justify adding a fourth preset or expanding the current scope.

A search found primary Nintendo Iwata Asks interviews about Super Mario 3D Land.
Direct reads of pages 3 and 4 both timed out. No detailed teaching-structure claim
from those unavailable pages is adopted here, and no third-party summary substitutes
for the original. The already-approved teach/practice/combine arc remains a design
requirement, not newly verified Nintendo behavior.
