# Round 40 — Readable pressure and pixel presentation

Inspected 13 September 2026. Recommendation: make enemies demand route decisions through readable, committed pursuit and complementary terrain roles. Keep the player, unfinished cut and imminent danger clearer than the effects surrounding them. Higher speed alone is not evidence of smarter or more enjoyable play.

This is a research and acceptance brief, not an implementation or playtest report. The new pursuit behavior described below is our extension. No inspected XPOSED source establishes adaptive AI.

## Pressure that creates decisions

The proposed implementation uses a bounded optional pressure policy on selected enemies: a visible warning, a target snapshot, a committed route, then a cooldown. A trail pursuer threatens an exposed segment; an interceptor threatens a projected destination. Neither should silently retarget every frame or cross blocked cells. Preserve the existing collision footprint, topology rules and movement speed unless a new authored edition explicitly changes them. Snapshot/restore must retain the same commitment, and closure/recovery must cancel it according to the declared rules. These are engineering recommendations, not claims about the reference engine.

Prioritize this three-mission teaching sequence when authoring the new edition:

1. **Read and evade:** one conspicuous pressure actor plus ordinary field motion. A greedy long cut should expose a readable interception opportunity; a shorter reconnect and an alternate route should provide counterplay. Keep the first warning distinguishable before adding competing effects.
2. **Choose an exit:** barriers and a contour patrol make two exits meaningfully different. Add an interceptor whose locked target can be baited. Place one slow-terrain crossing or useful pickup so route choice has a visible tradeoff.
3. **Defend what was earned:** combine pressure with a claimed-ground rover or eroder. Threaten the return path and secured frontier without making every safe cell dangerous simultaneously. Keep at least two demonstrated solutions; avoid compulsory idle waits or invisible narrow corridors.

Measure warning duration, committed speed, target distance and time to contact in simulation units. Tune the enemy/player speed ratio rather than increasing both and cancelling the pressure change. A proposed warning duration is a value to test with players, not an inferred XPOSED timing. Retain Gentle as a separately verified choice. Microsoft's [difficulty guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/108) supports adjusting different sources of difficulty and describing their effects clearly; it does not establish that our existing two presets satisfy every accessibility recommendation.

## One visual language for every theme

The viewed [FPV body concept](../concepts/round-07-fpv-body.png) has a clear central axis and separated arms. The [terrain comparison](../concepts/round-07-terrain-balance.png) supports a hybrid: compact functional patterns with selected larger props. The supplied [top-side drone photograph](drones-examples/drone-example-skin.png) and [underside photograph](drones-examples/drone_sign.png) help distinguish camera/body direction and negative space around arms. They are reference photographs, not ready sprites; no model, ownership, real-world capability or redistribution right is inferred. Do not copy their lettering or identifiable markings into game artwork.

| Element                  | Recommended readable treatment                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Player                   | Bright stable center and obvious heading. Propeller animation, shadow and exhaust are decoration; they must not imply a larger collision footprint or cover the next turn. |
| Unfinished cut           | A continuous, high-contrast path with an unmistakable leading point. Settled territory uses a quieter, thinner contour.                                                    |
| Ordinary enemies         | Role-specific silhouettes and short, discontinuous motion traces. Their decorative tails must not resemble the dangerous player cut.                                       |
| Pressure actor           | Warning brackets and a target cue; a distinct committed heading; a separate cooldown state. Shape/orientation remain readable without color or sound.                      |
| Travelling line impact   | Draw the actual front positions supplied by the core. Visual sparks must not arrive ahead of the damaging front or keep travelling after cancellation.                     |
| Erosion / capture / loss | Show the lasting topology change, then a local effect. Mark loss and respawn separately. Keep the camera stable and the player/head visible.                               |
| Reward                   | Unobstructed artwork, then legible medals/score and direct actions. A deliberate advance may finish the animation without also activating the next action.                 |

Use a consistent source-pixel grid and stable sprite pivots; nearest-neighbor scaling avoids blurred pixel art. Canvas exposes this through [`imageSmoothingEnabled`](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/CanvasRenderingContext2D/imageSmoothingEnabled). Unity's [Pixel Perfect documentation](https://docs.unity3d.com/Packages/com.unity.2d.pixel-perfect@5.1/manual/index.html) likewise emphasizes consistent pixel density and crisp presentation; this is supporting art guidance, not a proposal to replace our renderer. Do not quantize authoritative physics to make decoration look crisp.

## Acceptance before calling it harder, clearer or finished

- **Pressure and counterplay:** run the old greedy straight-cut and edge-following probes against the new edition and record actual coverage, lives, warnings, contacts and route length. Verify legal wins on all three maps under both turn policies and both difficulties. Add an alternative escape route for each new pressure interaction. A solver win establishes reachability, not fun; a failed probe alone does not establish fair difficulty.
- **Ordinary continuation:** retain the simple first Down → capture → long stationary interval regression, with pause/save/load before and after closure. Replay equality, simultaneous events, freeze, recovery and cancellation need coverage alongside curated winning routes. The earlier R4 failure after a successful capture is the reason this mundane path matters; see [domain recovery tests](../../game/test/classic-domain-recovery.test.mjs).
- **Clarity at play size:** inspect compact and detailed actor treatments at actual supported phone/desktop sizes, over both bright and dark revealed art. Target at least 4.5:1 for ordinary important text and evaluate the least favorable background, following [XAG 102](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/102). This document contains no measured compliance result.
- **Multiple cues:** identify actor roles, lethal terrain, warning/commit/cooldown and impact fronts with shape or pattern as well as color. Sound and haptics supplement visible information; they cannot carry an essential warning alone. [XAG 103](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/103)
- **Reduced effects and pause:** remove decorative shake, blur, large zooms and excess particles while retaining authoritative danger positions and clear state transitions. Pause must not advance warnings or damage. [XAG 117](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/117) and [Apple's Reduced Motion criteria](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-accessibility/reduced-motion-evaluation-criteria) support alternatives to distracting motion; neither requires erasing all useful animation.
- **Explain the objective:** show current/target coverage and the next progression action. Provide an on-demand interactive example of a pressure warning and escape, rather than relying only on a controls diagram. [XAG 109](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/109)
- **Human judgment:** ask players to identify the threatening actor, explain a loss and demonstrate one intentional escape. Record confusion, response time and perceived pressure separately from completion. Listen to the actual mixed music/effects on target devices before claiming an audio improvement; that audit remains outstanding here.

All numeric tuning, new threat behavior and presentation treatments above are proposals for our original game. Existing reference images remain evidence only. No reference assets, maps or music were imported into production by this work.
