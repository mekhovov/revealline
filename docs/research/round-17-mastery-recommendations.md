# Round 17 — optional mastery feedback without blocking the picture

Primary sources accessed **12 September 2026**. This is a narrow follow-up to [Round 16 progression research](round-16-progression-references.md), applying platform guidance to the first Steady Signal increment. It recommends clear optional goals, restrained feedback and a stable completed picture. It is not evidence that achievements cause retention or that the game has passed accessibility certification.

The [observer](../mastery-observer-contract.md), [record metadata](../mastery-records-contract.md) and [frozen compatibility baseline](../mastery-compatibility-baseline.md) now exist in source. Replay verification, profile migration and interface integration are being added separately. The recommendations below are acceptance targets, not a claim that every interface path was observed. Public documentation text was retrieved; no new gameplay video, audio or reference artwork was inspected or copied in this pass.

## One concrete optional-goal flow

**Before flight:** keep the concise required mission summary and Start/Continue visible. Under an explicitly optional label, explain: **Close one cut through eight distinct active interference cells with signal-resistant equipment, then finish without losing a life.** In this definition, the credited cells are those sampled during that live cut; visiting them on safe ground or combining separate short cuts does not qualify. Do not add this full explanation to every ready overlay if it buries the primary action. The full authored brief remains available in the flight deck.

**During flight and pause:** separate the best completed cut from the current unfinished cut. Suitable original copy is “Best closed resistant cut: 6 / 8 cells” and, while relevant, “Current cut: 8 cells — return to safety to complete it.” Show the clean-run condition separately. An aborted cut loses its pending credit; a life loss makes this attempt ineligible for the clean-win condition while leaving the ordinary mission playable. This distinction follows the actual [observer contract](../mastery-observer-contract.md), not an inferred rule from another game.

Update detailed progress when it is useful to read. A discrete notification after a qualifying closure can be helpful; per-cell sound, repeated popups and a live announcement every frame are unnecessary. The criterion remains reviewable if the player missed a transient cue. Do not imply the optional goal has been earned merely because the live preview meets its threshold.

**After a win:** show the completed picture and ordinary result promptly. Optional replay reconstruction may display “Checking your equipment goal…” without disabling collection, Next or leaving. If verification finishes after the player moves on, update the appropriate stored detail and status without reopening the result or moving focus. An aborted or rejected check must never produce the success fanfare.

When a verified record is retained only in the current session, say so and keep an export route available. A practice qualification is a **practice result**, not an earned profile record. Imported local records can be displayed as portable metadata; they are not newly verified attempts or cryptographic certificates. The host's practice eligibility, profile generation and replacement guards remain necessary alongside replay verification.

**Celebration and exit:** fit a small seal accent into the existing picture celebration, rather than appending another compulsory sequence. Its duration is a project presentation decision, not a number established by these sources. Keep Skip and reduced effects effective, let the scene settle, and retain the picture afterward. A calm collection action is a complete ending to the session. Another route or a harder optional challenge can remain a secondary invitation; there is no reward expiry, lost streak, automatic difficulty escalation or repeated claim button.

## Bounded acceptance checks

| Check                                 | Expected result                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Ordinary win without mastery          | A legal Interceptor win still earns the same picture, score, ordinary medals and progression. The optional seal does not block the next accessible map.                                                                                     |
| Unfinished credit                     | Eight pending cells followed by recovery do not become closed-cut credit. Two separate four-cell cuts remain insufficient. The explanation matches the observer.                                                                            |
| Restored live cut                     | Reconstruct the complete recorded prefix in both steering modes. Pause text distinguishes restored pending cells from completed credit; no imported preview totals are trusted.                                                             |
| Delayed verification                  | Continue or move focus while a check runs. Completion creates no focus jump, modal reopening or duplicate claim; import/Undo or an invalidated attempt cannot receive a late award.                                                         |
| Phone, controller and reduced effects | At a normal phone width, the optional paragraph does not bury Start/Continue. All result actions remain reachable with the current controller map. Muted/reduced-effects play shows the same meaningful text and seal shape.                |
| Assistive status feedback             | A screen reader receives a few complete changed-state phrases, not ticking counts. Important results remain visible for later review. Actual assistive-technology testing is still required; DOM attributes alone do not establish success. |
| Capacity or storage failure           | Ordinary pictures are preserved. An unsaved seal has a truthful status and an exportable in-memory record where retention succeeded; no successful-storage wording appears after a failed write.                                            |

The next playable increment should remain **one optional Steady Signal goal on the existing Copper Orchard map**, with these preview/result states and the existing compatibility proofs. Additional class seals and the staged boss remain separate increments in the [mastery plan](../round-15-mastery-plan.md). A short observed playtest should ask whether players can explain the goal, distinguish pending from completed credit and leave comfortably after the picture. That can reveal confusing feedback; it cannot establish an “addictive” or retention claim.
