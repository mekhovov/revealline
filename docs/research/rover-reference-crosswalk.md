# Rover Yard — original spatial adaptations

These six stills were visually reinspected and their SHA-256 hashes rechecked
on 20 September 2026 against the preserved observation ledger. They establish
visible geometry, not movement, collision, capture or warning semantics. The
game's explicit actor catalogue remains authoritative. No source artwork or
coordinates are imported. All dispositions remain provisional greybox redesigns,
not final shipped acceptance.

| Reference | Observed spatial idea | Original adaptation and reason |
|---|---|---|
| Pack1 level8 | Alternating vertical strips, interrupted cyan paths and a central ring | Between the rows uses three unequal broad landings, one slow link and separate frontier/interior threat routes; removes ornamental partitions that would obscure the return decision. |
| Pack1 level10 | Interior patterns inside a perimeter, five red cross-shaped regions | Split berths replaces the repeated crosses with three offset platforms; joining return networks also expands a reclaimed roamer's movement domain. |
| Pack2 level3 | Split heart-like enclosure with stepped islands | Stepped return uses an original asymmetrical S foundation, without a heart silhouette; upper and lower return choices face the same warned roamer. |
| Pack2 level4 | Stepped quadrants with dense hazard clusters | Broken yard uses one broad branching foundation, two outer platforms and a single marked pocket. This keeps escape ordering readable instead of copying dense decoration. |
| Pack3 level2 | Eight chamber-like regions around internal return geometry | Sorting yard reduces this to two occupied chambers and unequal stubs, avoiding eight repetitive cleanup tasks. Each side can activate a separate roamer. |
| Pack4 level6 | Broken horizontal dividing geometry | Open frequency combines three broken spine sections with separate vertical landings; reconnecting the board is an optional mastery route, not a new capture exception. |

Wake the yard is an additional original introductory map, not attributed to a
numbered reference. Two three-mission arcs first expose and practice reclaimed
roamers, then combine established terrain/frontier decisions. The Remix adds no
new mandatory rule. All maps keep measured actor tiers, fixed player physics,
untimed completion and the shared capture contract.

## Rejected teaching gap

The first unpublished Stepped return and Broken yard versions admitted legal
Standard/immediate clears before the roamer was active. Their inputs, original
identities and checkpoints are retained as negative design evidence. Revision2
starts the same roamer on a broad foundation, distant from spawn, with the
existing120actor-tick warning; no surprise damage, extra quota or speed increase
was added. All presets retain five seconds of no-input spawn decision space.

Sorting yard's four-cut Standard route takes13.25simulation seconds, activates
both roamers and earns both chambers. That is an omniscient lower-bound route,
not evidence of a human clear duration or enjoyment. It remains a pacing review
candidate; no arbitrary delay or inflated quota was added to manufacture duration.

Final art, readable warning/counterplay in native play, optional-goal alternatives,
broader timing/seed sampling, human comprehension and final dispositions remain
separate gates. Studio inspection is not publication or campaign promotion.
