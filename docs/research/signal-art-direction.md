# Signal Gardens — original reveal direction

Status: production brief, not generated artwork, a collision map, or visual approval.
No placeholder is an immutable AssetRevision. The existing greyboxes stay labelled
as such until original pixels are generated, inspected and pinned.

## Campaign identity

A botanical radio observatory after rain: slate-blue ceramic, warm copper,
translucent seed vessels, reed gardens and distant quiet antenna structures.
Use a composed illustrated environment rather than an overhead board diagram.
This moves beyond Border Bloom's limestone/fern gardens without changing the
functional meaning of any gameplay symbol. Avoid bright, narrow neon lines,
tiny repeated dots, warning crosses or moving-looking specks in reveal artwork.

Each composition has a legible large subject, a smaller discovery on another
side of the frame, and a quiet region that still reads when only a thin strip
has been earned. Revealed details are rewards, never collision or route promises.
No copied Xposed pictures, lettering, layouts, logos or characters.

The game board is 2:1. Generate a separate original 2:1 composition for each row;
inspect the actual result before specifying bytes, size or hash. Full-picture
victory viewing must not change earned coverage. Keep actor, trail and material
overlays separate from the picture. No image should contain game UI or text.

## Functional presentation and characters

Keep reclaimed ground, active trail, craft, field keepers, outer patrols and
frontier patrols recognizable across campaigns. Signal-specific materials may
change, but movement-domain silhouettes and warning timing must not. Preserve
the paired-dash slow symbol and framed-cross lethal symbol in reduced effects
and muted audio. Artwork is not evidence that these combinations pass contrast.

Guide or Engineer reactions should be optional short captions after an accepted
capture, not a prompt that blocks the next direction. A personality variant must
retain the factual terrain/capture explanation, not replace it with a joke.
No new actor behavior or mandatory mechanic is introduced by a visual skin.

## Asset acceptance sequence

1. Keep route decisions and greybox evidence attached to the actual mission ID.
2. Generate each original through the image-generation workflow only after the
   media-space guard is satisfied; do not substitute a programmatic fake asset.
3. Inspect the generated picture for distinct focal structure, unreadable text,
   accidental gameplay symbols and near-duplicate compositions.
4. Pin exact immutable bytes, SHA-256 and dimensions in the shared asset registry.
5. Inspect small partial reveals, broad captures and full-picture victory in
   Solo and paired-board layouts; inspect Team separately for Shared detour.
6. Verify low-motion, muted-audio and small-screen readability while enemies are
   present. Keep human visual/comprehension approval pending until observed.
7. Only then add the new campaign to the authored continuation route and rerun
   exact-source qualification, packaging, Pages and rollback gates.

This brief does not establish accessibility conformance, human enjoyment or
permission to silently publish generated content. Studio imports remain local,
inspectable and explicitly applied.
