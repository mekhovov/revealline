# Ukrainian source round — 25 September 2026

This research-only round adds six nonduplicate leads for M4. It does not approve,
download, publish or admit any recording. The comparison baseline is game main
`f54a6ecbdf329c7fd175d90ec258537d035c72e7` and soundtrack archive main
`275525e791d7f135e581f56c22100eb1bca16804`, whose catalogue contains 128
recordings.

The machine-readable evidence is in
[`ukrainian-source-round-2026-09-25.json`](./ukrainian-source-round-2026-09-25.json).
Titles and artists were normalized with NFKD case folding and compared with the
128-track catalogue plus current JSON audition holdings. All six returned zero
matches. The existing **Oi u luzi chervona kalyna**, **A v kryvoho tantsia** and
**Oi khodyt son kolo vikon** auditions therefore remain separate.

are not recording admission. All six remain excluded from Recording mode because
no source states the Content ID or automated gameplay-video claim status. A CC
licence permits gameplay-video use, but does not guarantee that a platform will
avoid automated claims.

## Rejected or held leads

- YouTube-only recordings remain excluded because availability does not grant a
  reusable recording file or public MP3 redistribution.
- Pixabay candidates retain their standalone-distribution hold.
- Kyle Misko arrangements retain the unresolved sampled-recording-provenance hold.
- Dmytro Ignatov's surfaced Hutsul/Ukrainian releases are all-rights-reserved.
  Purchase or streaming does not grant archive redistribution.
- Marcus Denight and Sergey Korzhenevskiy's **Kolyadka** is another Shchedryk
  arrangement, so it cannot count as a new composition.
- Ukrainian artist nationality and generic Ukrainian/folk tags do not establish
  Ukrainian repertoire.
