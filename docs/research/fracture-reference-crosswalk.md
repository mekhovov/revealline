## Repetition and cut review

Pack3/8 and4/8 initially risked becoming the same island exercise. The current
candidates differ in mandatory decisions: Island reserve teaches dependable
islands versus vulnerable earned connections, without objectives or roamers;
Staggered reserve orders two anchor captures around slow/lethal terrain and a
reclaimed-domain threat. The former's optional task joins all three foundations
and repairs eroded ground; the latter neutralizes both terrain pockets and banks
both anchors. They remain a human distinction/merge review pair. If players do
not perceive this difference, merge or cut one; geometry variation alone is not
acceptance evidence.

Five landings revision1 admitted a legal route that reached quota before either
anchor, leaving972simulation ticks of objective cleanup. The exact old input,
identity and checkpoint remain a negative replay fixture. Revision2 puts anchors
on upper/lower expansion routes. All42 qualified clear routes now obtain their
required anchors before their first quota crossing. This does not prove that
every possible player route avoids cleanup; broader human play remains necessary.
