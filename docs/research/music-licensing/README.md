Creator submissions—not website footers—identify recording licenses. [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) permits copying, adaptation and commercial redistribution. [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) adds attribution, license/source information and applicable modification notices, and prohibits additional restrictions on its freedoms. Neither establishes endorsement, sample-chain warranties or Content ID clearance. Credits remain attached to CC0 recordings too. These are not RevealLine originals.

The prior 24 works were revalidated at [Holizna](https://opengameart.org/content/retro-wave-collection), [Emma_MA](https://opengameart.org/content/megasong), [HydroGene](https://opengameart.org/content/determination-anime-style-music), [Vitalezzz / Silver Bullet](https://opengameart.org/content/silver-bullet), [Vitalezzz / Devoted Guard](https://opengameart.org/content/devoted-guard) and [3xBlast](https://opengameart.org/content/7-pop-punk-chiptune-tracks). The 3xBlast archive's old restriction and creator's dated public removal remain documented together. Retained audio matches the earlier Git objects.
