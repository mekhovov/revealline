The source screenshots establish visible shapes, not precise terrain semantics,
actor domains, fill rules, speed, collision tolerance or countdown behavior.
Our carrier behavior is the explicitly versioned Reveal Line contract. The
cross-band compiler initially rejected overlapping slow/lethal rectangles;
the greybox now splits slow bands around the lethal crossing instead of relying
on ambiguous material precedence. No runtime terrain rule was loosened.

Two additional original candidates, **A return in reserve** and **Two ways home**,
introduce the role with nearby alternative returns. **Close the circuit** is an
optional known-rule Remix. The two three-mission arcs introduce only selective
trail impact in the first; the second adds no mandatory rule. Bands7–8 continue
the Fractured Grid band7 ending. These ratings and90–150second duration targets
are design intentions, not measured enjoyment or human difficulty evidence.

All seven are asset-free greyboxes and every disposition remains non-final.
Route-specific mastery, all-preset full clears, timing alternatives, native
readability, human counterplay, original art, mode/release and Pages acceptance
remain gates. Similar return-race decisions should be merged or cut if playtests
cannot distinguish them; there is no minimum shipped count or filler quota.
