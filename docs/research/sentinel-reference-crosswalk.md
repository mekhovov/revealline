**Crown audience** is an original four-shield synthesis. **Rings of return** is a
separate optional Remix combining the known directional field and protected
connector with staged release. Four core candidates form a single learning arc:
one, two, three, then four shields. The compact candidate count is deliberate,
not a reduction in validation requirements or a promise that all will ship.

All share one catalog-owned boss cadence, the existing player handling and
deterministic difficulty presets. Bands11–12 continue Crosswind's ending band11.
There are no countdowns, random drops, per-level speed overrides, copied artwork
or claims of measured human difficulty. Final backgrounds and human tests remain
open gates.

## Probe-driven rejection and revision

The first Standard/immediate/seed1 legal-input search cleared the first three
drafts in8.45s,12.05s and10.35s, using simple straight captures. Those drafts are
rejected as pacing evidence. More shields alone did not make better decisions:
ordinary empty-region fill often captured distant shield objectives together.
The initial search output remains in `.cache/sentinel-standard-routes-r1.jsonl`.

Revisions move the first two missions' shields into connected core chambers,
preserve distinct return openings, and bring the third mission's shields closer
to the core. Known non-retaining patrol/ground roles add route pressure without
changing capture seeds or shortening warnings. The chamber asks for turning
cuts, so a straight-line-only search is insufficient; additional legal-input
feasibility and replay checks are required before acceptance.

The revised First relay has a fresh-input verified 25.35-second no-loss route with
five closures. The revised Twin receivers and Relay perimeter still permit
9.96/12.15-second optimized clears. In particular, isolating the single retaining
core can legitimately capture several shields at once. Do not suppress that
rewarding genre behavior merely to increase duration. Both remain provisional:
verify genuinely different optional routes, then use human evidence to decide
whether to retain, redesign, merge or remove them. No final disposition or
measured human difficulty is asserted by these solver outputs.

Static validation also caught an initial shield on reserved connector cells and
a landing overlapping a newly authored wall. Both were moved into their proper
domains; the shared validators were not weakened. Every current initial field is
one genuinely retained component, with no remote objective or half-board auto-fill.

## Primary-source checkpoint

The [official Volfied rerelease description](https://www.arcadearchives.com/en/title/aca-301/)
distinguishes vulnerable craft contact and incomplete-line contact when protection
is absent. Our design inference is to keep those failure targets explicit and
make the reclaimed-ground shelter, locked lane, live trail and core body legible
as different things. This is not evidence that Volfied uses our shield-relay
system, nor a claim that this mechanic guarantees popularity or retention.

The Fortix2 Steam listing was region-unavailable during this recheck, so no new
feature claim or exact rule is derived from that response. The approved plan's
previous research remains separately attributed.
