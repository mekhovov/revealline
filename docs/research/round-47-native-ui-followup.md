## Additional touch and storage review

Apple's [WWDC26 touch session](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/358/) was reviewed through its published transcript. It recommends anchored layouts that respect safe areas, placing frequent actions near the thumbs, and hiding unavailable actions. These support our contextual controls and separate arena/control space. The session's Swift/Metal APIs are native-platform options, not dependencies of our browser renderer. Portrait comfort, finger occlusion and physical-controller behavior still need device evidence.

MDN's [storage guidance](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Storage_API/Storage_quotas_and_eviction_criteria) distinguishes best-effort from persistent storage and documents quota failures and origin eviction. Browser usage estimates are approximate, and a persistence request can be denied. Keep explicit downloads, accurate availability, export and exact restoration; do not describe a successful request as protection against the player deleting site data.

The source capacity review found a separate product gap: current external chapters cannot be removed through ordinary pack mutation, and still history retains original blobs. The 12 installed-pack limit and shared 256 MiB media budget therefore need an explicit chapter-offload workflow before the full collection can be rotated on a device. Preserve earned identity, scores and saved attempts while separating downloadable bytes from durable records. This is a proposed implementation priority, not a feature delivered by the retention request. Capacity must not be solved by silently raising caps or deleting player progress.
