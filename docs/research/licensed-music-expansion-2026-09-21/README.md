## Rights evidence

Every selected attachment appears in the **File(s)** area of its creator's OpenGameArt submission. Each submission explicitly links CC0, CC BY 3.0 or CC BY 4.0. Attachment URLs and byte lengths were read from the official HTML's download anchors and MIME `length=` values; no preview URLs were substituted. These are declared sizes, not measured payload sizes or integrity hashes.

[CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) permits commercial copying, modification and redistribution without permission. [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) and [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) permit commercial copying, redistribution and adaptation with the applicable credit, source/license links, modification notices, and no additional restrictions. On that basis, these licenses support hosted browser-game assets, offline caching, creator-share/backup copies, standalone redistribution and gameplay video. Preserve title/author/source/license credits even for CC0. For CC BY, carry those credits with exported files/packs and video attribution. This is a license-based inference; it is not a separate creator statement naming this game.

**Content ID registration is unknown.** A permissive license does not prove absence of automated platform claims. None of these recordings is claimed as a Revealline original. No public-domain game melody, copyrighted game remix or paid-only file is intentionally included. The source authors identify the selected works as their compositions; musical resemblance and third-party sample provenance have not been independently audited by listening.

## Storage and completion gates

The 32 direct audio attachments total **135.65 MiB** in their published lengths. The eight ZIP downloads total 318.46 MiB transferred, but only one ZIP is needed at a time; the largest is 55.76 MiB. Keep ZIP scratch below 64 MiB, record archive SHA-256 and MP3 member name/CRC, extract only the supplied MP3, and discard the archive after verification. Exact retained MP3 size remains unknown until intake. OGG conversions add storage, so measure retained source plus runtime MP3 bytes and stop before the **300 MiB expansion budget** and **650 MiB combined active budget**.

No encoded hash, duration, loudness, peak, decode success or listening approval is asserted here. All 40 durations are deliberately null because the selected creator pages do not expose them. Several Joth and chip compositions are loopable; reject any that prove to be a very short jingle or fragment during decoding instead of inflating the full-composition count. Also review clipping, complete endings/loop boundaries, palette, energy and credits before publication. No runtime tests were required or run for these research-only files. JSON structure, unique IDs and official attachment hosts were checked locally.
