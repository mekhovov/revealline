## Cultural source boundaries

- The [Museum Fund embroidered towel record 94261](https://museum.mincult.gov.ua/collections/rushnik-vishitiy-94261)
  documents geometric ornament, a broad rhombus-and-triangle band and a large rhomboid figure with
  branching exterior details. Read the arrows borrows only central-diamond, staggered-triangle and
  outward-branch organization.
- The [Ivan Honchar Museum woven towel record КН-761](https://honchar.org.ua/collections/detail/2461)
  documents bands of different widths, checker-arranged squares and alternating plain and
  ornamented bands. Windbreak weave borrows only alternating band width and checker-like offset.
- The [Museum Fund gerdan record 40778](https://museum.mincult.gov.ua/collections/40778)
  documents two parallel ornamental strips, a joining rhomboid form and symmetric rows separated
  by contrasting cross forms. Long wave borrows only parallel-strip, central-join and separated-row
  organization.

No source object, motif, pattern chart, stitch or weave draft, palette, meaning or coordinates are
copied. These records provide broad compositional vocabulary; they do not prove gameplay balance
or universal symbolism.
