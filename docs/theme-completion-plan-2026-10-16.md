## Research applied to this completion pass

- [Factorio's GUI progress matrix](https://www.factorio.com/blog/post/fff-277) tracks separate stages across individual screens. Adopt a screen/state evidence matrix: implementation, rendered review and device qualification are separate columns. A settings screenshot cannot close results, recovery or creator coverage.
- [Factorio's tooltip work](https://www.factorio.com/blog/post/fff-318) separates information into consistent sections and emphasizes readable hierarchy. Keep primary actions and recovery explanations visible; reserve recessed surfaces for longer reading. Do not solve weak hierarchy by adding more decorative texture.
- [Factorio's icon production process](https://www.factorio.com/blog/post/fff-348) reviews size-specific outputs instead of relying on one large source image. Review marks at actual player size, close/far flight distances and grazing angles. A source atlas or handsome close-up alone is not approval.
- [Captain of Industry's surfaces and decals](https://coigame.com/Blog/cd-43) separate surface variation from functional markings. Retain deterministic materials and independently bound landing/orientation cues. Avoid duplicating entire materials just to add a stripe.
- [Xbox contrast guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/gaming/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/102) covers loading, errors, HUD and controls, and measures textured backgrounds at their lowest-contrast region. Use the existing captured-pixel sampler; retain the 4.5:1 normal, 3:1 inactive and 7:1 high-contrast gates. Token contrast is necessary, not sufficient.
- [Xbox navigation guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/112) requires consistent input, focus order and scaling without two-direction scrolling. Extend the verified settings/brief returns to results, recovery and creator routes. Physical controller and touch qualification remain distinct from keyboard and viewport emulation.

These are original design adaptations. No reference-game textures or icons are copied. Additional families are not needed to prove the framework.

## Completion rules

1. A check is complete only when its named output and evidence exist. Write `pending`, `failed` or `blocked` otherwise; do not silently shrink the gate.
2. Test fixtures may prepare documented, isolated review state. They must not fabricate successful outcomes, bypass ordinary access gates or overwrite a player's existing save.
3. Keep engine/package limits from the current admission code. Do not restore stale counts from an older plan or enlarge limits to accommodate a review fixture.
4. Keep SIM appearance and performance evidence separate from simulation verification. Browser frame timing is not GPU timing or physical-device certification.
5. After completing the available work, publish the tested changes and report any exact external blockers. The project is not complete while required gates remain pending.
