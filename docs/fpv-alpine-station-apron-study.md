# Alpine station: grounded concourse study

The already saved quiet-timber pilot-height view shows detailed station wings separated by a nearly uniform bright snow field. This source-only study gives that reachable concourse a restrained hardstanding apron, so the wall bases and open approach read together. It is an architectural surface, not an objective marker or promised additional ground support. The station still has opaque backed wall panels and its existing open gap.

Only 260 complete, existing visual-only terrain-following triangles change from snow to the already approved road-apron colour. The 208 × 40 m rectangle runs from x3296–3504 m and z3080–3120 m, extending beyond both ends of the wings and just beneath their wall bases. Every vertex remains at its original canonical terrain height plus 3 mm. No polygon is added, removed, lifted, clipped or made collidable. All terrain assets, solid/visual triangle positions, oriented normals, UVs, textures/samplers, starts and route clearances stay exact. The existing `alpine-overlays` opaque depth treatment applies in all three tiers without a new runtime capability or shader.

`prepare-station-apron.mjs <exact r4 source> <new external output>` requires the accepted `relief-r4-quiet-timber` manifest hashes and refuses an existing output. It prepares a separate `relief-r5-station-apron` source while retaining every previous source, edition and pin. Original RevealLine contributor work is CC0-1.0; no external imagery or simulator assets are copied.

The complete source is 22,584,529 bytes, 5,725 bytes more than r4. Splitting the two material groups adds 2,048 bytes of resident geometry at the station: all tiers report 769,524 geometry bytes, the same 458,760 texture bytes and 1,228,284 total resource bytes. The region's conservative retained-plus-staged maximum stays 2,992,680 bytes, within the unchanged 8 MiB authoring bound. There are no new texture pixels. These ownership measurements do not establish GPU cost or physical-device frame rate.

`verify-station-apron.mjs <r4> <r5>` passes exact oriented triangle/material/UV/normal scope across all 104 source cells, two canonical GLBs with zero validator errors/warnings, deterministic preparation and idempotence, all three approved material tiers, six repeated window transitions and twelve bidirectional low-approach sweeps per tier, physical terrain support, preserved solid walls, both safely disarmed modes and zero owned disposal. The public preparation/verification receipts are in `receipts-station-apron-r1/`.

Native same-camera appearance, approach legibility and final art acceptance remain separate; this branch stays Draft until root's serial source review. It adds no Library row or default player change. Immutable r5 delivery, exact package and normal player/Library installation are later gates if this source is accepted. Existing r4 Library #1501 remains an independent exact-package/native gate, and old editions, failed packages and player storage remain untouched. No elapsed endurance, full-offline/restart, public launch, physical-device or final whole-region art claim is made.
