# Canyon Expanse: original broad-relief source study

This separate source identity covers an actual 8,192 × 8,192 m square on a
32 × 32 grid of 256 m cells. It preserves the published 384 m arrival and 768 m
six-district revisions. It does not enlarge, tile, stretch or overwrite either
older source. All landforms and route placements here are original analytic
composition; no external mesh or texture is bundled.

Six regions have different spatial roles: a low braided basin; an elevated mesa
and western terrace; a long eastern escarpment with two saddles; a three-channel
confluence around an eroded island; a southern fan with broad hoodoo groups; and
a horseshoe amphitheatre with an open western mouth. Eighteen relief landmarks
anchor approaches and turns. Twelve routes use actual triangle heights, not a
flat flight plane. Routes slow their requested horizontal speed on steep climbs
so the same production flight authority can follow them without altered physics.

The source keeps 33 × 33 samples per cell, so terrain sample spacing is 8 m.
That trades fine relief for extent: it cannot represent the earlier 4 m detail,
narrow arches, overhangs or buildings. Landmarks have broad shoulders resolved
by this lattice. Canonical render and collision triangles remain identical.

## Boundaries of this increment

This is terrain-only preparation. The existing scenery profile requires a real
nondegenerate surface in every cell, and mixed manifests require matching grids.
No hidden triangles or arbitrary repeated props are used to fill empty scenery
cells. The strict installed delivery path currently expects a paired terrain and
scenery profile. Sparse scenery support or an explicit terrain-only delivery
capability therefore remains a separate requirement before Library admission.

The 64 MiB delivery ceiling, manifest counts, resident source/geometry budgets,
physics and existing public maps are unchanged. Actual generated bytes, complete
canonical/seam checks, bounded production windows and representative commanded
flight evidence are recorded alongside the source before publication. Source
coverage alone does not establish sustained device FPS, final art quality,
continuous traversal of the entire extent or a qualified player release.

for terrain-following checks. The controlled verifier limits requested vertical

schedule exceeded that tracking tolerance; terrain and physics were not changed

## Authoring camera compatibility

The first native inspection showed visible terrain in FPV/Chase but sky in
Loaded area. The authoring preview still calculated chunk bounds at 128 m and
used a fixed 900 m far plane, despite accepting the new 256 m grid. It now uses
the validated manifest cell size for both. Production regional rendering already
handled this distinction. An executable check invokes the actual preview bounds
callback and shared camera code for both sizes, asserting that the admitted
ground centre is centred and the resident corners survive depth clipping.
Native re-inspection on exact source `9abbccdf` passed on 15 October 2026.
Loaded area showed terrain at all six starts: Balanced basin, confluence, hoodoo
flats and amphitheatre; Quality high mesa; Performance red escarpment. All
prepared disarmed at zero ticks/throttle with 456,408 geometry bytes. At the
amphitheatre, explicit FPV arm and keyboard flight followed by pause reached
807 ticks and zero throttle. No console errors or warnings were observed.
The screenshot is `canyon-expanse-9abbccdf-overview-20261015.png` in the external
review receipts. The earlier 9071 sky-only failure remains recorded above.

This accepts the source and camera technically. The visible neighbourhoods remain
broad smooth slopes with sparse rounded relief. The 18 analytic features are
authored relief anchors, not finished scenery or a representative final-art
standard. Native device performance, continuous whole-region traversal and
installed/offline package qualification remain open.
