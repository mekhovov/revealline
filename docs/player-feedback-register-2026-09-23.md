## Priority and acceptance rules

- Latest reference-matching feedback supersedes blanket increases to every actor's
  speed/count. A map can become harder through route decisions and threat domains
  rather than constant acceleration or clutter.
- With v0.89 delivered and v0.90 source merged, finish v0.90 promotion and the
  collision-only fairness correction before bulk new content. Then finish
  mission-launch defects and current-speed balance. Keep deferred ideas visible
  instead of implying their removal.
- Historical gp1/pressure-v2 route passes do not prove gp2 balance or identical
  route feasibility. Human enjoyment and physical-device checks remain distinct
  from automated feasibility and basic public availability.
- Use F01–F30 in relevant feature acceptance notes. Record the implemented slice,
  exact released version, evidence and remaining scope; do not close a whole row
  because one example or unit test succeeds.
- Journey P00–P15 and original whole-game P00–P18 are separate identifiers; use
  the [original programme crosswalk](cross-mode-execution.md#original-whole-game-phases-remaining-work-crosswalk)
  rather than assuming one phase's completion closes the other.
- Soundtrack-specific completed/remaining work is maintained in the main plan's
  soundtrack section. This register does not reinterpret private music imports as
  publicly licensed assets or resume paused original production.
