# Apex readable-pressure pair

Status: candidate only; human balance and public integration pending.

This stacked successor applies already taught pursuit/interception roles to the first two adjacent
Apex Aurora missions. It preserves the earlier Long wave and Returning light candidates exactly and
does not register a route, change a default, bump a version or alter a historical edition.

| Mission           | Replacement                                 | Two intended approaches                                                                                                                                                          |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Crossing complete | Upper field keeper → heading interceptor    | Feint through the marked center and turn toward a terrace after lock; or wait on a terrace through the commitment and enclose a side belt while the patrol routes are separated. |
| Final broadcast   | Former carrier/field keeper → trail pursuer | Secure the upper anchor and bank its permanent link; or draw pursuit through the broad center, close at the lower anchor and decline irrelevant erosion cleanup.                 |

Both roles retain field and reuse the `journey-actors-v9` 90-tick warning, 144-tick finite
commitment and preset-specific recovery. Their committed target never follows a later turn. Actor
IDs, counts, tiers, starting positions and headings remain exact. Maps, foundations, terrain, speed
zones, relays, objectives, erosion, other patrols, coverage, pictures, campaign order and previous
candidate/history records are unchanged.

## Focused evidence

The tests compare this source directly with the preceding candidate, resolve all three presets in
Solo and Versus, and verify one-for-one role replacement, retained-field semantics and target lock
after a course change. Four existing legal-input routes were replayed through the new simulation:

- Crossing complete Gentle/immediate: 72.25-second no-loss clear, thirteen cuts, one warning and
  one commitment.
- Crossing complete Expert/Grid + Buffer: 67.95-second no-loss clear, nine cuts, two warnings and
  two commitments.
- Final broadcast Gentle/immediate: 41.05-second no-loss clear, nine cuts, one warning and one
  commitment.
- Final broadcast Standard/Grid + Buffer: 30.25-second no-loss clear, five cuts, four warnings and
  three commitments.

No route collected a bonus. Each candidate checkpoint reproduces and each input finishes equal
independent paired-board races. This demonstrates deterministic feasibility across both steering
policies and more than one preset; it does not qualify every preset/control combination or prove
that both authored approaches are human-viable. Native/device readability, objective-order quality,
erosion usefulness, mastery, broad seeds, difficulty ranking, fairness and fun remain **balance
review pending**.
