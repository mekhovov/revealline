# Ordered listening rewards

`CompletionRewardDefinition.v1` can optionally include `audioGroups`. Each group uses the registered `revealline-ordered-audio-group.v1` presentation recipe: a stable `id`, English/Ukrainian `locales` containing a title, and ordered `payloadIds` referring to existing audio payloads in the same reward. A reward allows up to four groups, each containing two to eight recordings; a recording can occur in at most one group. The existing sixteen-payload and 64 KiB definition limits remain unchanged.

This is a presentation over the ordinary exact audio payloads, not another reward type or listening store. Every original and both transcripts retain their current hashes, source/rights admission, selected edition ownership and export dependency closure. Video remains in its existing captioned native viewer, separate from a soundtrack playlist. No external streams, autoplay, repeat, shuffle or automatic advancement are accepted.

The shared viewer puts the list at its first member's authored position and respects the explicit track order. Other payloads keep their relative order. Selecting a recording disposes the old media player and displays the selected transcript and controls; Play must be pressed explicitly for every track. Keyboard arrows move focus within the list without starting playback. Previous/Next selects a recording without playing it. Playback uses the existing foreground audio owner, volume, mute and soundtrack ducking. Closing the reward or Studio preview cancels requests, releases URLs and stops playback.

Use **Reward listening playlists** in the shared Level/Campaign or Company discovery editor to add, remove and reorder already-authored audio payloads. The existing Soundtrack original handoff supplies approved exact audio and the media adapter still requires English and Ukrainian transcripts. Preview uses explicitly chosen local originals; it cannot grant a win or save listening state. Apply creates an immutable reward revision and rebinds only exact old author references. Removing grouping does not remove recordings or change completion requirements.

Player Collection, earned results, Company eligible preview and Level/Campaign preview use the same ordered grouping. Printable discoveries retain the group heading and ordered exact transcript sections. Imports and earned receipts validate the recipe alongside the original definition. An existing promise or earned receipt retains its exact old order and pins; later author edits cannot replace it. Definitions omitting `audioGroups` retain their legacy bytes and behavior.

All existing edition/offline size limits apply. A playlist is not a mechanism to admit long media or bypass source rights. Authors may distribute larger licensed recording packages only through an independently admitted optional package.
