# Prepared main v0.82.1 public byte auditor

Preparation only: no public requests, remote mutations or body downloads have been performed. The future inventory is deliberately unset. Original game metadata is pinned to v0.82.1 source `64ec9fd2e5688f4248fe005ac6a47c0c1394ea9e`, tree `402f5f12d83cb86dbceb9d9dceec12731ea48980`, distribution SHA `eb98abe00155176d77facbcedcd1510219031057afc56a5669f98c7330b2b3af`.

The only request origin is `https://mekhovov.github.io/revealline/`. Four workers stream every exact actual deployed inventory row, including main aliases, catalog files, the complete canonical current graph, and historical bridges. Redirects, compression, wrong MIME/status/final URL, oversize/truncated/changed bodies are refused. Each file has at most three attempts; every failure and final row is retained. Bodies are never written to disk. Acceptance deadlines are 30 minutes overall / 90 seconds per attempt, checked before and after body reads and after response close. The 25-second socket timeout is an inactivity bound, not a strict process wall limit. A blocking DNS/connect/TLS/header/read/close operation may overrun a deadline until it returns; an expired completion is then refused, including exact bytes followed by delayed EOF. HTTPResponse.read1 is preferred over accumulating read(n) where available. Consequently the process can finish later than its acceptance deadline. The output needs at least 1 GiB free.
