# Team cue readability follow-up — 18 September 2026

Primary guidance: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/gaming/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/101

The guidance applies text-size requirements to text inside icons/glyphs as well as ordinary UI and recommends scaling without loss of content or functionality. For RevealLine, the inferred next acceptance checks are actual displayed Team pilot/rescue/cue text at small arena sizes, legible backings and no clipping/overlap in both text faces. A larger nominal canvas font is not by itself proof of readable screen size. Preserve arena geometry, hitboxes and simulation while propagating the shared presentation preference.

This research is a design input, not Xbox certification or physical-device acceptance.
