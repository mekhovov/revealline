# Navigation research refresh — 18 September 2026

Freshly opened primary sources:
- https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/dialog-modal/
- https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/112

W3C prescribes focus inside an open modal, contained Tab/Shift+Tab navigation, Escape close, a visible close control, and restoration to the invoking element or a logical workflow successor. Large content may initially focus a static heading to keep the beginning visible.

Xbox guidance calls for consistent spatial navigation and complete digital keyboard/controller access. Apply the same confirm/back convention throughout the game.

Application to current work: Studio guide, Enemy Workshop startup close, and Team reward viewer must retain visible Back/close and restore logical focus. Verify on the actual shipped route and at ordinary playing sizes. Source-preview browser, digital input tests and physical controller acceptance are separate. This research does not establish a new passed journey or close a phase.
