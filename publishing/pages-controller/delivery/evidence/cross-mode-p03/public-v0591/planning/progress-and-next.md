## Research application

The practice exit follows [W3C no-keyboard-trap guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/no-keyboard-trap.html); the wider menu matrix follows [Xbox UI navigation guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/112). These sources inform the design; they are not a compliance certificate or evidence of physical-device testing.
