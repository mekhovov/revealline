## Delivery rules

Every logical feature/correction receives reviewed related hunks, appropriate tests, updated guidance, a new immutable version, source PR and release, Pages selector PR, full deployed inventory check and affected real browser journey. Failed observations remain visible. Source previews, simulated devices, audible listening and real hardware are reported separately. No phase closes merely because a build or deployment succeeded.
