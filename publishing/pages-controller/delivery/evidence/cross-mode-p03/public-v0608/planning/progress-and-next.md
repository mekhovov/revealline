## New-player experience recommendation

The observed introductory First Signal run in this release recorded a win at 0:08. It proves that the retained intro/reward loop works; it does not establish the harder, more engaging enemy pressure requested in F02. Make the introduction’s purpose clear, keep learning replayable, and guide the player promptly to the three-level wide FPV benchmark while preserving explicit choices and saved flights. Measure first-session attempts, loss causes, route variety and discovery before changing future defaults. Preserve historical archives and score-authoritative recipes.

The [retained research note](player-experience-review.original.md) uses Microsoft’s [interface-context guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/114) and [tutorial guidance history](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xag-version-history). These support clear onboarding and revisitable learning, not a claim that a particular RevealLine challenge is enjoyable. The onboarding note remains its original research snapshot; this report makes no gameplay change. The earlier [status draft](plan-status-draft.original.md) remains unchanged for provenance.

For the remaining About/Workshop exits, follow [Xbox navigation guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/112): keep digital-input navigation consistent, provide visible return paths and preserve a logical focus order when layouts scale. Use [W3C modal-dialog guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/dialog-modal/) for predictable return focus. Apply these rules to future responsive and controller journeys; today’s keyboard checks do not establish physical-controller acceptance. These primary sources were rechecked by the release coordinator during this delivery.

Every completed slice still needs its own exact-source qualification, immutable release, reviewed publisher, full public-byte check and affected actual player journeys. Keep mock/lifecycle tests, resized browser windows, physical input, listening, offline behavior and human playtests distinct. P03/P05/P08/P18 and the full plan remain active.
