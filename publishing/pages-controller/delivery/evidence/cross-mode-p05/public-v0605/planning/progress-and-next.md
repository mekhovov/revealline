3. **P08-A existing-map and actor parity.** Complete both Team arenas, all fifteen built-in Versus maps and compatible installed/imported paths with exact artwork, recognizable roles, readable heading/scale and the whole arena. The separate Team teaser candidate still needs native wins, short-landscape checks, final integration and its own release.

Interface color preferences alone are not complete themes.

Complete the benchmark and human playtests before P10 encounters and bulk campaigns.

All candidates retain their own final qualification, release and public gates; their test counts are not added together as unique coverage.

Physical keyboard/controller/touch journeys, ordinary browser/layout observations, modeled lifecycle regressions, actual BFCache, media/offline recovery and human playtesting remain separate evidence categories. Unavailable physical evidence stays open; it does not justify adding unrelated tools or repeatedly requalifying unchanged behavior.
