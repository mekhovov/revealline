# Curation of public v0.60.1 evidence

This delivery packet preserves exact original bytes from the Team public audit. [retained-files.json](retained-files.json) maps every original absolute path to its retained relative destination, size and SHA-256. The root decision in [acceptance.json](acceptance.json) accepts only the named Team correction; the [current report](README.md) and [plan](planning/progress-and-next.md) are generated successors, not edits to historical evidence.

All nine binding inputs, both reviewed/unreviewed bindings, complete inventory/attempt/result/report files, before/after observations, fresh live-authority responses and their request/exit/stderr records are retained. The original small receipt ZIP is retained once. Its receipt member is the canonical `tools/inputs/c1576deeab14bea21b00449912a6a465c3efaf90/receipt.json`; the second member is `receipt-intake-main1/zip-receipt.json`. The duplicate extracted receipt and copied published-release body are resolved through explicit byte-verified aliases. `originals/` retains the predecessor helper bodies pinned by preparation provenance.

The two complete attempt/result files are meaningful distinct outputs even though this zero-retry run produced identical bytes. Distinct timed API observations are retained. Preparation notes, the initial core self-check failure, successful successor, and earlier unreviewed/pending statuses remain unchanged. They are historical records, not the final delivery status.

The SHA-qualified source evidence ZIP, source tar, distribution ZIP, existing source verification and Archive 16 admission remain in their immutable published/committed locations linked by the report. No game/media HTTP bodies, browser data, node_modules, future-candidate fixtures or large archive duplicates are included.

[curation-review.json](curation-review.json) records exact-copy validation, direct binding/runner/request/acceptance pin closure, ZIP-member correspondence and new Markdown link checks. Historical references inside source qualification, runtime inventories, catalog/admission descriptors and predecessor records describe immutable source or published assets; they are not promises that those large bodies exist in this small delivery packet. Original absolute cache paths are provenance, not portable execution commands. Re-running a live audit requires fresh review and actual current authorities, not executing a historical request blindly.

This package does not alter the source, versions, publication selector or workflows. A later delivery-only PR records acceptance of the already-published release. Under the inspected publisher workflows, its main push path is excluded from automatic Pages deployment; the normal PR preview remains enabled.
