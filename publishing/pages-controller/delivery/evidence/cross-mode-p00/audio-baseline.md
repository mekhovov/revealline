## Existing protection that P02 must preserve

- **Soundscape cancellation:** `game/ui/audio.mjs`, `enable()`/`resume()` capture `transition`; `disable()`, `suspend()` and `dispose()` invalidate pending transitions. `disable()` synchronously changes enabled state, cancels published pending cues and previews, stops voices, zeros bus targets, suspends context and releases the iOS playback session. Ordinary persistent-mode `pause()` stops gameplay effects while preserving music intent.
- **iOS activation support:** `requestPlaybackAudioSession()` runs before context creation/resume. Solo `activateAudio()` and gesture handlers preserve the synchronous activation path where possible. `initializeSoundtrack()` calls `prepare()` after stored selection loads, allowing already prepared local media to play without first waiting for storage.
- **Track-generation protection:** `game/ui/soundtrack-player.mjs`, `cancel()` increments generation and aborts the current operation; `clearMedia()` removes listeners/source, pauses media, revokes its URL and advances resource identity. `pause()` clears `desired`, cancels preparation and pauses both transports. `suspend()` preserves intended playback separately from active playback.
- **Published-track continuation:** published-file preparation/play checks enable success, generation, disposal and desired state before continuing after context enable. Preserve these guards when unifying master controls.
- **Gain ownership:** soundtrack `gains()` applies the host master × music × fade × lowest active duck lease to HTML media; Soundscape receives the corresponding procedural music gain. `acquireGain()` returns a release handle scoped to one borrower. Story closure must not release another owner's lease or overwrite base mix settings.
- **No late cue replay:** `createPublishedCues()` fetches/decodes on cache miss and returns procedural fallback immediately. A completed decode populates cache; it does not replay the old event. Enable/lifecycle/cancellation checks prevent stale decode adoption; active voices are owned by Soundscape.
- **Sound Studio lifecycle:** audition token checks, `stopAudition()` and disposal own the temporary media URL. Visibility handling stops audition and preserves intended soundtrack restoration explicitly. Do not remove that ownership when introducing a shared master.
- **Story lifecycle:** story preparation has generation/abort checks; video play uses `playEpoch`/`desired`, invokes native `play()` before awaiting, and handles obsolete completions. Preference changes immediately update video mute/volume. Pause/disposal mute and stop playback, release ducking, cancel work and release the URL. Hidden/blurred pages pause the story or cancel preparation.
- **Page lifecycle:** Solo `suspendInteraction()` releases inputs, invalidates content switching, suspends audio and pauses play; `restoreListening()` and gesture recovery distinguish transport intent. Versus `suspend()` pauses, suspends both audio transports, clears controller state and invalidates frame state. These are local lifecycle boundaries, not global mute preferences.

These statements describe guards present in source. They do not certify browser autoplay behavior or every asynchronous interleaving.
