These references were checked on 18 September 2026; physical hardware acceptance remains separate.

For each completed feature/phase, review and commit only related hunks (whole related binary assets with hashes), allocate the next unused version, pass source/build gates, review and merge the source PR, freeze immutable release assets, update Pages through its PR, then verify actual deployed bytes and affected journeys. Report the exact source/PR, playable URL, passed checks and limitations. Only accepted scope becomes Complete. It does not establish public v0.61.6 acceptance or close a parent phase.
